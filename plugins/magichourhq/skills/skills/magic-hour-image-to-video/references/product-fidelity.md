# Keep the product recognizable in motion

Use for product demonstrations, packaging reveals, unboxing or tactile close-ups. Animate an approved source directly when it already establishes the shot. A fidelity prompt is a constraint to inspect, not a preservation guarantee.

## Separate facts from motion

Keep a compact record from the supplied photos and approved product information:

- **Fixed geometry:** silhouette, proportions, parts, openings and quantities.
- **Fixed surface:** materials, colors, printing, logo and exact label text.
- **Allowed motion:** one action showing a verified function without inventing a capability.

Do not infer unseen sides, internal parts or package contents. An approved generated view can guide a creative treatment, but it does not prove the real product's hidden geometry. Obtain a real view when accuracy depends on it, or keep the known view visible.

Assign available references a role: appearance, actual side geometry, label detail or scale. Additional images and reference tags work only where the current schema supports them. For a single-image route, establish the needed view in one inspected start frame; do not redesign an accepted frame just to add references.

## Choose supported motion

Locked framing protects identity and small text. A slow push-in or lateral move may suffice; an orbit exposes unseen surfaces and needs stronger evidence. Increase camera complexity only when the requested shot needs it and the result retains the product.

Occlusion can hide a detail that returns changed. Keep a grip away from critical printing and inspect before, during and after hands, lids or fabric cover the product. Split a difficult reveal into reviewed shots when needed instead of packing unrelated actions into one short job.

## Plan a tactile reveal

Use the actual approved packaging and contents. An invented box is a proposed design, not what a customer receives. Adapt these beats to the requested interval; not every row needs a separate generation:

| Visual | Action | Sound, when requested and supported |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Closed packaging in the intended setting | Settle a hand on the lid or lift one corner | Tap or paper movement |
| Same box, contents visible | Hold the base and open the lid | Lid movement or tissue rustle |
| Product at credible scale | Lift or demonstrate one verified function, then hold | Relevant click, pour or other effect |

Use native audio only if the live route exposes it. Otherwise retain the sound plan for an available, authorized edit using real or licensed effects. Writing a SOUND block does not create audio. Listen separately; do not add music to an unaccompanied reveal.

## Review and reuse

Watch the full clip against the original product: label, parts, shape, scale, color and hand contact, including the ending. Record failed attributes and times. Three sampled frames do not establish stability throughout.

Repair only failed shots within the combined budget. After a wait timeout, retrieve the same project instead of creating another generation. Reuse accepted footage for captions, crops and offers. If exact printed detail cannot survive motion, use an authentic packshot in a deliberate end card or permitted composition and disclose that treatment; an overlay does not make inaccurate footage faithful.

**Evidence limit:** adapted instructions do not establish a validated Magic Hour unboxing success rate or guaranteed product preservation.
