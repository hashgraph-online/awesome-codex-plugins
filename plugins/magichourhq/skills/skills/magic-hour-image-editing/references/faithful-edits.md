# Faithful background changes and reframing

Use this for edits where preserving the supplied person, product or artwork matters more than inventing a new composition.

> Make a vertical version of this product photo with room for a headline. Keep the complete bottle and actual label. Use a layout if cropping would cut it off; don't redraw the product just to move the text.

## Choose the smallest operation that satisfies the brief

| Source and requirement                                         | Route                                                                  | Do not substitute                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Enough existing canvas for the new crop                        | Crop the original with an available image tool                         | A paid regeneration of the subject                                 |
| Exact identity and a designed border/panel are acceptable      | Contain the original in the requested canvas and place copy separately | Stretching or a claim of full-bleed output                         |
| New environment or missing background must be invented         | AI Image Editor from the original; preserve viewpoint and identity     | A prompt-only recreation                                           |
| Literal pixel preservation, a mask or transparency is required | Use an available tool that actually supports it                        | A generative “keep unchanged” instruction presented as a guarantee |

## Make the new setting believable

For a background replacement, specify the new contact surface and shadow as well as the background. Glass and metal may still reflect the old scene; inspect those regions. If preserving the original reflections would look wrong, explain the necessary relighting as part of the requested edit.

For recoloring, distinguish the target material from logos, skin, hardware and shadows. Judge hue in a neutral region rather than a colored highlight. For object removal, inspect the previously hidden background and repeated textures; do not claim reconstructed content matches the unseen original.

If exact layout is required, translate the copy region into a boundary and inspect the delivered file. Repeated missed coordinates are a reason to choose a compositor, not add stronger adjectives. Return to the original when a prior attempt changed identity; do not propagate that drift into the next edit.

Deliver the edited file beside its source, including a close view when small protected details matter. Retain the original and accepted edit separately. **Evidence:** [AURORA reframing](https://github.com/magichourhq/skills/tree/main/examples/aurora-edits) shows why requested ratios and placement cannot be treated as delivered facts.
