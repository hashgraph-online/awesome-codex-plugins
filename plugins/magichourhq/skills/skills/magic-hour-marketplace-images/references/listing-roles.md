# Choose listing roles by evidence

| Role                   | Required evidence                                               | Useful execution                                                                         |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Main image             | Exact product/pack sold and destination rules                   | Whole product, supported background, no invented accessories                             |
| Detail                 | A source image that actually shows the detail                   | Crop the original where possible; do not invent texture or a mechanism                   |
| Dimensions             | Verified measurements and units                                 | Exact editable labels and proportional guides; never infer measurements from perspective |
| Feature                | Product documentation supporting the claim                      | One legible claim with a matching visual; avoid decorative unlabeled arrows              |
| Lifestyle              | Intended use and enough reference to preserve identity          | Edit the setting; inspect hand/product contact and implied scale                         |
| What's included        | Confirmed contents and quantities                               | Show exactly those items; props must not imply included accessories                      |
| Comparison             | Verified like-for-like facts and identified comparison basis    | Readable table composed outside generation; no invented competitor data                  |
| A+ or enhanced content | Eligibility, current placement requirements and supported story | Design separate modules for the actual destination, not one oversized poster             |

An observed material color is not a verified ingredient, capacity, sustainability claim or performance result. Do not infer fragrance notes from a perfume bottle, waterproofing from droplets, or medical efficacy from packaging.

When supplied claims conflict, resolve the conflict before putting them into an image. When a role has no supporting evidence, replace it with a useful supported role or omit it. Never fabricate a review, certification, before/after result or professional endorsement.

For sets, establish consistent margins, typography and information density. Keep one major idea per secondary asset and ensure the smallest required text is readable at the expected display size. A catalog main image may need a very different composition from a branded secondary card.

After generation, separate two reviews: fidelity to the actual product and suitability for the destination. Passing one does not imply the other. Record the official requirements checked and their date with the delivery when platform-specific compliance matters.
