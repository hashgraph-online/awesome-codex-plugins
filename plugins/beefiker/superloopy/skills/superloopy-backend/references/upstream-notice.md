Public evidence is evidence of documented capability and published practice, not private practice inside any company. It cannot establish how every engineer works, how broadly a pattern is adopted, or whether an undisclosed deployment is safe. Product-specific limits, identity paths, workload budgets, and recovery claims require authorized tests against the target system.
