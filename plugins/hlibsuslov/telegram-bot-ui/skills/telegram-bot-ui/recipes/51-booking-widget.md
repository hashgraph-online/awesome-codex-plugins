# 51 — Booking widget: day strip, time slots, confirm (private chat, production)

Registry rows: `10.4-W02`, `10.4-W04`, `10.4-W07`, `10.4-W08` (`recipes/registry.json`). Checked 2026-11-15, Bot API 10.4.

1. **Request and visible result.** “Clients book haircuts by typing dates back and forth. Give them a picker in the chat.” — A card with the service (Haircut and styling · 45 min · €32.00), a strip of 7 days, the free half-hour slots of the chosen day (taken ones muted), Confirm. After booking it shows the appointment with Cancel booking; the caption becomes “Booked: Mon 16 Nov, 11:00 · Haircut and styling”.
2. **Why the new feature.** 10.4 widgets: a grid of slots that updates in place with the server's real availability, without opening a web app. Before: date keyboards across several messages.
3. **Requirements.** Bot API ≥ 10.4; production: private chats only; `allow_queries: true`; `allowed_updates` includes `widget_query`. Size 3201 bytes compressed (English), 3402 (Ukrainian), limit 4096. Free slots travel as 7 hex bitmasks (bit *i* = 09:00 + 30 min × *i*), ≈ 180 bytes of state.
4. **Payload.** `examples/payloads/r51-booking.json`; fixture `examples/fixtures/widget_query_slot_pick.json` (`"data": "pick:1:1030@0"`). Key part: `{"chat_id": 111111111, "html": "…", "caption": "Haircut and styling · 45 min · EUR 32.00 · pick a time", "chat_data": "{\"svc\":\"Haircut and styling\",\"dur\":45,\"pr\":3200,\"cur\":\"EUR\",\"d0\":1794700800,\"m\":[\"2d5ab\",…],\"sel\":\"\",\"bk\":\"\",\"r\":0}", "allow_queries": true, "reply_markup": {"inline_keyboard": [[{"text": "Today", "callback_data": "bk:day:0"}, …]]}}`.
5. **Code.** Widget `examples/widgets/src/slot-booking.html` (day strip is local UI, slots from the bitmask), demo `examples/widgets/demo/slot-booking.json` (default, selected, booked, full). Bot `examples/python/widget_gallery/h_slot_booking.py` (`pick` is a stale verb, `book` re-checks the slot, `cancel` frees it), recipe `examples/python/recipes/r51_booking.py`.
6. **Sequence and state.** sendWidget(r0) → tap 10:30 → `pick:1:1030@0` → owner check → slot free → edit `sel` (r1) → answer `{ok,s}`; same update redelivered → skipped; another customer books 10:30 on the website → bot `push` clears the bit (r2); tap Confirm → `book` re-checks → taken → `{err:"taken", msg, s}` with the selection cleared (r3); a tap drawn at r0 → `{err:"stale", s}`; `pick:1:1100@3` (r4) → `book` (r5) → answer “Booked for Mon 16 Nov, 11:00” → `editMessageCaption`.
7. **Result.** Mock-up (320 px): `Haircut and styling · 45 min · €32.00` · `[Sun 15][Mon 16]…` · grid `09:00 09:30 … (taken muted)` · `[Confirm 11:00]` → `Booked: Mon 16 Nov, 11:00 [Cancel booking]`.
8. **Typical mistake, limits, alternative.** Booking optimistically (another user competes for the slot: draw only the server's answer); trusting the widget's view of free slots (re-check on `book`); sending a full calendar in state (bitmasks, 7 days; more days = paging by query); a vertical scroll of slots (nothing scrolls — fit the grid or page it). Alternative: inline keyboard of days, then of times (works in groups too).
9. **Run and verify.** `py -3 examples/python/recipes/r51_booking.py` · `py -3 scripts/build_widget.py examples/widgets/src/slot-booking.html --strict --lang uk --demo examples/widgets/demo/slot-booking.json` · `py -3 scripts/preview_widget.py examples/widgets/src/slot-booking.html --serve 8765` (mock). Evidence: offline contract and handler tests; not rendered in Telegram clients.
10. **Adapt.** Replace the bitmask source with your calendar (slot length, opening hours, time zone of the business: label slots in the business's zone, and say so), keep `pick` stale-checked and `book` re-checked, mirror the booking into the caption, send a reminder message before the appointment.
