# Shopping: electronics, goods and household

Checked 2026-10-29. Policies change: confirm on the retailer's own policy page.

## The order of moves

1. **Is this the right product?** The previous model, a lower tier or a well-reviewed cheaper
   brand often does the same job. Name what the user gives up.
2. **Is now the right time?** Check the price history before calling something a deal:
   - Amazon: Keepa (charts on the product page, target-price alerts) or CamelCamelCamel.
   - Google Shopping "price insights" shows whether a price is low, typical or high for the last
     few months, and can track a price.
   - Europe: idealo (DE, AT, UK, FR, IT, ES) with price history and target-price alerts;
     PriceRunner (UK, Nordics); PriceSpy or Prisjakt (UK, NZ, FR, Nordics).
   - Israel: Zap.co.il for electronics and appliances; Pricez for supermarket and pharmacy chains.
   - Big sales: Black Friday and Cyber Monday, Prime Day and competing events, back to school,
     model launches (the previous model drops). If the price is at its historical low, buy; if it is
     high and the user can wait, set an alert.
3. **Which seller?** Compare the identical item across retailers, including shipping, tax, return
   window and warranty. Marketplace third-party sellers can be cheaper but riskier: check seller
   rating and who handles warranty.
4. **New, refurbished, open-box or used?**

| Option | Typical terms (verify) |
|--------|------------------------|
| Apple Certified Refurbished | Up to about 15 percent off, same 1-year warranty as new, new battery and shell on iPhone-class devices, AppleCare available |
| Back Market | 30-day returns, at least 1-year seller warranty |
| Amazon Renewed | 90-day guarantee; Renewed Premium has a 1-year window and higher battery health (North America) |
| Amazon Resale (formerly Warehouse) | Inspected returns graded Like New to Acceptable, normal Amazon returns |
| eBay Refurbished | Graded Certified to Good; Certified Refurbished listings carry a 2-year warranty |
| Best Buy open-box | Graded Excellent, Good, Fair, normal return period; check for missing accessories |

5. **Then stack** codes, cashback and card offers: [../promo-codes.md](../promo-codes.md).
6. **After buying,** set a reminder to re-check the price inside the price-adjustment or return
   window.

## Price match and price adjustment (2026)

| Retailer | Policy |
|----------|--------|
| Best Buy | Matches its own lower price during the return period; matches listed major competitors on identical, in-stock items sold by the competitor itself (not marketplace sellers or membership prices) |
| Target | No competitor matching since July 2025. Matches its own lower price within 14 days of purchase; a holiday price match runs for purchases from Nov 1 to Dec 24, 2026 |
| Amazon | No price match and no post-purchase price protection. Only option: return within the window and rebuy, or ask support for a discretionary credit |

## Warranties and protection

- Store extended warranties rarely pay off on electronics. Check first whether the user's credit
  card extends the manufacturer warranty.
- Grey-market and parallel imports are cheaper but may have no local warranty, a different charger
  or region locks. Say so.
- Very low prices from unknown shops can be scams or counterfeits: check the shop's age, reviews
  and company details.

## Groceries and household

- Compare unit price (per kg, per liter, per item), not package price.
- Store brands are usually made to similar specs for staples.
- Bulk only for non-perishables the user will finish.
- Subscribe-and-save style discounts help for steady consumables; cancel anything that piles up.
