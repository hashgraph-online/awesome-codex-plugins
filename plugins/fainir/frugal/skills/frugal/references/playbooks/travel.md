# Travel: flights, hotels, cars

Checked 2026-10-29. Airline and hotel rules change: confirm on the official fare or rate rules.

## Flights

1. **Flexible dates and airports.** Google Flights date grid and price graph, Skyscanner "whole
   month" and "Everywhere" searches. One or two days earlier or later, or a nearby airport, often
   beats any code.
2. **Total cost, not fare.** Budget airlines charge for bags, seats and sometimes check-in. Price
   the trip with the bags the user will actually bring.
3. **Track instead of guessing.** Set price alerts on the exact route and dates.
4. **Book once it is good, then watch it.**
   - In the US, most bookings can be cancelled for a full refund within 24 hours.
   - Many full-service airlines no longer charge change fees on standard economy and up (not basic
     economy): if the fare drops, rebook and keep the difference as credit.
5. **Two one-ways** on different airlines can beat a return ticket.
6. **Points and miles** the user already has, and card travel credits, before paying cash.
7. **Hidden-city ticketing** (booking past your real destination) breaks airline terms. Airlines
   cancel onward segments, charge the full fare and ban accounts. Mention it only as a known risk;
   never book it without the user's explicit, informed choice.

Myth: incognito mode or clearing cookies does not lower fares. A 2026 Dutch regulator market study
and earlier academic and Consumer Reports tests found individual searches do not change prices.
Faking the country with a VPN is a different thing and can break the seller's terms.

## Hotels and stays

- Compare the same room and cancellation terms across booking sites and the hotel's own site.
  Member rates (free sign-up) often make booking direct cheapest, but not always.
- Book a free-cancellation rate early, re-check the price, cancel and rebook if it drops.
- Ask the hotel directly for a better direct rate, breakfast or an upgrade (see
  [../negotiation.md](../negotiation.md)).
- Apartments: compare the total including cleaning and service fees; ask for weekly or monthly
  rates on long stays.
- Best-rate guarantees exist at big chains but are hard to collect; do not count on them.

## Cars and transport

- Book a pay-later, free-cancellation car early and re-check before the trip.
- Check whether the user's credit card covers rental damage before buying the counter insurance.
- Trains and coaches: advance fares, railcards and passes for the user's age group or frequency.

## Travel insurance and perks

- Check the cards the user already holds: trip cancellation and delay cover, rental car cover,
  lounge access, free checked bags, travel credits. Do not sell them insurance they already have.
