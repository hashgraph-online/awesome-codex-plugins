# Starting an online store or dropshipping business

People usually start from a tutorial that names a stack of paid tools. Most of those links are
affiliate links. Keep the method, judge each tool on its own (see
[../sponsored-advice.md](../sponsored-advice.md), which includes a worked example).

## The cost stack, and the frugal move for each

| Cost | Typical pitch | Frugal move | Watch for |
|------|---------------|-------------|-----------|
| Store platform | "Free trial, then 1 USD/month for 3 months" | Take the intro offer, set a reminder before full price starts; compare the platform's cheapest plan that fits; WooCommerce or a marketplace (Etsy, eBay, Amazon) if the user wants no monthly fee before the first sale | Full price after the intro period, payment processing fees per order, extra fees for third-party payment gateways |
| Store builder or theme | "AI builds your store", premium themes | The platform's free themes and built-in AI tools | Upsells to a "pro store" |
| Product sourcing app | Paid automation app with a 1 USD trial | Free plans of supplier-integrated apps, or the supplier's own free app; pay for automation only after orders justify it | Monthly fee from day 4, product limits per plan |
| Product research | Paid "winning products" databases | Platform trend pages, social feeds, supplier best-seller lists, ad libraries that are free to search | Products every other store is already selling |
| Domain | "Free first year" on a new TLD | A `.com` or country TLD at an at-cost registrar | Renewal price of the free TLD |
| Logo and branding | Paid design tools | Free AI image tools, free design tools | Trademark conflicts |
| Product photos and videos | AI avatar UGC tools, paid creators | Film the real sample product; free editors; pay creators only for proven products | Subscription after free credits |
| Ads | "Run paid ads from day one" | Post organic videos first; pay to promote only the ones that already get views; small daily budget with a kill rule | Ad spend with no conversion tracking |
| Apps (reviews, upsells, email) | Install everything the tutorial shows | Install one at a time when a real need appears; audit monthly | Many apps bill monthly and add up |
| Samples | Often skipped | Always order samples of the hero product; cheap way to avoid refunds | Quality differences from listing photos |

## Honest unit economics (do this before spending on ads)

Per order: `price - product cost - shipping - payment processing - platform transaction fees -
returns and refunds allowance - ad cost per purchase = contribution`.

Per month: `contribution x orders - (platform plan + every app subscription + domain/12 +
other tools) = profit`.

If contribution per order is under about 30 percent of the price before ads, paid ads will
rarely make it profitable. Say so plainly.

## Legal and platform basics that cost money if skipped

- Business registration and sales tax or VAT rules in the user's country and the buyer's country.
- Refund, shipping and privacy policies the platform requires.
- Product safety and import rules (electronics, toys, cosmetics, anything for children or pets).
- Supplier shipping times shown honestly on the store; slow, unannounced shipping drives refunds
  and chargebacks.
