Changes vs 2024:
- GitHub no longer gives verified students free Copilot Pro: since 2026-03-12 they are on a "Copilot Student" plan, and since 2026-06-01 it is metered at 200 AI Credits/month with Auto-only model selection (teachers and OSS maintainers still get free Copilot Pro).
- Cursor's free year of Pro for students closed to new sign-ups on 2026-06-25 (cited fraud); replaced by campus-event credits.
- AI vendors now run time-boxed student promos instead of standing discounts: ChatGPT Plus 4 months free (US, claim by 2026-10-31), Google AI Pro 1 year free (US) or AI Plus (140+ markets) until 2026-12-31; Anthropic has no student price but pays US$3,600 Campus ambassador stipends and gives US K-12 teachers a free year.
- Microsoft's college offer changed from a free year of Microsoft 365 Premium to 50% off Microsoft 365 Personal; Adobe's student plan is now "Creative Cloud Pro" at $19.99 then $39.99/mo.
- GitHub Student Pack no longer lists DigitalOcean credits; JetBrains student licenses now bundle an AI subscription (completion, chat, agents incl. Codex/Claude Agent) plus an AI Pro trial.
