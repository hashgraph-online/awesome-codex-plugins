Changes vs 2024:
- Microsoft ended the free 10-seat Microsoft 365 Business Premium grant and the Office 365 E1 grant, effective at each nonprofit's first renewal on or after 2025-07-01. What remains is 300 free Business Basic seats, Business Premium at $5.50/user/mo, and the $2,000/yr Azure grant. Some orgs that missed the switch reportedly lost data.
- TechSoup is shrinking as a channel. Zoom left in May 2025 (now Zoom Cares) and Adobe Express left in September 2025. Goodstack is now the common verification partner for Zoom, Canva, Atlassian, Asana, OpenAI and Anthropic.
- Atlassian renamed the Community License to the Social Impact License. Standard/Premium for Jira, Confluence, Loom and Teamwork Collection went from 75% off to 100% off for teams of up to 25 users.
- AI vendors now have nonprofit tiers. Claude Team is $8/user ($3 in low and middle income countries, launched Dec 2025) and ChatGPT Business Standard is $8/user. Neither discounts API usage.
- The Twilio.org starter credit is $100, down from the $500 kickstart credit cited in older material.
