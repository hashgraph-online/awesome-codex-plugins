## Programs nobody thinks to ask about

These are common and rarely remembered. Ask about them only when the amount justifies it.

- **Employer perk portals** (often run by a benefits provider): discounts on phones, software, gyms,
  travel, cars. Ask "Does your employer have a perks or discount portal?"
- **Bank and card benefits:** card-linked offers, purchase protection, extended warranty, travel
  insurance, lounge access, streaming credits. Only the card's own benefits page is a reliable source.
- **Memberships:** warehouse clubs, auto clubs, alumni associations, unions, professional bodies,
  AARP-style senior groups, museum memberships with reciprocal entry.
- **Identity-verified discounts** for military and veterans, first responders, teachers, nurses and
  medical staff, seniors and young people, usually verified through SheerID, ID.me or similar.
  Only suggest these if the user may qualify; never suggest claiming a status they do not have.
- **Public and government programs:** library cards (free ebooks, audiobooks, films, courses,
  sometimes museum passes), energy-efficiency rebates and tax credits, public transport passes,
  low-income internet and phone programs. These vary by country and region: search the official
  government site for the user's location.
- **Accelerator and investor perk packs:** if the startup is in an accelerator or has a VC, the
  investor's portfolio perks often unlock the large credit tiers in [startups.md](startups.md).
