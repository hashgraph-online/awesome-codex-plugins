---
description: TS cohort-analyst `cohort-profile` mode — analyze student readiness data
---

Trigger the `cohort-analyst` skill in `cohort-profile` mode. Turns ability lists or questionnaire results into an aggregate cohort readiness profile (distributions, misconception prevalence, heterogeneity) with mandatory instrument-strength caveats; only aggregates enter the Course Passport.

Mode reference: `ts/MODE_REGISTRY.md`. Skill entry: `ts/cohort-analyst/WORKFLOW.md`.
