# Voice and positioning

Choose emphasis from purpose, target role or search direction, and verified record. Infer known inputs; clarify only a material missing fact. A generic resume needs no invented employer or JD.

## What the reader needs to see

This matrix synthesizes writing guidance and employer frameworks; it is not a proven callback formula. Titles vary; the actual mandate matters.

| Target | Foreground when supported |
|---|---|
| Senior hands-on engineer | System/component, constraints, personal design/implementation/debugging, operational or user effect. Preserve coding recency. |
| Staff / Principal IC | Architecture tradeoffs, technical direction, adoption and influence across teams. Line management is not implied. |
| EM / Head of Engineering | Team remit, hiring/coaching, product partnership, delivery and quality. Distinguish manager actions from team implementation. |
| CTO / executive | Company stage, mandate, strategic choices, organization, business and operational constraints. Keep technical proof; do not infer budgets, P&L, fundraising, or manager-of-managers experience from title. |
| Fractional CTO / consultant | Client problem, engagement type and duration, diagnosis, decisions, implementation or handoff within the actual mandate. Advice is not a shipped change. |
| Role or industry transition | Concrete transferable mechanisms, actual projects/training, and limits of direct experience. Preserve historical title and industry. |

## Preserve contribution and evidence

Use action, context, and known evidence: a metric, shipped artifact, documented decision, adopted process, or bounded contribution. If only the action is known, state it without its hoped-for effect. Team size describes scope; it does not itself establish a business result.

Preserve role and engagement type, public display names, NDA limits, dates and precision. Employment, implementation, and article publication dates are separate facts. A later article does not extend employment or prove ongoing advisory work.

For numbers, retain units, period, baseline, and whether they describe production observation, load test, design target, or estimate. Claim causation only when supported. Reframing preserves the real industry and contribution; missing JD experience remains a gap or unverified item.

Hypothetical examples below illustrate wording, not candidate facts:

- **Collaborative contribution:** “Designed payment retry rules; worked with the backend team to implement idempotency checks.” / «Спроектировал правила повторной обработки платежей; вместе с backend-командой внедрил проверки идемпотентности».
- **Advice:** “Assessed the release process and proposed rollback criteria.” / «Оценил процесс релизов и предложил критерии отката».
- **Agent-assisted work:** “Designed the service architecture and reviewed agent-generated implementation before deployment.” / «Спроектировал архитектуру сервиса и проверил подготовленную агентами реализацию перед развёртыванием».

Personal architecture and review with agent-assisted execution do not establish daily manual coding. “Contributed,” “supported,” or «участвовал» can be accurate when specific. A stronger verb must not increase ownership.

## Match the artifact

Application resumes select evidence; master profiles preserve the comprehensive record and uncertainty. Cover letters and application notes use natural first-person prose: one truthful reason for this role and relevant examples. Follow the requested length and the author's appropriate voice. A polite close is acceptable; a sales CTA is optional. Do not invent enthusiasm, company research, mutual contacts, or unique superiority.
