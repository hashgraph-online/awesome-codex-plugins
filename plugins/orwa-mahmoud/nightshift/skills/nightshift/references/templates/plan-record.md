# Plan Record

> The plan room's notebook. The planning conversation keeps it current as the discussion moves, so
> compaction, a closed tab or a new day loses nothing: entering the plan room reads it first and
> resumes from it. It records how a plan was reached; the plan itself is captured into
> `drafting-table.md`, and only on the owner's yes.

**Each entry** is one `- ` bullet below the rule, one per plan, with its notes indented under it:

```text
  - **<topic>** · open since <local time>
    - Where we are: <the exact point the conversation reached, and the next question>
    - Decided: <decision> — <why>
    - Rejected: <option> — <the reason; not proposed again without new evidence>
    - Open: <a question still waiting for an answer>
```

An entry stays open while its plan is being worked out. It closes by appending ` · captured: <the
drafting-table heading>` once the plan is in the drafting table, or ` · dropped: <why>` when the
owner sets it aside. Archive files closed entries with their shift and leaves open ones here.

A `Filed:` line points at plans already filed — follow it and search that file by topic.
