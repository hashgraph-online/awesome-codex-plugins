# Campaign case bank (mechanisms to borrow)

How to *use* these (levers L1–L12, the 5-point test, the `campaign.md` worksheet): `unconventional-marketing.md`. This file is the case bank.

These campaigns don't persuade. They create an experience or event people can't ignore, so users become the media. Each entry: what · mechanism · why it worked. ✓ = checked against a source this session; unmarked = well-known or from the user's notes; (u) = figures not verified.

## A · Physical mechanism: the product forces the action
- **Coca-Cola "Friendly Twist"** (2014, Colombia; the user's "co-op bottle opening" favourite) — gear-shaped caps open only when two people twist them together; 3,000 bottles in campus vending machines in the first week of term. You can't drink without making a friend.
- **Milka "Last Square"** (2013, FR/DE) — 10M bars missing one square; a code lets you send that square to someone. The defect becomes a gift ritual. (u: +33% sales, Cannes Gold)
- **Milka "Let's Neighbor Up"** ✓ (Poland, Sept 2024, Ogilvy) — 100,000 parcels sent to the flat next door in new-build blocks; inside, two bars, one for the neighbour who brings it over. The "Neighborly" edition sold out: 5.5M buyers. Ran for three years (door hangers 2025, nameplates 2026).

## B · Spatial impossibility: a scene that only exists on site
- **Skyworth wallpaper TV** ✓ (China, autumn 2026) — bus-shelter ads where the TV "slides out" of the frame: "Not the installer's fault — the TV is just too thin." Passers-by think the ad is broken; some brought a TV remote to pose.
- **Lixin (立信) "paper TV" bus shelter** (Shenzhen, Sept 2026) — a red board says the TV is "too thin, like paper", with workers seemingly mid-installation. The brand comes from the user (seen first-hand); a Weibo video shows the board but doesn't name the brand. Same device as Skyworth: the medium itself proves the product is thin.
- **Mizone protruding bottle** (2025) — half a 1.5 m bottle bursting out of bus-stop ads. Mixed reviews; the controversy itself spread.
- **Nike × Zheng Qinwen** (Shanghai, 2025) — poster on one bank of the Huangpu, a giant ball on the other: she "hit it across the river". The space between two places is the content.
- **Carlsberg "Probably the best poster"** (2015, London) — a billboard that pours free beer. The promise becomes a taste.
- **Mercado Livre "Field Barcode"** ✓ (Cannes 2026 Outdoor GP) — stadium grass mowed into a 104 m barcode, scannable from the TV broadcast for 25% off.

## C · Story: borrow a moment everyone already feels
- **"What is Peppa?" (啥是佩奇)** (2019, China) — a rural grandpa builds a "Peppa" out of a blower for his grandson; Spring Festival guilt about not going home.
- **Red Bull Stratos** (2012) — a jump from ~39 km, live on YouTube; 8M+ concurrent viewers. The brand didn't claim energy; it staged a human limit.
- **Biti's Hunter "Đi để trở về"** ✓ (Vietnam, 2017–18) — an online #teamđi vs #teamtrởvề debate, answered with a Tet MV; reported sales +300% in a week (agency source).

## D · Co-creation: the user is the hero
- **Spotify Wrapped** — your year as shareable cards: "this is me", not "Spotify is good".
- **Lay's "Do Us a Flavor"** — fans invent flavours for a prize; buyers become inventors.
- **Uber Eats "Build Your Own Super Bowl Commercial"** ✓ (Cannes 2026 Media GP) — remix 1,000+ unaired clips in the app; each combo unlocks a deal; best Super Bowl sales (+$36M).
- **Mixue jingle** ✓ — public-domain melody, fans made 20+ language covers on Bilibili.
- **Photo-booth collab frames** ✓ (Korea → Asia) — Photoism and Life4Cuts sell limited-time branded / idol frames in selected branches; fans travel to collect them.

## E · Gamification: turn a product fact into a behaviour
- **Jif "Text PEANUT 1,300 times"** ✓ (Canada, Oct 2024, Leo Burnett Toronto) — a 1 kg jar holds 1,300+ peanuts; the first 1,300 people to text PEANUT 1,300 times got a free jar. Spam culture flipped.
- **Burger King magnet wall** ✓ (Singapore, Bugis MRT, June 2025) — a wall of fries-shaped magnets; each one is a voucher. Seeing and getting are the same act.
- **Kaufland "TURBOKADDY"** (Czechia, 2022) — a "grandparents' supermarket" builds a gamer shopping cart with an esports team. (u: +222% consideration)
- **Budweiser "One Second Ads"** ✓ (Cannes 2025 Audio GP) — guess the song from one beat.

## F · Hijacked channel: the medium is the idea
- **Hyundai "Coquí Alarmed"** ✓ (Cannes 2026 Audio GP) — a tourist's complaint about Puerto Rico's frogs → rental cars' lock beep became the coquí call; buyers asked for it, it became a feature.
- **999 Cold Remedy "dialogue billboards"** ✓ (China, Jan 2026) — next to Jackson Yee's Huawei ad: "the handsome guy next door… wear more layers" (a meme about him refusing long johns). Also "blown crooked by the wind" boards in Beijing and an upside-down "666" board for the gaokao.
- **Whopper Detour** ✓ — the rival's restaurants unlock a 1¢ Whopper.

## G · Imitable content: the ad is a meme
- **Old Spice "The Man Your Man Could Smell Like"** (2010) — absurd one-take, then hundreds of personalised video replies; endlessly parodied.
- **Dove "Real Beauty Sketches"** (2013) — two sketches of each woman, one from her own description and one from a stranger's; she discovers it herself.
- **Moldy Whopper** ✓ — a 34-day time-lapse as proof.
- **Yili "嚼柠檬" / Luckin × Duolingo "brand wedding"** ✓ (China, 2025) — absurd, meme-native plots with offline pop-ups.

## Shared principles
1. **Mechanism > event.** It keeps working inside normal use (a cap, Wrapped, a broken-looking ad).
2. **Bind the action to the core need.** Not "encourage sharing": no friend, no Coke; no peeling, no fries.
3. **User is the hero, brand is the tool.**
4. **Embed in an existing moment or emotion** (first week of term, Tet, waiting for the bus, a cold snap, a celebrity meme).
5. **Physical impossibility is the scarcest content.** It can't be seen online, so people have to go and film it.
6. **Proof over claim; channel is the idea; plan the afterlife.**

## Our own accepted case: tutti.pics (2026-10-24, live at tutti-site-bi5.pages.dev; method in `web-craft.md`, code in `assets/web-hero/`)
- **What** — pre-launch site for a photo app with no App Store page yet; job: collect a beta list and spread. One full-bleed key visual: a live, finger-pushable wall of the slogan in huge warped type, a real person cut out in front, grain. Each tap = camera flash → a new poster, and every poster is one of the app's real templates with its real 6-character share code.
- **Mechanisms stacked** (each verified on the live site): L-media-hijack (`/p/CODE` prints only the code, no brand; curiosity → search → `/c/CODE`), fake-broken (404 is a grey "broken" wall; tap to "fix" = flash = the product's action), product-as-demo (drop your own photo → local cutout → you are on the poster; "save" exports the poster with the code = share card), visible scarcity (waitlist rank, +5 places per invited friend), daily look (the opening poster changes every day).
- **Why it was accepted** ("非常好了，非常完美了……这回真的很好"): the page *is* the product doing its thing to a person; nothing on it is a description of the product. Round 1 (page layouts graded against references afterwards) was rejected as "web 感太重，完全不是一个事情".
- **Afterlife loop**: code on every exported image → code page → try with your photo → waitlist → invite link with the poster attached.
