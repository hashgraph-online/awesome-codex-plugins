# Suede AI Prospecting Skills: Worked Handoff

## Inputs

Request: “Find three design partners for a developer release tool.” Supplied ICP:
B2B SaaS, 10–50 engineers, a public release-process pain stated within the last
90 days, and an identified public business contact route. Supplied evidence is
two current company pages and one dated public issue. No contact validator is
available. Use the Demand-signal branch because learning from first customers,
rather than list coverage, is the objective.

## Expected output

Return the evidence report from `demand-signals.md`: candidate, quoted pain,
source URL, capture date, ICP fit, signal age, confidence, demand-fit score with
its component rationale, disqualifiers, and the unresolved question. Keep the
public issue's author separate from a verified company decision-maker. Rank only
the candidates actually supported by the supplied evidence; a target of three
does not justify inventing a third. State the search scope and sources read.

## Failure case

An issue mentions a problem but identifies no company or business contact. Keep
it as a demand observation, not a send-ready lead. An email guessed from a domain
is `unverified`; exclude it from send-ready exports. Do not silently promote a
forum participant to a business prospect or scrape a social profile to fill gaps.

## Validation

Open each retained source and confirm the exact pain, date, and company mapping.
Check that every score uses the Demand-signal rubric rather than Hot/Warm/Cold,
each rejected candidate has a reason, and no row implies validated contact access
or outreach. The handoff ends with evidence questions, not a sent-message claim.
