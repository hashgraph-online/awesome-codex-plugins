# Worked contract: Suede AI Free Tools Skills

This is an illustrative application, not a claim of measured customer results.

## Input

A bookkeeping product wants a free invoice-fee calculator. Users enter invoice amount and payment fee; the paid product automates reconciliation.

## Expected output

Scope a calculator with explicit currency, fixed versus percentage fees, rounding rule, a worked example and an un-gated result. Give the evaluation scorecard, MVP exclusions, indexable explanation and events for result completion and qualified product interest.

## Failure and validation

For amount 100, percentage 2 and fixed 0.30, the illustrative fee is 2.30 in the selected currency. Reject negative amounts and missing units instead of returning a plausible number. Do not collect contact details before the basic result unless the gating decision justifies it.
