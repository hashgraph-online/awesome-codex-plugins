# Title evidence and decision examples

Fictitious illustrations, not search performance claims.

| Page | Observed symptom | First action | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| /docs/billing | HTML title has unresolved placeholder; visible H1 is Billing API | Repair template fallback, test locale variants | Broken markup is reproducible and within control |
| /services/copy | Search displays a healthy alternate title for one query | Record the query and its displayed text; no forced rewrite | Query variation is normal and the text is accurate |
| /guides/migration | Aggregate CTR fell amid a shift to mobile and worse positioning | Split comparable query/device/market and rank cohorts before assigning cause | Raw before/after CTR mixes different exposure |

A useful proposal tells readers what they get, in words the page supports.
If a heading says "Workspace migration guide" and the page is limited to
exporting content, do not propose "Complete workspace migration in minutes."
A candidate like "Export content from your workspace" matches the reader's
actual change of state. Preserve meaningful product names from approved
references rather than trading them for an unsupported category phrase.
