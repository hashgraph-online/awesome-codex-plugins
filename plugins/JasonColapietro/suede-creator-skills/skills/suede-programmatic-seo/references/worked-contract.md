# Suede AI Programmatic SEO Skills: Worked Contract

This is a synthetic application scenario, not a client result or a required output filename. Adapt the values to the supplied evidence. The skill's existing output format and authority boundaries apply.

## Input

A service business proposes location pages for fifty cities. Only ten cities have actual coverage records and locally useful service details. The remaining rows contain city names alone.

## Expected output

Return the Output Contract with the chosen playbook, source provenance, data schema, quality criteria, representative page sample, linking/sitemap plan, phased rollout, and expansion KPIs. Scope the useful sample to rows with genuine coverage; record which proposed rows lack page-specific value.

## Missing input and recovery

When a source row is stale or rights are unclear, preserve its status and owner in the data model instead of filling it with generic paragraphs. Report the consequence of the failed quality check under the advisory gate policy. Do not represent unbuilt or unpublished pages as live.

## Validation

Compare two generated pages after removing city names: both must retain distinct useful information. Check source freshness, canonicals, indexability, internal links, and sitemap membership on the representative sample. Validate the sample before recommending expansion.

## Detailed scope and routing

Suede AI programmatic SEO for data-backed page systems at scale: location, persona, integration, comparison, template, glossary, and directory pages. Chooses the playbook, defines the data model and template, and enforces index-worthiness gates against thin, doorway, and scaled-content-abuse patterns, plus internal linking, sitemaps, phased rollout, and the KPIs that decide whether to expand. Use when planning, building, or rescuing many templated pages. NOT FOR: auditing an existing site (use suede-seo-audit); AI answer-engine visibility and measurement (use suede-ai-seo); planning editorial content (use suede-content-strategy); publishing bulk pages without approval.
