# Worked contract: Suede AI Cold Email Skills

This is an illustrative application, not a claim of measured customer results.

## Input

A qualified finance lead said on a supplied public interview that month-end reconciliation consumes two days. The product brief documents a reconciliation dashboard; no time-saving result is supplied.

## Expected output

Draft a 2-to-4-word subject and a body under 75 words connecting the cited problem to the documented capability. Ask one question, such as whether a workflow comparison would help. Return the interview URL beside the personalization and a bounded follow-up schedule.

## Failure and validation

Do not convert the two-day pain into a claim that the product saves two days. If the interview cannot be retrieved, use a verified company fact or mark personalization pending; do not invent an opening. Draft delivery does not send mail.
