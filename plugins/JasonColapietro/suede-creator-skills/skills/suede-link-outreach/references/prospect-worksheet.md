## Review external vendors separately

Record the fee and what is included; any auto-renewal, cancellation or refund terms; who selects and edits the placements; whether visible sponsorship labels and link attributes match the arrangement; observed audience overlap; and one example verified independently. A network that will not show the publisher or promises concealment does not qualify as earned coverage. Do not interpret existing budget as purchase approval.

**Draft shape:** Identify the concrete reader problem, give the evidence and its limitation, then note how the editor could include or ignore it. For multiple prospects, vary the actual argument and evidence instead of swapping company names in a form letter.
