# Suede AI Marketing Council Skills: Worked Contract

This is a synthetic application scenario, not a client result or a required output filename. Adapt the values to the supplied evidence. The skill's existing output format and authority boundaries apply.

## Input

A founder must choose between improving a self-serve onboarding funnel and testing partner-led acquisition this quarter. Available capacity is one engineer and one marketer. No partner conversion evidence exists.

## Expected output

Use council-session mode with three to five relevant roles. Each returns its owned lens, evidence, proposed action, and failure condition. The chair names the actual disagreement, recommends one bounded next step, and identifies the observation that would reverse that recommendation.

## Missing input and recovery

If an advisor needs acquisition cost data that was not supplied, mark it missing and propose a measurement task. Do not convert a simulated role opinion into external expert testimony. A request for a real person becomes the closest role lens with that substitution stated.

## Validation

Verify the synthesis contains one recommendation, at least one explicit disagreement, and an evidence test with an owner and review date. Remove any sentence attributed to a real person without a real supporting source.

## Detailed scope and routing

Suede AI marketing council: twelve role-based simulated advisors (Positioning Strategist, Offer Strategist, Search Visibility Strategist, Brand Reach Scientist, and others) apply named Suede frameworks to one bounded marketing decision, map the disagreements and the evidence that would settle each, and return a chair's synthesis with next steps and skill handoffs. Use when the user wants expert lenses, a simulated advisory board, a pressure test of a contested or irreversible bet, or a skeptic's read before committing. NOT FOR: impersonating or quoting real people, primary research (use suede-customer-research), writing the plan itself (use suede-marketing-plan), or executing the chosen tactic (route to the relevant Suede skill).
