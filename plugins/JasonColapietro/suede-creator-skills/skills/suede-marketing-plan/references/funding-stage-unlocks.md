# Funding and Budget Capability Unlocks

Funding labels do not determine a marketing budget, hiring plan, or channel mix.
Use this reference to document what *verified resources* make possible and what
remains gated.

## Evidence Required

For every proposed unlock, record:

- available cash, runway floor, and board-approved burn;
- current team capacity and named accountable owner;
- measured funnel, retention, margin, and blended-CAC ranges;
- current vendor price, commitment, cancellation terms, and data rights;
- operational dependencies such as creative, sales, onboarding, and support;
- approval state, maximum exposure, review date, and stop conditions.

If any input is missing, label the unlock `hypothesis` or `blocked`. Never infer
spend or headcount from `pre-seed`, `seed`, `Series A`, or another stage name.

## Capability States

### Current

Work the existing team can execute with approved accounts, cash, data, and
review capacity.

Document:

- live channels and their measured constraints;
- active owners and weekly capacity;
- committed tools and contracts;
- known evidence gaps and compliance limits.

### Approved Test

A bounded experiment with explicit approval.

Document:

- hypothesis and decision;
- exact channel and audience;
- maximum cash and time exposure;
- success, pause, and stop conditions;
- owner and review date.

### Conditional Unlock

Work that becomes possible only after a named condition is confirmed, for
example, financing closes, an owner is hired, tracking is verified, or a test
meets its decision rule.

Do not schedule or spend against a conditional unlock before the condition and
approval are both verified.

### Deferred

Work that does not fit current evidence, capacity, risk, or strategy. State the
reason and the smallest new evidence that could change the decision.

## Applying It to the Plan

### Section 3: Current State

List verified resources, owners, contracts, constraints, and approval status.
Do not assign a standardized tier.

### Sections 4–8: AARRR

Mark every move `current`, `approved test`, `conditional unlock`, or `deferred`.
Tie it to evidence and an owner.

### Section 9: 90-Day Roadmap

Include only current work and approved tests. Conditional work may appear as a
decision checkpoint, never as an assumed execution task.

### Section 10: Outlook

Show scenarios tied to explicit conditions. Use dated low/base/high assumptions
from `budget-planning.md`; do not present a funding-stage benchmark as a
forecast.

### Section 11: Operations Stack

Map approved tools and public Suede skills to accountable owners. A tool route
does not prove capacity or replace headcount.

## Category Adjustments

Category changes the questions, not the answer:

- **Consumer:** verify acquisition economics, platform fees, creative capacity,
  and retention before paid scale.
- **B2B:** verify sales-cycle length, pipeline capacity, contract value, and
  enablement ownership.
- **Hardware:** include inventory, fulfillment, returns, and blended margin.
- **Regulated / clinical:** require qualified legal, claims, privacy, and
  evidence review.
- **Marketplace:** model supply and demand separately.
- **Developer / open source:** distinguish community adoption from commercial
  conversion.

All recommendations remain client-specific and approval-bound.
