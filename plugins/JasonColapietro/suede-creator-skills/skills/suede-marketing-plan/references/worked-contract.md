# Suede AI Marketing Planning Skills: Worked Contract

This is a synthetic application scenario, not a client result or a required output filename. Adapt the values to the supplied evidence. The skill's existing output format and authority boundaries apply.

## Input

A two-person SaaS team has eight marketer-hours per week and a fixed monthly test budget. Retention is healthy in the supplied cohort report, but acquisition evidence is thin. The request is a shortlist, not a full annual plan.

## Expected output

Score no more than eight candidate tactics using the existing rubric and return at most five. Each selected tactic names its audience, capacity and cost, first test, success metric, review date, and stop condition. Explain why the rejected high-effort tactic lost given the actual team constraint.

## Missing input and recovery

If the budget is missing, state the capacity assumption and keep paid spend uncommitted while drafting a bounded shortlist. Do not fabricate CAC, organic demand, or expected revenue to complete a table. Escalate only the missing decision that prevents a concrete plan.

## Validation

Sum planned weekly hours and test costs and compare them with supplied caps. Check that every selected tactic has a stop condition and a measurable first test. A shortlist request must not silently expand into a thirteen-section operating plan.

## Detailed scope and routing

Suede AI marketing planning in two modes. Shortlist mode scores up to 8 tactics from a 142-idea tactic library (including AI answer visibility, entity consistency, and comparison and pricing pages as search assets) on audience, stage, evidence, capacity, cost, and risk, and returns at most 5 with test, metric, review date, and stop condition. Full plan mode writes a 13-section AARRR operating plan (acquisition, activation, retention, referral, revenue) with a 90 day plan, 12 month roadmap, ops stack, idea bank, and measurement, sized to the client's real budget, team, and evidence. Use when the user is stuck and wants a shortlist of growth tactics, or needs a growth plan, go to market document, or roadmap. NOT FOR: isolated channel execution (use the relevant Suede skill), contested strategic bets that need debate (use suede-marketing-council), recurring workflows (use suede-marketing-loops), or positioning discovery alone (use suede-product-marketing).
