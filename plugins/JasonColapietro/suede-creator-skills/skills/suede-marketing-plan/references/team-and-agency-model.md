# Team and Agency Model: Ownership Before Org Charts

This reference helps assign work. It does not prescribe a first hire, title,
agency type, or in-house/outsourced ratio from funding stage or company size.

## Start With the Work

For each required capability, document:

- outcome and decision it supports;
- current owner and weekly capacity;
- skills, access, and data required;
- risk, confidentiality, and approval boundary;
- duration: durable ownership, bounded project, or temporary surge;
- success evidence and review date.

Do not open a role or vendor search until the gap is explicit.

## Ownership Options

### Existing Internal Owner

Use when the work requires durable company context and the owner has capacity,
authority, and the needed skill. Protect time and define what stops.

### New Employee

Consider when evidence shows a durable workload, close product/customer context
is essential, and the company can support recruiting, management, compensation,
and a realistic ramp. Title follows actual scope and reporting structure; never
assign it from hire order.

### Contractor or Specialist

Consider for bounded deliverables or specialized capacity. Require:

- precise scope and acceptance criteria;
- data/access minimization;
- IP, confidentiality, security, and compliance terms;
- maximum cost and change-control approval;
- named internal reviewer and handoff plan.

### Agency

Consider when coordinated multi-person capacity is demonstrably needed. Compare
the actual senior team, relevant work, commercial model, data handling, minimum
commitment, exit terms, and references. Agency size is not a proxy for fit.

### Defer or Automate

Defer work that lacks evidence, owner capacity, or strategic fit. Automation may
reduce repetitive effort only after the process, permissions, review gate, and
failure path are understood.

## Vendor Decision Scorecard

Score each candidate from evidence, not category reputation:

| Dimension | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Relevant delivery | Comparable, verifiable work and references |
| Senior attention | Named delivery team and allocation |
| Operating fit | Communication, cadence, and change control |
| Data / security | Access model, retention, subprocessors, incident path |
| Commercial fit | Total cost, minimums, payment, renewal, exit |
| Handoff | Documentation, assets, accounts, and knowledge transfer |

Get explicit approval for the selected scope, vendor, maximum cost, terms, and
access before signing or provisioning anything.
