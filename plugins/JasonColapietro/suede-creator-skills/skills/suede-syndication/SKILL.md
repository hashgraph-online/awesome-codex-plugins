# Suede Syndication Skills

Start with the [placement packet and checks](references/placement-packet.md) when documenting the asset, decisions, and handoff.

Start with a single canonical owned source and confirm its key claims, sample, media assets and intended audience. Be explicit about how a second channel adds value. Repackaging should give that audience a useful entry point, not create pages only to borrow a host's visibility.

| Destination | What to prepare | What to verify |
|---|---|---|
| Trade/media desk | Short factual release with a dated event or finding, relevant business contact and evidence link | Editorial relevance, contact consent, embargo, fee and paid labeling |
| Developer publication | Reproducible walkthrough with constraints and a source link | Native canonical support, code/media permissions, duplicate-content settings |
| External opinion/article channel | A self-contained lesson adapted to its readers, with a disclosed author relationship | Platform rules, attribution, asset rights and current URL behavior |

If a paragraph-for-paragraph republication is appropriate, use the platform's canonical facility where supported; treat canonical as a hint, not guaranteed indexing behavior. If the host cannot express the intended relationship, prefer a genuinely distinct piece with a clear reference to the original. No hidden sponsorship or invented authority.

Return the owned URL, a brief per destination, exact proposed titles, asset and attribution inventory, canonical decisions, approval requirements and a recheck table for live URL, actual canonical, referral traffic and meaningful conversions. Keep public contact data minimal and permissioned. Do not submit, publish, set up accounts or buy distribution from this skill.

## Separate evidence and audience decisions

Before drafting, record original URL and author, publication state and date, the claim or method to reuse, and the source or permission for each chart, quote, screenshot and downloadable file. Identify information that must stay private: customer identifiers, workspaces, email or phone numbers, contract amounts, unreleased features, and internal or timed events. A permission to publish a chart does not establish permission to distribute underlying records. Use a dedicated media address or named business contact only if approved, and remove inadvertently visible private details from images as well as text.

Pick the reader action per destination. For a technical outlet, this may be to reproduce a benchmark or check a false-positive rate; for a trade outlet, it may be to understand who is affected by a dated change. Establish that each asset's lead, example and next step are useful independently. A merely rewritten introduction to the same body is not a distinct article. If a third party wants full text, verify its syndication and attribution rules and note who can change the placement after publication.

## Record publication and discoverability accurately

For true republication, record the specific canonical URL the editor should enter and how it will be checked in the rendered source. An attribution link is not a canonical tag. HTTP redirects, indexing directives, authentication and host templates can override expectations; a draft being submitted is not evidence of a workable live page. If the destination does not support the requested relationship, prepare a differentiated summary, an excerpt with permission, or note that the placement is a poor fit. Preserve a self-canonical original where appropriate. Do not promise that a search engine will choose either URL based on this metadata alone.

Keep fee, sponsorship disclosure and editorial control on the same decision row. A paid promotion may be legitimate, but label it separately from editorial pickup and never hide the material relationship. For attribution, collect the approved tracking scheme without changing the original canonical or adding private details to query strings. Report actual referral data with dates and a useful denominator, like qualified inquiries per referral visit, not just a count of placements. Return unconfirmed placements as pending rather than assigning any traffic or link benefit to them.

## Boundaries

- Do not use third-party hosts merely to benefit from their ranking reputation.
- Separate paid placements, disclosure, link attributes and posting approval.
