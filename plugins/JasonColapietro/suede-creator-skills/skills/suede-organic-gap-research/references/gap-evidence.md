# Organic gap evidence example

These rows are fictional. Before evaluating one, retain the raw provider
provenance privately and normalize page-level duplicates without merging
distinct language or geography variants.

| Evidence | Decision | Why | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Two comparables rank with short integration guides; own current page already outlines the integration | Refresh owned integration page | The actual task is setup, not purchase; avoid creating another overlapping URL | Recheck current result types and render the updated setup steps |
| A highly trafficked competitor glossary targets a subject the product never implements | No action | High volume estimate does not imply commercial fit | Record why the user would leave unsatisfied |
| One magazine links to an independently sourced comparison chart replicated across 12 articles | Verify one prospect | Publication context and the original citation matter, not twelve backlink rows | Confirm the source URL, outbound link, editorial fit, and owned alternative |

For stages requiring external communication, stop at candidate plus draft.
Record scope, sender, audience and live evidence so an authorized outbound
workflow can make a decision later.
