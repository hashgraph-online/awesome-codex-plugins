# Suede AI Newsroom Skills: Worked Contract

This is a synthetic application scenario, not a client result or a required output filename. Adapt the values to the supplied evidence. The skill's existing output format and authority boundaries apply.

## Input

A founder supplies an interview transcript and one product walkthrough. The team wants one flagship draft and three distribution drafts. No performance data from earlier campaigns is available.

## Expected output

Create the campaign record and role handoffs. Give the flagship one angle, then assign distinct supported entryways to the three drafts, such as proof, mechanism, and workflow. Include source locations, the editor's collision check, and the human approval queue. Log performance as unmeasured.

## Missing input and recovery

If the source has no result evidence, the result entryway is unavailable. If two drafts make the same central claim, identify the pair and shared claim and return the existing collision options. Do not solve the collision by changing only wording or inventing a proof object.

## Validation

Reduce each draft to one sentence, check claim/opening/entryway collisions, and apply the three standalone checks. Verify that every consequential agent-added claim traces to the evidence packet and no draft was published.

## Detailed scope and routing

Suede AI editorial pipeline for agent-run content: six roles with written contracts, one inspectable campaign record, and a distribution stage that gives every asset its own argument instead of a shorter draft. Use when content agents return generic, duplicated, or unsourced work, when one flagship piece has to carry a week of distribution and become the page search engines and AI answers cite, when someone asks to build an AI content team or one-person media company, or when a founder wants a weekly interview-led content system across personal and company accounts. NOT FOR: choosing topics, pillars, or clusters (use suede-content-strategy); platform mix, cadence, calendars, listening, or Instagram programs (use suede-social); running a stage on a schedule with state and stop conditions (use suede-marketing-loops); lane ownership for agents changing code (use suede-agent-teams); writing the flagship piece itself (use suede-ship-copy).
