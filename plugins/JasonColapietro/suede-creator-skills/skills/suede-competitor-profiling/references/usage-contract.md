# Worked contract: Suede AI Competitor Profiling Skills

This is an illustrative application, not a claim of measured customer results.

## Input

Compare two named project-management tools for a ten-person agency. URLs and the agency product brief are supplied; paid SEO access is unavailable.

## Expected output

Save dated pricing and product captures, then create one profile per tool and a comparison summary. Keep advertised annual prices distinct from monthly billing and from any inferred effective price. Mark search-volume and backlink measurements unavailable.

## Failure and validation

A pricing page that requires login is an unknown, not a zero-cost plan. A search snippet is a discovery lead until the underlying page is checked. Every synthesized factual claim should resolve to a saved capture and URL.
