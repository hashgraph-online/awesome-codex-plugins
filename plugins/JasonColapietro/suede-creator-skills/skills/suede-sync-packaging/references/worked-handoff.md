# Suede AI Sync Packaging Skills: Worked Handoff

## Inputs

Request: “Make a sync one-sheet for this track.” Synthetic supplied facts: a
confirmed master path, instrumental missing, clean version unknown, lyrics
provided, tempo 96 BPM, drums drop out at 1:48, AI involvement not declared,
and no documented publishing split. The user names a documentary montage as the
target world; no sample issue is reported or resolved by that statement.

## Expected output

Fill every asset as exists/missing/unknown before draft copy. A scene-fit line
may describe the supplied arrangement: “A 96 BPM montage cue; drums drop out at
1:48 for a dialogue break.” Keep instrumental missing, clean version unknown,
publishing split unknown, and AI involvement unknown in the one-sheet and open
clearance questions. Screen the supplied lyrics for the listed flag classes.
Use every required one-sheet field, including contact and link availability;
do not pad the scene list to three or label the song one-stop.

## Failure case

No confirmed master path means no completed sync angle. An unresolved sample
triggers the documented risk/choice path; do not silently proceed to pitch copy
or remove the OPEN flag. Without lyrics, write “not screened, lyrics not
provided” instead of “clean.” A rights gap routes to the audit mode of
`suede-rights-passport`; this skill does not clear it.

## Validation

Compare every musical statement with the provided facts or inspected media.
Re-read the one-sheet field by field and confirm every OPEN/UNKNOWN item survived
verbatim. Check that no banned vague musical adjective replaced evidence and no
one-stop, placement, clearance, or sent-email claim appeared. The result is a
review package with a named next review step.
