# Worked delivery contract

This synthetic scenario demonstrates the expected artifact shape; its numbers and outcomes are example inputs, not measured customer results.

## Input

Prepare prelaunch iOS metadata for an offline pantry app. Supported functions are barcode entry, expiry reminders and shopping lists. Locale is en-US; no keyword volume or conversion history is supplied.

## Expected output

Return proposed title/subtitle/keyword fields with exact character counts, feature-to-claim evidence and a screenshot sequence showing real supported flows. Label keyword priority as a hypothesis until search evidence exists. Preserve a separate list of facts needed from the console before a performance audit.

## Failure case

The request includes competitor download estimates without a source. Omit them from factual scoring and identify the missing evidence. Do not invent rank, volume, ratings or conversion lift for an app that has not launched.

## Validation

Check each field against current official locale/store rules and count the exact string, including punctuation. Confirm screenshots match the named build and claims do not introduce unsupported capabilities.

## Coverage and routing

Suede AI app-store optimization for keyword fields, titles, subtitles, descriptions, screenshots, ratings context, and competitor listing audits. Use when improving App Store or Google Play visibility or listing conversion from a live app URL and current console evidence, or when authoring keyword, name, subtitle, and promo fields for an app that has not shipped yet. NOT FOR: building or releasing the app (use android-app-factory or site-to-ios-app; native iOS builds are a private Suede AI companion, not in this pack: ios-app-factory), creating paid ad assets (use suede-ad-creative), or install-event instrumentation (use suede-analytics).
