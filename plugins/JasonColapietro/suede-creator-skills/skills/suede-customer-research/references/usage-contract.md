# Worked contract: Suede AI Customer Research Skills

This is an illustrative application, not a claim of measured customer results.

## Input

Synthesize six onboarding interviews and 40 support tickets. Two interview quotes describe the same customer; the goal is to identify activation friction.

## Expected output

Produce a source index, verbatim quote bank, themes and jobs to be done. Count unique customers separately from mentions. A theme can state that two of five unique interviewees struggled with imports; ticket frequency is a separate sample.

## Failure and validation

Missing speaker identity means the unique-customer denominator is uncertain. Do not join potentially duplicate people by guesswork or turn a hypothesis into a persona fact. Preserve source locations while removing unnecessary personal identifiers from the handoff.
