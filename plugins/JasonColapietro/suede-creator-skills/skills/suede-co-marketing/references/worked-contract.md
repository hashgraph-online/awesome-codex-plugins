# Worked delivery contract

This synthetic scenario demonstrates the expected artifact shape; its numbers and outcomes are example inputs, not measured customer results.

## Input

A scheduling product and an invoicing product serve independent consultants. Both can contribute a guide and promote it to their own opted-in audiences. No permission exists to exchange subscriber lists.

## Expected output

Score audience fit and reciprocity from supplied evidence, leaving unknown criteria as ?. Return a bounded joint-guide campaign with each owner, promised asset, approval date, canonical landing page, separate sends and aggregate KPIs. Keep lead sharing disabled unless an explicit consent/terms design supports it.

## Failure case

One candidate has audience fit 5 but reciprocity 2. Report scored-and-dropped with the failing criterion; do not let a large audience compensate for the mandatory reciprocity threshold.

## Validation

Check the two 4+ thresholds before pursuing a candidate, cap the shortlist at five, and verify each commitment has an owner. Distinguish agreed promotion from a proposed pitch and use compatible denominators for shared results.

## Coverage and routing

Suede AI co-marketing discipline: partner fit scored on audience overlap and reciprocity, joint offers and campaign formats (content, webinars, integrations, community), operating roles, lead-sharing and consent terms, shared measurement, and joint content built to earn links and AI citations for both brands. Use when identifying potential partners or planning a specific joint campaign or cross-promotion. NOT FOR: affiliate or customer-referral programs (use suede-referrals), product-launch orchestration (use suede-launch-packaging), or partner-facing sales collateral (use suede-sales-enablement).
