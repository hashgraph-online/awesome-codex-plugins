# Co-Marketing Campaign Formats

Read this when selecting the format for a joint campaign: the output skeleton
requires the selected format to be named from one of the four families below.

## Content Partnerships

| Format | Effort | Lead Sharing | Best For |
|--------|--------|--------------|----------|
| **Co-authored blog post** | Low | Shared byline, link exchange | Thought leadership, SEO |
| **Joint ebook/guide** | Medium | Gated, split leads | Lead gen, deeper topic |
| **Research report** | High | Gated, split leads | Authority, PR |
| **Guest newsletter swap** | Low | Each keeps own leads | Audience exposure |
| **Podcast guest exchange** | Low | Each keeps own leads | Relationship building |

## Webinars & Events

| Format | Effort | Lead Sharing | Best For |
|--------|--------|--------------|----------|
| **Joint webinar** | Medium | Gated, split leads | Lead gen, product education |
| **Virtual summit panel** | Medium | Host keeps registrations | Multi-partner exposure |
| **Co-hosted workshop** | High | Gated, split leads | Hands-on education, deeper engagement |
| **Conference booth sharing** | Medium | Each keeps own scans | Cost splitting, audience overlap |
| **Joint happy hour/dinner** | Low | Each keeps own leads | Relationship building at events |

## Product & Integration Marketing

| Format | Effort | Lead Sharing | Best For |
|--------|--------|--------------|----------|
| **Integration launch** | Medium | Each keeps own signups | Existing integration partners |
| **Joint case study** | Medium | Each keeps own leads | Shared customers |
| **"Better together" landing page** | Low | Split by form owner | Integration discovery |
| **Bundle or discount** | Medium | Split by purchase path | Conversion boost, cross-sell |
| **In-app cross-promotion** | Medium | Each keeps own users | User activation |

## Community & Social

| Format | Effort | Lead Sharing | Best For |
|--------|--------|--------------|----------|
| **Social media takeover** | Low | Each keeps own followers | Audience exposure |
| **Joint giveaway/contest** | Low | Gated, split entries | List building, engagement |
| **Slack/Discord community collab** | Low | Each keeps own members | Community building |
| **Joint AMA or Twitter Space** | Low | Each keeps own leads | Thought leadership |

## Lead-sharing shorthand

Two arrangements cover almost every format above, and the campaign plan must
state which one applies:

- **Gated / split**: one shared form, leads delivered to both parties under an
  agreed split. Requires a written data-handling and consent arrangement.
- **Each keeps own**: no shared list; each side keeps whoever it brought.
  Simpler, and the default when the parties have not agreed data terms.
