# Caption review checklist and handoff

## Evidence schema

| Start | End | Track | Speaker or region | Text | Confidence and issue | Origin and QA method |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 00:00:09.200 | 00:00:12.640 | Speech | Speaker 1 | Example cue | Confirmed | User-provided recording; playback checked |
| 00:00:10.000 | 00:00:14.000 | Visual | Lower third | Example role; name partly cut off | Partial | User-provided video; frame checked |

## Validate before delivery

1. Match the exact playback file name/version and confirm whether the time base includes an intro.
2. Confirm cue bounds, positive durations, ordered times, syntax, and intentional versus accidental overlap. Use three spaced playback checks to catch an offset or drift.
3. Verify all public quotes, names, amounts, and corrections, or make the exact unresolved interval and source restriction visible.
4. Keep cue data, visual annotations, translation, and editorial notes separately labeled.
5. Record who may receive the media, captions and notes. Never transfer a private file to make a requested format convenient.

**Negative fixture:** Captions are provided with a five-minute gap and the recording is unavailable. Keep the gap explicit, offer chapters based on available segments, and withhold any wording or timecode for missing speech. Do not label the transcript as playback verified.
