# Platform Limits and Verified Facts

Platform limits, composer behavior, and account entitlements change often. The
facts below were checked in October 2026 against the sources listed. Anything
marked "verify" was not confirmed against an official page in that check; read
the current help page or the authenticated composer before relying on it.

In every run:

1. Check the official help page or the authenticated composer.
2. Record account tier, surface, locale, and the check date.
3. Keep copy under the verified hard limit with room for links and disclosure.
4. Preview truncation and attachments before approval.
5. Treat hashtag count and placement as test variables, not reach levers.

## Verified changes that affect planning (2024 to 2026)

| Platform | Fact | Effective | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Posts and Reels accept at most 5 hashtags. Instagram's guidance: a few targeted tags beat many generic ones. | Announced December 2025 | Instagram @creators on Threads, reported by Blog du Modérateur, Dec 19, 2025 |
| Instagram | Reels can run up to 3 minutes. Profile grid previews moved to a taller 3:4 crop. | January 2025 | Instagram announcement, Jan 2025 |
| Instagram | Public photos, videos, carousels, and Reels from professional accounts of adults can appear in Google and other search engines. On by default; opt out under Settings, Privacy. | July 10, 2025 | Instagram Help Center; PPC Land, "Instagram content becomes searchable on Google" |
| YouTube | Shorts can be up to 3 minutes for square or vertical uploads made on or after Oct 15, 2024. A Short over 1 minute with an active Content ID claim is blocked globally. | October 15, 2024 | https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877 |

## Evidence rules

- A hard limit needs a current official source or an authenticated composer
  readback.
- A recommendation needs current account or campaign evidence.
- A starting range is labeled a hypothesis and compared with a baseline.
- Never claim fewer hashtags, a placement choice, or a feature improves reach
  without a controlled comparison on the current account.
