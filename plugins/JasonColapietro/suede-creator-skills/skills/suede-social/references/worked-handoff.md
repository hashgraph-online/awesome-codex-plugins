# Suede AI Social Skills: Worked Handoff

## Inputs

Request: “Audit our Instagram growth and plan next week.” Supply the account,
goal, 30 recent posts with dates and format, reach/views, profile actions,
follows, qualified leads or sales where measured, and audience context. If only
screenshots of five posts exist, the inspected sample is five, not a 30-post
audit. Supply rights-cleared product assets and approved factual claims for drafts.

## Expected output

Build the Account Evidence Pack and select audit plus planning mode. Separate
views from profile visits, follows, and business outcomes. Name which goals each
post serves, identify repeated patterns using the actual sample, and return an
approval-ready calendar using the main skill's row schema. Each proposed post
has a platform-native asset contract, sourced claim, reader action, and measured
goal. A Reel idea can test a product demonstration; it cannot claim a measured
follower gain before execution.

## Failure case

Missing conversion tracking means lead and sales impact is unmeasured. Avoid
calling the account successful from views alone. If live access is unavailable,
use supplied evidence and disclose the sample. If an asset lacks rights or a
claim lacks support, return the exact replacement needed; do not borrow footage
or fill a calendar with unverified testimonials.

## Validation

Check all date ranges and denominators, distinguish paid from organic where
known, and inspect each draft's current platform limits and safe areas. Verify
captions, links, proof, brand assets, and any consent needed for people shown.
Report the content as drafted; publishing, comments, DMs, and scheduling remain
separate authorized actions, never assumed from a request for a plan.
