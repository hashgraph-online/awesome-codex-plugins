# Asset Packages: Short Video, Carousel, Story

Use these templates after the Account Evidence Pack and objective are known.
They fit Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn document posts, and
Instagram or Facebook Stories. Check the platform limits in
[platform-limits.md](platform-limits.md) before setting duration or slide count.

## Hook selection

Score every candidate 0–4 on each item:

1. Target viewer recognition.
2. Specific payoff.
3. Proof support.
4. Honest curiosity gap.
5. Voice fit.

Reject a hook when any promised result lacks proof, the hook could apply to
almost any niche, or it requires fake fear, outrage, or scarcity.

### Useful faceless modes

- product or website screen recording;
- hands-on process or studio detail;
- owned before/after evidence;
- licensed B-roll with original narration;
- animated diagrams and kinetic type;
- document, data, or workflow walkthrough;
- customer result with documented permission;
- original stills edited into a paced sequence.

Do not use anonymous stock footage that conflicts with the claim or audience.

```text
Slide 2: problem or stakes / evidence
Slide 3: idea, step, or proof / visual
...
Final slide: earned summary / one CTA

Caption (first line carries the search term):
Alt text (describe the slide content, not "carousel image"):
Source and claim notes:
Experiment variable:
Primary metric:
```

### Slide QA

- Cover identifies audience or payoff in one read.
- Each slide makes one claim or performs one step.
- Every statistic, quote, result, and comparison has a source.
- Text fits at mobile size without requiring a zoom gesture.
- Visual hierarchy, page number, and progress signals are consistent.
- Final CTA follows naturally from the delivered value.

## Stories package

Use Stories for a specific job: research, proof, objection handling, launch
support, community reply, or conversion. Do not fill a daily quota.

```text
Frame | visual | exact text | sticker/interaction | purpose | next frame
```

When using polls, questions, links, or product stickers, verify that the feature
is available on the authenticated account and that its response path is staffed.

## Voiceover markup

```text
[pace: conversational]
[emphasis: exact word]
[pause: 0.3s]
[beat]
[pronounce: word = phonetic guide]
```

Use punctuation for comprehension first. Never make the voice sound urgent,
intimate, or emotional in a way the account's real samples do not support.
