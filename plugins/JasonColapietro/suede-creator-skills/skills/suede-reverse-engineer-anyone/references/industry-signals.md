# Industry Signals

What changes by industry: the curve source, the signals worth logging, what Stage 3 may do, the rules that limit copying, the plays that usually transfer, and the metric to watch. Confirm the industry after Stage 1 and load its section. When a target spans two (a marketplace for local services, a creator who sells a course), load both and apply the stricter guardrail wherever they differ.

Legal notes are dated pointers, not legal advice: check the current rule, and counsel for regulated claims, before copying a play. Every Stage 3 action needs per-action approval under the user's real identity (`go-inside.md`). Routing follows `play-routing.md`.

---

## SaaS

- **Curve:** visits from one vendor plus brand search; dev tools use monthly downloads from one registry, GitHub stars as `secondary`.
- **Signals:** free-plan limits over time; integration listing dates; template gallery growth; changelog cadence; readable experiment names; `/vs` pages; pricing unit changes (seat to usage or credits); review spikes; sales hiring by region; partner programs; public peers' filings.
- **Stage 3:** trial to first result; hit the free limit; demo only under the user's name; log emails around trial end. No extra accounts to stretch a trial; read terms for anti-competitor clauses.
- **Copying limits:** accurate comparison claims; review-platform incentive rules; CAN-SPAM, GDPR, PECR; trademark use in ad text.
- **Plays:** template pages (`$suede-programmatic-seo`); comparison pages (`$suede-competitors`); free limits and upgrade wall (`$suede-pricing`, `$suede-paywalls`); faster first result (`$suede-onboarding`); integration listings (`$suede-co-marketing`, `$suede-directory-submissions`); AI answers (`$suede-ai-seo`); affiliates (`$suede-referrals`).
- **Watch:** sign-up to activation, free to paid, net revenue retention, time to value.

## Ecommerce and DTC

- **Curve:** visits from one vendor plus brand search.
- **Signals:** catalog with publish dates and best-seller order from the public storefront; installed apps; sale calendar from homepage captures; new ad creatives per week (count new, not only running); longest-running ads; creator partnership ads; discount depth from compare-at prices; wholesale or stockist pages.
- **Stage 3:** subscribe through the popup; abandon a cart and log whether the discount grows; buy once within the purchase budget. Never place orders meant to be cancelled; SMS only on the user's own number.
- **Copying limits:** FTC Endorsement Guides; 2024 FTC fake reviews rule; TCPA for texts; ROSCA and state auto-renewal laws (California, July 1, 2025); real prior prices behind "was" claims.
- **Plays:** creative testing (`$suede-ad-creative`, `$suede-ads`); capture popup and welcome offer (`$suede-site-alchemy`, `$suede-emails`); cart and post-purchase flows (`$suede-emails`, `$suede-sms`); bundles and thresholds (`$suede-offers`); creator and affiliate program (`$suede-influencer-outreach`, `$suede-referrals`).
- **Watch:** blended CAC and MER, contribution margin per order, repeat purchase rate.

## Consumer Hardware and Crowdfunding

- **Curve:** brand search interest when traffic is thin; read it launch by launch. A flat curve can mean sold out.
- **Signals:** crowdfunding backers, totals, and update dates; archived reservation pages; "ships in" text; stockouts; dated launch reviews; disclosed creator seeding; gift-guide placement; retailer logos; model generations; FCC equipment authorization filings (a pre-launch signal).
- **Stage 3:** join the pre-launch list; buy to keep within budget and log upsells, inserts, setup app, review-request timing. No device teardowns; no reviews of a rival's product.
- **Copying limits:** FCC rules before authorization (47 CFR 2.803); the FTC Mail Order Rule on ship times; crowdfunding prototype and render rules; Magnuson-Moss warranties.
- **Plays:** pre-launch list with referral (`$suede-referrals`, `$suede-marketing-plan`); reward tiers (`$suede-offers`); embargoed seeding (`$suede-influencer-outreach`, `$suede-public-relations`); post-purchase flow (`$suede-emails`); demo videos (`$suede-video`).
- **Watch:** list-to-order conversion, return rate, attach rate.

## CPG, Food, and Beverage

- **Curve:** store count from archived locator captures (`primary`), brand search (`secondary`).
- **Signals:** retailer announcements; "where to buy" list; retail media listings; shelf resets in trade press; new SKUs; retailer ratings; promotion frequency; sampling and demos; trade-show exhibitor lists; broker hiring.
- **Stage 3:** buy at a retailer and the DTC shop within budget; note on-pack QR codes. Never pose as a retail buyer; never buy to distort a store's sales data.
- **Copying limits:** FDA labeling and claims ("healthy" rule, December 2024); FTC substantiation and Green Guides; TTB and three-tier rules for alcohol.
- **Plays:** creator and sampling program (`$suede-influencer-outreach`); retailer launch PR (`$suede-public-relations`); where-to-buy pages (`$suede-programmatic-seo`); coupon and recipe emails (`$suede-emails`, `$suede-lead-magnets`); sampler bundles (`$suede-offers`).
- **Watch:** points of distribution, velocity per store per week, repeat rate.

## Retail and Omnichannel

- **Curve:** store count from archived locator captures, brand search as `secondary`.
- **Signals:** openings and closures; new-store job posts by city; location page quality; reviews and reply rate per location; circular cadence; loyalty changes; app rank; local inventory ads; a retail media network page; FDDs for franchise retail.
- **Stage 3:** loyalty sign-up; app onboarding; pickup flow within budget; in-store photos of displays only where policy allows. Never photograph staff or customers.
- **Copying limits:** reference pricing rules; state privacy notices for loyalty incentives; TCPA; FTC Franchise Rule; ADA accessibility.
- **Plays:** store pages (`$suede-programmatic-seo`, `$suede-seo-audit`); reviews per location (`$suede-referrals`); loyalty emails (`$suede-emails`); app install push (`$suede-aso`); grand openings (`$suede-public-relations`); SMS offers (`$suede-sms`).
- **Watch:** same-store sales, loyalty penetration, new store ramp.

## Franchising

- **Curve:** open units from FDD Item 20 and archived locators; brand search as `secondary`. Signed is not open.
- **Signals:** FDDs from free state databases; Item 20 openings, closures, transfers; Item 19 performance; fees and marketing obligations (Items 5, 6, 7, 11); SBA loan data by brand; Discovery Days; broker and portal presence; per-unit reviews.
- **Stage 3:** request franchise info only if the user is genuinely exploring buying one. Never contact franchisees listed in Item 20.
- **Copying limits:** FTC Franchise Rule (FDD 14 days ahead; earnings claims only via Item 19); state registration and ad filing; July 2024 FTC guidance on undisclosed fees.
- **Plays:** per-unit location pages (`$suede-programmatic-seo`); investor webinars (`$suede-community-marketing`, `$suede-emails`); development site on unit economics (`$suede-copy`, `$suede-site-alchemy`); broker cost per sale (`$suede-revops`, `$suede-attribution`).
- **Watch:** net unit growth, signed-not-open pipeline, cost per franchise sale.

## Local and Home Services

- **Curve:** visits from one vendor; roll-ups use location count.
- **Signals:** Local Services Ads and the Google Verified badge by market; reviews per location monthly; call-tracking swaps by source (calls are the conversion); service-area page rollout; acquisitions and rebrands; lead marketplace presence; membership and financing offers; storm and seasonal ad starts; contractor licenses.
- **Stage 3:** contact only if the user is a genuine customer at their own address. No fake quotes; a truck roll costs real money. Never record a call without consent.
- **Copying limits:** LSA verification (Google Verified replaced Google Guaranteed, October 2025); Business Profile bans on stuffed names and virtual offices; FTC reviews rule (October 21, 2024).
- **Plays:** city and service pages with local proof (`$suede-programmatic-seo`); review requests after each job (`$suede-referrals`, `$suede-sms`); call tracking (`$suede-attribution`); memberships (`$suede-offers`); storm-response ads (`$suede-ads`).
- **Watch:** cost per booked job, speed to lead, review velocity.

## Residential Solar and Home Energy

- **Curve:** visits from one vendor, read against the policy timeline first.
- **Signals:** net metering and incentive dates (the federal residential credit ended for systems installed after December 31, 2025); state interconnection data; service-area expansion; financing partners; canvassing recruiting; dealer programs; lead aggregators; complaints and enforcement; licenses.
- **Stage 3:** quote only for a home the user owns and would consider; forms often sell details to several installers. Never allow a credit check or signature.
- **Copying limits:** FTC Cooling-Off Rule and solicitor permits; state disclosure documents; substantiated savings and tax credit claims.
- **Plays:** savings calculator (`$suede-free-tools`); state and utility pages (`$suede-programmatic-seo`); battery offers after outages (`$suede-emails`, `$suede-offers`); customer referrals (`$suede-referrals`); fast quote follow-up (`$suede-revops`).
- **Watch:** cost per install, lead to contract, cancellation rate.

## Healthcare

- **Curve:** visits from one vendor; multi-site groups add location and provider counts.
- **Signals:** condition page rollout; "medically reviewed by" bylines; reviews per location; payer directories; pixel removals and consent changes over time; ad claims; intake quiz up to health questions; state availability; NPI records; telehealth licenses; Open Payments.
- **Stage 3:** never book an appointment the user won't keep; never enter real or fake health information; stop intake before health questions. Asking the front desk about price under the user's name is fine.
- **Copying limits:** HIPAA and HHS tracking guidance (no PHI to pixels); FTC Health Breach Notification Rule; FDA drug ad rules and FTC health claims; Anti-Kickback Statute and Stark Law.
- **Plays:** clinician-reviewed condition pages (`$suede-content-strategy`, `$suede-programmatic-seo`); provider pages with schema (`$suede-seo-audit`); post-visit reviews (`$suede-referrals`); price-revealing quiz (`$suede-lead-magnets`); consent-aware tracking (`$suede-analytics`).
- **Watch:** cost per booked appointment, show rate, new patients.

## Care Services

- **Stage 3:** tours or assessments only for the user's own family need; no inquiries to placement services; never photograph residents or children.
- **Copying limits:** Anti-Kickback Statute for federal-program referrals; state placement agency disclosures; licensing terms in ads; HIPAA; parental consent.
- **Plays:** family decision guides (`$suede-content-strategy`, `$suede-lead-magnets`); location pages with ratings (`$suede-programmatic-seo`); tour follow-up (`$suede-emails`); referral partners (`$suede-co-marketing`); caregiver recruiting (`$suede-ads`).
- **Watch:** occupancy or enrollment, inquiry to tour, caregiver turnover.

## Fitness and Wellness

- **Signals:** presale pages for new locations; intro offer history; pricing by location; cancellation terms over time; aggregator listings; challenge calendar; reviews per studio; instructor followings; corporate wellness deals; seasonal offers (January, pre-summer).
- **Stage 3:** intro class only if someone attends; log the intro-window messages; cancel an approved intro membership and record each step. No member photos.

- **Plays:** intro offer test (`$suede-offers`); intro-window follow-up (`$suede-emails`, `$suede-sms`); location presale (`$suede-marketing-plan`); challenges (`$suede-community-marketing`, `$suede-referrals`); compliant save offers (`$suede-churn-prevention`).

## Legal and Professional Services

- **Curve:** visits from one vendor plus brand search.
- **Signals:** practice and location page rollout; paid search on problem terms; TV, billboard, and podcast ads; directory rankings; partner count; case results pages; thought leadership cadence; intake and chat tools; SEC Form ADV; public dockets; lead generator presence.

## Real Estate

- **Stage 3:** no showings or valuations for homes the user won't buy or sell; never enter someone else's address.

## Finance

- **Curve:** visits from one vendor; banks add FDIC quarterly deposits.
- **Signals:** CFPB complaints by month; FDIC deposits; SEC filings; sponsor bank disclosures; Form ADV and BrokerCheck; sign-up and referral bonus history; rate and APY history; comparison-site placement; credentialed bylines; calculators; regulatory actions; NMLS licensing.
- **Stage 3:** approval before any application; credit products can mean a hard pull, so prefer pre-qualification. Never apply in another's name or enter false details.
- **Copying limits:** Regulation Z and DD trigger terms; UDAAP on bonus and "free" claims; FINRA Rule 2210; Google financial verification; Meta's financial special ad category.
- **Plays:** two-sided cash referral (`$suede-referrals`); calculators (`$suede-free-tools`); pre-qualification step (`$suede-site-alchemy`); comparison list placement (`$suede-public-relations`); credentialed content (`$suede-content-strategy`); time-limited bonus (`$suede-offers`).

## Insurance

- **Curve:** visits from one vendor; written premium from statutory filings where available.
- **Signals:** SERFF rate filings (competitive rates can explain growth in a state); state licenses; paid search on "[line] insurance"; aggregator placement; quote flow length (count questions); embedded partnerships; agent network size; complaint ratios; Medicare enrollment-season ads.
- **Stage 3:** run a quote to the price screen without binding; warn the user about score checks and agent calls; only the user's own true details.
- **Copying limits:** state producer licensing and unfair trade practice laws; anti-rebating rules; CMS Medicare marketing rules; TCPA consent.
- **Plays:** shorter quote flow (`$suede-site-alchemy`); state and line pages (`$suede-programmatic-seo`); quote abandonment follow-up (`$suede-emails`, `$suede-sms`); embedded partnerships (`$suede-co-marketing`); coverage calculators (`$suede-free-tools`).
- **Watch:** quote completion, bind rate, cost per bound policy.

## Crypto and Web3

- **Curve:** active addresses or TVL from one public dashboard; compare with peers in the same market cycle.
- **Signals:** on-chain activity; points and airdrop announcements; retention after incentives end; governance proposals; grants; integration and exchange listings; referral codes; token unlocks; paid attention programs; community size; audits and incidents; licensing by region.
- **Stage 3:** a wallet the user controls; never request seed phrases; no signing or spending without review; KYC only under the user's identity. Never farm incentives with multiple wallets.
- **Copying limits:** securities treatment of tokens and yield; GENIUS Act for stablecoins (July 18, 2025); ad platform licensing; MiCA and UK FCA promotion rules.
- **Plays:** referral program (`$suede-referrals`); integration listings (`$suede-co-marketing`); build-in-public posts (`$suede-social`); how-to docs (`$suede-content-strategy`); community roles (`$suede-community-marketing`).

## Gambling and Betting

- **Curve:** state regulator handle or revenue reports where published; otherwise visits from one vendor. Line up market launch dates first.
- **Signals:** license and launch dates by jurisdiction; welcome offer history (offers shrink as markets mature); affiliate footprint; promo codes; sponsorships; app availability by state; responsible gambling features; filings.
- **Stage 3:** only if the user is of legal age and located where it's legal; no VPNs or false locations; stop at identity verification; no deposits without a set amount; never multi-account bonuses.
- **Copying limits:** licensing per jurisdiction; state ad rules and the AGA code; UK Gambling Commission and CAP codes (bonus wagering capped at 10 times since January 2026); never target minors or vulnerable people.
- **Plays:** per-market launch plan (`$suede-marketing-plan`, `$suede-offers`); compliant affiliates (`$suede-referrals`); market pages (`$suede-programmatic-seo`); sponsorship codes (`$suede-co-marketing`, `$suede-attribution`).
- **Watch:** first-time depositors, CAC, net gaming revenue.

## Regulated Goods

Alcohol, cannabis, hemp, tobacco and vaping, firearms.
- **Curve:** markets live and doors from regulator and retailer data; brand search as `secondary`. Closed ad platforms make traffic understate growth.
- **Signals:** TTB COLA approvals (dated); state license footprint; state sales data; dispensary menu and retailer app presence; distributor announcements; FDA tobacco authorizations; DTC shipping states; age-gated lists; ambassador programs.
- **Stage 3:** buy only where legal, of legal age, with approval; never misstate age or location. Prefer public menus and locators.
- **Copying limits:** TTB labeling and ad rules and audience-age codes (above 70% of legal age by code); federal cannabis scheduling (partly changed April 2026); FDA tobacco rules; platform bans.
- **Plays:** age-gated email, SMS only where allowed (`$suede-emails`, `$suede-sms`); retail and menu presence (`$suede-co-marketing`); membership club (`$suede-offers`); state launches (`$suede-marketing-plan`, `$suede-public-relations`).
- **Watch:** doors, depletions, markets live, owned list growth.

## Manufacturing and Industrial

- **Stage 3:** no fake RFQs. Download gated CAD under the user's identity and log follow-up. Never apply as a fake dealer.

- **Plays:** product and application pages (`$suede-programmatic-seo`); gated CAD with fast follow-up (`$suede-lead-magnets`, `$suede-revops`); distributor program (`$suede-co-marketing`); trade show follow-up (`$suede-cold-email`); account outreach (`$suede-prospecting`).
- **Watch:** RFQs, spec-in wins, distributor sell-through.
