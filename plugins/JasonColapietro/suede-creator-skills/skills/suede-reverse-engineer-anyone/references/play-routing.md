# Play Routing

Every play names the Suede skill that runs it and, for repeat work, the loop that keeps it going. Use this table to route each one. Write the play's prompt so it opens with that skill (`$suede-...`) and passes along the teardown's specifics: the formula, the page family, the evidence link, the metric.

## Loops and the Monthly Watch

Use `$suede-marketing-loops` to schedule any loop named above, with the teardown's files as its state.

**The monthly growth-move watch loop** keeps a teardown alive. Author it in `$suede-marketing-loops`, modeled on its competitor-watch loop, at a monthly cadence:

1. Re-collect the Stage 1 and Stage 5 signals (page scripts, pricing and headline captures, sitemap families, DNS, certificate logs, job board, reviews, ad libraries, paid promotion sweep, last month's posts, new interviews). Write a fresh `moves.csv`.
2. Diff against last month's log:
   ```bash
   node <skill-dir>/scripts/timeline.mjs diff --old teardowns/<target-slug>/moves-YYYY-MM.csv --new teardowns/<target-slug>/moves.csv --out teardowns/<target-slug>/new-moves.csv
   ```
3. Report only meaningful new moves (a new ad network, page family, loop, pricing change, content format, release pattern, hiring burst in one channel, complaint theme), plus any launch radar flag from `hidden-signals.md`. "No new moves" is the usual result.
4. For each new move, say whether it changes a play and which Suede skill takes it.
5. **State:** the dated move logs in `teardowns/<target-slug>/`. **Stop:** when the user ends the watch or the target goes quiet for three months.

## Plays That Don't Transfer

Some plays worked because of something the user doesn't have: a large round, an existing audience, a famous founder or feature artist, a label budget, a platform deal, a viral moment someone else made. Those go in plays to skip, with the reason. Every play also needs its metric tracked; if the user can't measure it today, add a `$suede-analytics` step to the prompt.

## Writing the Prompt

A good prompt gives the skill everything it would otherwise ask for:

1. **The skill:** "Use $suede-programmatic-seo."
2. **The evidence:** what the target did, its label, and the link.
3. **The user's version:** the specific asset, scaled to the user's stage.
4. **The formula or house style** from Stage 6, for content plays.
5. **The metric** and when to check it.

## Example

> Use $suede-programmatic-seo. Acme's `/templates/*` family (480 pages, Observed in Wayback CDX, pulled 2026-10-30) carried about 41% of their non-brand search traffic (Semrush estimate, pulled 2026-10-30), and it existed four months before their growth inflection. We have 12 template pages. Plan a template page family for our invoicing product: the template types our customers search for, the page structure, the data each page needs, and internal links from our pricing and features pages. Start with the 20 highest-volume template keywords. Track impressions for `/templates/` in Search Console weekly, and check at 60 days.

> Use $suede-campaign-in-a-box. [Artist]'s last three singles each ran a 21-day rollout: a 15-second hook clip on day 1, a lyric card on day 7, a pre-save push on day 14, and a live version 10 days after release (Observed, links in `moves.csv`). Each rollout sat inside a cluster with +9 to +14 pp lift in monthly listeners (one tracker, pulled 2026-11-01). Plan the same four-beat rollout for our next single, scaled to two posts a week. Track pre-saves and day-7 streams, and check 14 days after release.
