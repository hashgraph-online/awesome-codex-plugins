# Suede AI Sales Enablement Skills: Worked Handoff

## Inputs

Request: “Build a one-page battle card for a competitive renewal.” Supply the
current product specification, approved customer proof, competitor pricing URL
with capture date, discovery notes, and the intended sales audience. Synthetic
constraint: the product spec confirms CSV export but supplies no evidence that
onboarding is faster than the competitor's.

## Expected output

Use the battle-card fields in the main skill: when encountered, their pitch,
where we win, pushback, discovery questions, pricing context, and proof. A
supported differentiator can state the observed export capability with its
source. Replace “faster onboarding” with a discovery question about the buyer's
actual onboarding requirements until evidence exists. Add the claim-source table
with source/date or modeled assumptions and external-use approval for every
number, name, and capability. Keep the card scannable during a live call.

## Failure case

Competitor pricing is behind authentication or has changed since the capture.
Mark it unavailable or stale and specify the required verification; do not guess
an enterprise price or present an old price as current. A customer logo without
permission stays out of external collateral. Return the draft and the exact
approval or evidence gap rather than sending it to a prospect.

## Validation

Read every claim against its source and compare any public comparison page for
consistency. Check that an internal model is labeled with its inputs and that no
modeled savings is described as a customer outcome. Confirm a rep can locate the
buyer-specific question, proof, and likely objection without reading a deck.
