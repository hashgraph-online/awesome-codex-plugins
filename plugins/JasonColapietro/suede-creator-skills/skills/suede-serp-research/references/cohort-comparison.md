# Comparable SERP cohort example

All observations below are fictional. Put AI references and local/shop result
packs in separate columns so they are not counted as ranked organic URLs.
The landed URL and first URL may differ; retain both when the redirect affects
whether the result represents the same task.

| Query pair | Cohort | Shared landed organic results | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| “site move steps” and “site migration checklist” | Same market, device and time | 7 of common depth 10 | Likely one guide, after confirming the successful pages provide a task sequence |
| “plumber near me” and “how to fix a drain” | Same market, device and time | 1 of 8 | Likely separate tasks: hiring and education; confirm owned coverage before making a new page |
| “what costs to migrate a site” and “agency migration cost” | Mixed continents and result types | Not comparable | Recollect or mark unknown; do not estimate overlap from keyword semantics |

A citation result should name the specific source URL, how it supported
the answer, and the observed feature. Do not infer from a result URL's
presence alone that it supplied a claim or that the feature ranks it
the same as conventional organic search.
