# Suede AI Lead Magnet Skills: Worked Contract

This is a synthetic application scenario, not a client result or a required output filename. Adapt the values to the supplied evidence. The skill's existing output format and authority boundaries apply.

## Input

A small B2B invoicing product targets freelance designers. Support tickets show confusion about late-payment reminders. The team can maintain one checklist, has no conversion baseline, and wants qualified trials rather than a large list.

## Expected output

Recommend a reminder checklist with a public, indexable explanation and an optional downloadable worksheet. Specify audience job, contents, completion time, form fields, delivery confirmation, consent language placement, and the next product action. Measure qualified trials per delivered download alongside capture and delivery rates.

## Missing input and recovery

When no licensed examples or original data exist, build the checklist from product expertise and mark research gaps. Do not invent a customer result, conversion benchmark, or permission to reuse a third-party template. Missing delivery access produces a delivery specification, not a claim that subscribers received it.

## Validation

Have a reader complete the checklist without a sales call. Check that each form field has a qualification or delivery purpose, unsubscribe/consent handling has an owner, and metric denominators distinguish visitors, downloads, delivered emails, and trials.

## Detailed scope and routing

Suede AI lead-magnet strategy: choosing a format (checklist, template, swipe file, guide, quiz, mini-course, original data), setting useful depth, deciding what to gate and what to publish as an indexable page that earns links and AI citations, landing page and delivery, and measuring qualified capture. Use when the user needs a downloadable or gated resource exchanged for contact details, or wants a lead magnet that also ranks. NOT FOR: interactive calculators, graders, or generators (use suede-free-tools), lifecycle sequences after capture (use suede-emails), or production copy (use suede-copy).
