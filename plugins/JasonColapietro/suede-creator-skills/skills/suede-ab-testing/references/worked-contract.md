# Worked delivery contract

This synthetic scenario demonstrates the expected artifact shape; its numbers and outcomes are example inputs, not measured customer results.

## Input

A checkout has 10,000 eligible sessions per week, a 4% purchase baseline and a proposed change to shipping-price visibility. The owner proposes a 10% relative improvement, not ten percentage points.

## Expected output

Write the hypothesis, randomization unit, primary purchase metric and denominators, 4.0% control and 4.4% alternative rates, chosen alpha/power and sample-size calculation inputs. Predeclare duration, a refund guardrail, assignment checks and the one-time or sequential decision rule. Return a plan; do not claim a winner before observations exist.

## Failure case

The export reports users in one arm and sessions in the other. Mark the readout non-comparable, retain raw counts and request the common unit/window. Do not compute significance on incompatible denominators.

## Validation

Recalculate absolute versus relative uplift and sample-size inputs independently. Ensure every arm uses the same eligibility/window and the guardrail stop is distinguished from a winning result.

## Coverage and routing

Suede AI experimentation discipline: hypotheses, sample size and power, test duration, guardrail metrics, significance and readouts, sequential designs, and an ICE-ranked experiment backlog with a reusable playbook. Use when comparing variants, deciding whether a result is reliable, or building an experiment program and cadence. NOT FOR: analytics instrumentation (use suede-analytics), post-click conversion diagnosis (use suede-site-alchemy), writing the variant copy (use suede-copy), or channel-level incrementality and geo holdouts (use suede-attribution).
