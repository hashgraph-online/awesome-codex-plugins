## Input

A support team reports twelve weekly escalations. Agents say each takes twenty minutes; a manager describes only the final approval step. The scope is escalation handling, not the entire company.

## Expected output

Return the Output Contract with the operator process map, silo inventory, friction quantified in the supplied units, ranked opportunities, engagement signal, and a thirty-day adoption observation plan. Preserve the team's figures as given and distinguish every inferred process step or estimate with [assumed].

## Missing input and recovery

If no operator is available, record that the map reflects a manager account and leave operator-only steps missing. Do not replace the supplied time with an industry benchmark. A system outside authorized access stays outside the read set; use an interview or export request instead.

## Validation

Reconcile the unit math: twelve escalations times twenty minutes is four hours per week. Check ownership for intake, action, approval, and exception handling. Ensure any agent-created benchmark is sourced or [assumed], never presented as the team's measurement.

## Detailed scope and routing

Suede AI operations-assessment discipline that maps how work actually happens before anyone designs a system: floor-level interviews with the people who do the work, an inventory of every silo including spreadsheets and inboxes used as databases, friction quantified in the requester's own numbers, opportunities ranked by annual value against build complexity, and adoption watched for the thirty days after launch. Use when nobody can answer what to automate, when an operation needs auditing before a build, when tribal-knowledge and key-person risk has to surface, or when a shipped system is quietly being routed around. NOT FOR: deciding what to build and in what order once the map exists (use suede-ops-architecture); researching external customers rather than internal operators (use suede-customer-research); lead lifecycle, scoring, and CRM routing rules (use suede-revops); product instrumentation and dashboards (use suede-analytics); getting a new user to first value (use suede-onboarding).
