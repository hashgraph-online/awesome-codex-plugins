# Worked delivery contract

This synthetic scenario demonstrates the expected artifact shape; its numbers and outcomes are example inputs, not measured customer results.

## Input

A software company wants to be cited for inventory alerts. It supplies its domain, product documentation, canonical organization facts and a fixed set of ten buyer questions.

## Expected output

Return a crawler-policy inventory distinguishing search access from training choices, entity conflicts with exact URLs, two citation-ready page briefs and the fixed prompt-panel protocol. Record engine, date, query and observed cited URLs for each sampled answer. Define citation share with its denominator and preserve zero/failed observations.

## Failure case

An answer engine is unavailable for three questions. Report seven completed and three unavailable observations; do not turn access failures into observed absence or claim a full ten-question visibility score.

## Validation

Reopen each factual source and distinguish a recommendation from a citation. Re-run the same panel under comparable conditions, retain the raw answers and report observed movement without promising ranking or citation outcomes.

## Coverage and routing

Suede AI answer-engine and generative-engine optimization (AEO/GEO) program for getting a brand cited and recommended in Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Copilot, Gemini, and Claude. Sets AI crawler policy (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended), entity and knowledge-graph consistency (Organization and Person schema, sameAs), citation-worthy page patterns, off-site consensus, llms.txt and agent-readable files, and AI visibility measurement: prompt panels, share of citations, AI-assistant referrals in GA4, and the Search Console and Bing AI performance reports. Use for AI SEO, AEO, GEO, LLMO, AI citations, AI visibility, optimize for ChatGPT or Perplexity, llms.txt, or OKF. NOT FOR: a scored nine-lane technical and on-page audit (use suede-seo-audit); a fast A-F promotion-readiness grade (use suede-visibility-grader); conversion-path rework (use suede-site-alchemy).
