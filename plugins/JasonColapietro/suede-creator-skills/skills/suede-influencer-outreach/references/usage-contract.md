# Worked contract: Suede AI Influencer Outreach Skills

This is an illustrative application, not a claim of measured customer results.

## Input

A vetted bookkeeping creator published a supplied tutorial on invoice cleanup. The offer is a fixed 500 fee for one tutorial, with no performance guarantee; the creator lists a manager contact.

## Expected output

Draft one manager-directed pitch citing the tutorial and explaining fit, deliverable, fixed fee and one low-friction ask. Return source evidence and two proposed follow-ups with dates. Keep negotiating details separate from the approved initial offer.

## Failure and validation

If the compensation currency or usage-rights term is missing, identify it instead of implying unlimited rights. Stop the follow-up sequence on a positive reply and hand off negotiation. The drafted manager contact route does not authorize sending a message.
