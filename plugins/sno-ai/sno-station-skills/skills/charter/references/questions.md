# Ask only for decisions that matter

Read the existing work and the settled decisions before asking. Do not ask for facts the files
answer or for permission the owner already gave.

Ask only when plausible answers would change the result and the choice belongs to the owner.
One question per message, at the end, after the answer to whatever they asked. Open with what
the thing is, which charter it concerns, and what happens for each answer, in plain words with
no internal names or codes. Do not bury a question inside a report. Give a recommendation and
say why.

Continue independent work while waiting. An unanswered required question is not an approval.
No coverage scan, minimum number of questions or formal checkpoint is needed: zero questions
is ordinary when the request is clear. Do not reopen a settled decision without a new fact.
