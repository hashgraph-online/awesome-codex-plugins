# Email template

Plain text that pastes cleanly into an email. Replace the parts in angle brackets. Leave out any section with nothing in it.

```
Subject: Social media update, <period, for example 22 to 28 September>

Hi <name>,

Here is how <account or brand> did on social media <this week / in September>.

Summary
- Posts published: <n> across <platforms>
- Views: <n>   Likes: <n>   Comments: <n>   Shares: <n>
- Followers: <account>: <n> (<+/-n>), <account>: <n> (<+/-n>)

Top posts
1. <platform>, <date>: "<first line>" <views/likes> <link>
2. ...
3. ...

What worked
<one or two sentences: what the top posts have in common>

Problems
<what failed and what was done about it, or "None.">

Coming up
<n> posts scheduled for <next period>, including <one or two highlights>.

Next steps
- <one concrete suggestion>
- <another, if useful>

<sign-off>
```

## Style

- Short sentences. No jargon.
- Numbers with thousands separators (12,400).
- "Not available" for a number the platform doesn't report.
- One suggestion the reader can act on is better than five.
