# Platform limits at a glance

`get_platform_rules` is the source of truth for every limit, including media counts, file sizes and video lengths. Call it when a post is close to a limit or uses a format you haven't used before. The text limits below are the ones PostEverywhere checks before publishing.

| Platform | Text limit | Media |
|---|---|---|
| X | 280 characters (25,000 on X Premium) | Optional |
| Threads | 500 characters | Optional |
| Bluesky | 300 characters | Optional |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 characters | Optional: images, a video or a document |
| Facebook | 63,206 characters | Optional |
| Instagram | 2,200 characters, up to 30 hashtags | Required: an image, a carousel, a Reel or a Story |
| TikTok | 4,000 characters | Required: a video or photos |
| YouTube | Title up to 100 characters, description up to 5,000 | Required: one video |
| Pinterest | 500 characters | Required: an image or video, and a board |
| Telegram | 4,096 characters | Optional |
| Discord | 2,000 characters | Optional |
| WordPress | A blog post with a title | Optional: the first image is the featured image |

## Writing for each platform

- **X, Threads, Bluesky:** short and direct. Put the point in the first line. One or two hashtags at most.
- **LinkedIn:** a first line that earns the "see more" click, short paragraphs, no hashtag walls.
- **Instagram and TikTok:** the caption supports the media. Hashtags at the end.
- **YouTube:** a clear title, and a description that says what the video is about in the first two lines.
- **Pinterest:** describe what the pin shows, in the words people search for.
