<motion id="one-surface">

<mood>Seamless, premium, calm. One continuous material. Nothing arrives, nothing leaves.</mood>

<dominant_idea>
Nothing appears or disappears — containers morph into their next state. The button becomes
the drawer, the card becomes the modal, the island expands into the panel. The page is one
surface changing shape.
</dominant_idea>

<signature_moves>
- Shared-element container morphs between every paired state.
- Island expansion: a compact element grows in place into its full form and returns.
- A single sliding highlight that travels between navigation items — one light, many stops.
- Progressive blur as spatial depth; layers recede rather than vanish.
- Numbers transition digit-by-digit, sliding in place.
</signature_moves>

<prohibitions>
- No entrance animations and no page-load choreography; the surface is simply there.
- Never more than one thing moving at once — a morph owns the whole beat.
- No particles, confetti, glitch, or texture motion; the material is smooth and singular.
- No hard cuts between states that could morph; a cut is a seam, and this surface has none.
- No motion for its own sake between unrelated elements — continuity only where identity
  genuinely persists.
</prohibitions>

<rhythm>Unhurried and certain. Morphs are long enough to track and short enough to never
wait for. Easing is smooth throughout — no bounce, no overshoot.</rhythm>

<best_for>SaaS products, fintech, dashboards, settings-heavy apps, premium consumer brands,
anywhere trust and polish outrank excitement.</best_for>

<degrade>With reduced motion: morphs become crossfades or instant swaps at the same
positions. Spatial continuity survives in the layout logic even when the tween is gone.</degrade>

<danger>A morph between elements that are not truly the same thing is a lie the visitor
feels. Map the identity graph first; only genuine continuities earn the transition.</danger>

</motion>
