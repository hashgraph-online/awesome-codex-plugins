Whisper is a speech-recognition model OpenAI released for free in 2022. It is not an app. Anyone can run it on their own machine.
Wispr Flow is a paid app from a different company. It runs in the cloud.
MacWhisper is a paid Mac app that runs Whisper locally on your own machine.
