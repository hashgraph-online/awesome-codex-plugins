# Rights for painted art

What fal's terms say about the pictures this skill makes on the person's own account, with the date the page was
read. Read the live page again before anything commercial is published, and update the date here.

- **fal** (https://fal.ai/terms, read 2026-10-02, "Last Updated: September 8, 2026"): the customer "owns and
  retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Customer Input" (the prompt, a frame, a reference). There
  is no matching clause that assigns the Output to the customer, and fal "does not represent, warrant, or
  covenant that any Output Content will be original, will not infringe rights of any third party". Third-party
  model outputs may not be used to train models that compete with them.
- **The models this skill uses** carry fal's "Commercial use" label (fal's FAQ: models marked "Research only" are
  non-commercial; never use one of those for a game).
- **FLUX [dev]** outputs through fal may be used commercially ("You may use Output for any purpose (including for
  commercial purposes)", the FLUX [dev] licence); its weights are non-commercial, so never run them locally for a
  commercial game.
- **Nano Banana** pictures carry an invisible SynthID watermark.
- **Copyright in purely AI-made pictures** may be thin: never claim more than the terms say. A cover from a real
  frame of the game (`frame`, `cover`) is the studio's own work.

The `models` skill's `references/RIGHTS.md` covers 3D models and the starter library.
