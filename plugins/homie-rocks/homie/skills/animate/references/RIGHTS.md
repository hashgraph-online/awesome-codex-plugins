# Rights for rigs and clips

What the sources' own pages say, with the date each was read. Read the live page again before anything commercial is
published, and update the date here.

## Free, in the starter library (CC0)

- **KayKit** (https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-character-animations, read 2026-10-03): "Creative Commons Zero v1.0
  Universal", "free for personal and commercial use, no attribution required"; 161 humanoid clips in the Character
  Animations pack (for its Rig_Medium and Rig_Large, "compatible with other humanoid characters via engine
  retargeting"). The adventurers and skeletons in the starter library carry 76 and 95 of those clips on their own rig.
  A request, not a term: "please don't resell unmodified copies or claim them as your own" (Homie never sells them).
- **Kenney** (each pack's License.txt; https://kenney.nl/support): CC0; the mini, blocky and graveyard characters and
  the cube pets carry their own clips.
- A CC0 clip retargeted onto another rig at build time stays CC0. The studio's own rig keeps its own licence.

## Reference only, never mirrored or put in a public repository

- **Quaternius** (https://quaternius.com/license.html, read 2026-10-03): "Quaternius Asset License (QAL) v1.0", last
  updated 8/28/2026: "No attribution is required", but you may not "extract, repackage, sublicense, sell, or otherwise
  redistribute the Assets (in original or modified form) as a standalone asset, asset pack, stock file, template, or
  similar product". Its Universal Animation Library is not in the starter library; a public game's repository may not
  hold it, and it is never in a shared part.
- **Mixamo** (Adobe): royalty-free in a game, but raw files may not be redistributed as standalone assets: never in a
  public repository; a private studio's shipped build only, and never in a shared part.

## Generated on the person's own account

- **Meshy through fal** (https://fal.ai/models/meshy/v7.1/image-to-3d, read 2026-10-03): labelled "Commercial use";
  a textured model with auto-rigging costs US$1.40 (US$0.80 base, US$0.40 textures, US$0.20 rigging; US$0.12 more for
  an animation preset). fal's terms (https://fal.ai/terms, last updated 2026-09-08, read 2026-10-03): the customer
  owns its input; no clause assigns the output; no training competing models on third-party outputs. Meshy's own free
  plan (outputs CC BY 4.0, Meshy owns them) never applies: calls through fal are paid calls.
- Copyright in purely AI-made files may be thin: RIGHTS.md says so and never claims more than the terms.

## Never offered (non-commercial in effect)

- Text-to-motion checkpoints trained on AMASS or HumanML3D (MDM, MoMask, T2M-GPT, MotionGPT): AMASS "prohibits the use
  of the Dataset to train methods… for commercial use of any kind"; SMPL model files are non-commercial.
- Video-to-motion tools that need SMPL (WHAM, 4D-Humans, MotionBERT) or say research only (GVHMR); RigAnything
  (non-commercial); the Bandai Namco motion dataset (CC BY-NC 4.0).
- HY-Motion (Tencent) is territory-limited ("does not apply in the European Union, United Kingdom and South Korea"):
  never a default; a later phase may offer it by region. NVIDIA Kimodo's SOMA weights are commercial-ready but need a
  large NVIDIA GPU on Linux: a later phase.
