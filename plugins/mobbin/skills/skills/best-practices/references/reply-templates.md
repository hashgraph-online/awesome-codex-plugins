# Reply templates

Fill in each `{…}` and keep the rest as written. A bullet is one sentence, and nothing follows the last bullet.

## Out of scope

> Sorry, the Mobbin MCP doesn't currently support {what they asked for, in their words}. {Your {thing} is still in Mobbin, but} from here I can only {what the Mobbin tools in this session do, in a few words}.
>
> What you can do instead:
> - **{What they get}.** {The step they take, if any, then what you'll do.}
> - **{What they get}.** {What you'll do.}

Keep the second sentence's opening only for something they have in Mobbin.

Example (unsupported action): "Switch my workspace"

> Sorry, the Mobbin MCP doesn't currently support switching your workspace. Your workspaces are still in Mobbin, but from here I can only search Mobbin's screens, flows and sections.
>
> What you can do instead:
> - **Find new references.** Tell me what you're working on, and I'll find screens or flows for you, with links leading back to Mobbin.

## Too broad

> Mobbin works best on one screen, flow or section at a time, so let's break {what they asked for} into smaller steps. Being specific helps Mobbin find closer matches.
>
> Where you can start:
> - **{What they get}.** "{A sample prompt naming one screen, flow or section.}"
> - **{What they get}.** "{Another.}"
> - **{What they get}.** "{Another.}"

For a clone, the opening is instead:

> Mobbin helps you design your own app by showing how real products solve problems. Rather than copying one, we can synthesize different products, and adapt the results to your work. Being specific helps Mobbin find closer matches.

Example (build an entire app): "Build me a social media app"

> Mobbin works best on one screen, flow or section at a time, so let's break a social media app into smaller steps. Being specific helps Mobbin find closer matches.
>
> Where you can start:
> - **Feed references.** "Show me iOS feeds that mix photo and text posts"
> - **A sign-up flow to follow.** "Compare phone number sign-up across three social apps"
> - **A better profile screen.** "Redesign my profile screen so the follow button stands out"

Example (clone another app): "Clone Airbnb"

> Mobbin helps you design your own app by showing how real products solve problems. Rather than copying one, we can synthesize different products, and adapt the results to your work. Being specific helps Mobbin find closer matches.
>
> Where you can start:
> - **Booking flow patterns.** "Compare how travel apps let guests pick dates and pay on mobile"
> - **Listing page references.** "Find listing pages that show price and reviews without scrolling"
> - **A stronger screen of your own.** "Redesign my listing page so the price is easier to find"
