# Introducing Mobbin

Answer an onboarding question about Mobbin. Use only the facts in this file. Don't add numbers, customers or features that aren't here.

## What to say

Copy the reply below word for word, with its bold headings, the (Browse), (Build) and (Research) tags and the **Pricing.** and **Limits.** labels. Don't reword, merge or reorder any of it, and don't rewrite it under `plain-writing`.

Fit the reply to the question by leaving parts out, never by changing them:

- "What does Mobbin do?" gets the summary and the three things.
- "What can you do?" or "How do I use this?" gets the three things and their prompts.
- A question about one thing ("can you compare apps?") gets that one, with its prompts.

Every reply keeps Pricing and Limits as two separate lines, then ends with the closing line, so nothing follows it.

> Mobbin is a reference library of real apps and sites. We can help you with your design tasks.
>
> **With the Mobbin MCP, you can:**
>
> 1. **(Browse) Find references.** Search for how real products handle one screen, flow or section.
>    - "Find social media feed references"
>    - "Show me paywalls with a free trial"
>    - "How do banking apps show a failed payment?"
> 2. **(Build) Redesign a screen you have.** Share a screenshot, code or a design file. Tell the agent which specific problems you'd like to fix, or see a couple variations backed by real apps. Your design system stays the same.
>    - "Redesign my settings page"
>    - "Show me three directions for this pricing section"
>    - "The checkout button gets lost on this screen. Fix it."
> 3. **(Research) Compare products.** See how several products handle one journey side by side, or settle one design decision by sampling what real apps do.
>    - "Compare onboarding across three meditation apps"
>    - "Compare Revolut, Monzo and Wise side by side for identity checks"
>    - "Modal or bottom sheet to confirm a delete?"
>
> **Pricing.** The Mobbin MCP requires a paid Mobbin plan. See [pricing plans](https://mobbin.com/pricing).
>
> **Limits.** The Mobbin MCP is in active development, with new tools and features planned. For now, it covers the three things above.

The `>` marks the reply's text. Leave the marks out of the reply.

**The closing line.** One line asking what they'd like to start with, such as "What would you like to start with?" When the request was about one of the three, offer instead to run one of its sample prompts on their product.

## After the reply

If they answer with a task, take it as a new request.
