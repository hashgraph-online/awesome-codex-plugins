# Featuring Nominations

The one official way to bring an app to the attention of Apple's editors. A featuring nomination is **not** an Apple Design Award entry — Apple does not say whether nominations feed into ADA selection — but it is the channel developers control.

Source, checked October 2026: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-featuring-nominations/nominate-your-app-for-featuring/

## Mechanics

- **Where:** App Store Connect → Apps → the app → sidebar **Featuring → Nominations** → "+" → **Create Nomination**
- **Who can submit:** Account Holder, Admin, App Manager, or Marketing roles
- **Types:**
  - **App Launch** — the launch or pre-order of a new app or game
  - **App Enhancements** — new features, functionality, or other significant updates
  - **New Content** — new content, offers, or events in the app or game
- **Lead time:** Apple's help page asks for "a minimum lead time of 3 weeks"; its featuring overview page says two weeks. Plan for at least **3 weeks**, and up to three months ahead for wider consideration
- **Supporting links:** up to **5 URLs** — documents, art assets, demo videos, and public TestFlight links
- **Helpful Details:** a field that explicitly invites "details about accessibility, inclusivity, level of priority of your nomination, or any unique aspects of your app or team" — use it
- **Other fields:** platforms, relevant countries or regions, localizations (pre-filled), related apps (up to 10 from the same account), and an approved In-App Event (iPhone and iPad only)
- **After submitting:** the nomination type and related apps cannot be changed

## Timing

- **Nominate each meaningful moment** — a launch, a significant update, new content. Featuring is not a one-shot opportunity
- **Ship publicly well before you want attention.** Real users find problems internal testing misses; a polished app with a few months of real-world use and reviews is a stronger candidate than one launched to coincide with WWDC
- **ADA season:** finalists have been announced in mid-to-late May and winners in early June, before WWDC. Treat the preceding months as the window in which the app must already be public and polished. These are patterns from past years, not published deadlines

## The Pitch

The description field is read by editors deciding in minutes. Write it like this:

1. **One sentence on what the app is and for whom** — in plain words, not a slogan
2. **The signature interaction** — the one thing it does better than anything else in its category
3. **Why now** — what is launching or changing
4. **Craft and inclusion** — accessibility, localization, platform-specific work (and put the detail in Helpful Details)
5. **Link to a short demo** that shows the signature interaction in the first seconds

Avoid superlatives without evidence, feature lists, and claims the product cannot back up.

## Beyond App Store Connect

- **Meet with Apple** offers developer sessions and one-to-one consultations (design, technology, App Store), mostly online, for Apple Developer Program members — useful for feedback on design and platform fit. Check current availability at https://developer.apple.com/events/
- **Show the work publicly** — a clear product website and write-ups of interesting design decisions make the app easier to discover and evaluate

None of these is a known input to selection; each makes a strong app easier to find.
