# Apple's Criteria

Quoted from Apple's own pages, checked in October 2026. Confirm against the live pages before relying on exact wording:

- Featuring: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/getting-featured/
- Apple Design Awards: https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/

## App Store Featuring Considerations

Apple states there is no checklist for getting featured, but its editors consider:

| Consideration | Apple's description |
|---|---|
| User experience | "Cohesive, efficient, and valuable functionality that's helpful and easy to use." |
| UI design | "Great usability, appeal, and overall quality, such as beautiful visuals or intuitive gestures and controls." |
| Innovation | "New technologies that solve a unique problem and provide an enhanced user experience." |
| Uniqueness | "A fresh approach to a familiar category that stands out from the crowd or defines a new genre." |
| Accessibility | "A great experience for a broad range of users with well integrated features." |
| Localization | "High-quality support for multiple languages with culturally relevant and appropriate content." |
| App Store product page | "Compelling screenshots, app previews, and descriptions, as well as positive ratings and reviews." |

**Games** are also considered on gameplay design, art and animation, controls, story and characters (if applicable), replayability (if applicable), sound design and music, technical performance, and overall value.

## Apple Design Award Categories

Six categories; each recognises one app and one game. In 2026 Apple named 36 finalists (three apps and three games per category) and 12 winners.

| Category | Apple's description |
|---|---|
| Delight and Fun | "memorable, engaging, and satisfying experiences enhanced by Apple technologies" |
| Inclusivity | "a great experience for all by reflecting a variety of backgrounds, abilities, and languages" |
| Innovation | "a state‑of‑the‑art experience through a novel use of Apple technologies that sets them apart in their genre" |
| Interaction | "intuitive interfaces and effortless controls that are perfectly tailored to their platform" |
| Social Impact | "improve lives in a meaningful way and shine a light on crucial issues" |
| Visuals and Graphics | "stunning imagery, skillfully drawn interfaces, and high-quality animations with a distinctive and cohesive theme" |

## How Selection Works (What Is Known)

- **No public entry process.** Apple's pages describe no application form or rubric; coverage describes Apple choosing finalists from apps already on its platforms
- **Finalists before winners.** Apple has announced finalists ahead of the winners since 2021. In 2026: finalists on 18 May, winners on 2 June, WWDC from 8 June
- **Teams of every size.** Finalists mix independent developers with large studios; solo developers have won (Art of Fauna, Song of Bloom)
- **Platforms.** Most 2026 winners were on iPhone; four of the twelve were on Apple Vision Pro or Mac
- **Technology in service of the experience.** Apple's 2025 announcement highlighted SwiftUI, VoiceOver and Dynamic Type, haptics, Apple Pencil, and Apple Vision Pro in winners — each used for what the app does, not as a feature list

## Using the Criteria

- **Pick one category** and make the app the best in its genre at that sentence. Read the description literally: Interaction asks for "perfectly tailored to their platform", so a stretched iPhone layout on iPad works against it
- **Accessibility and localization count twice** — two of the seven featuring considerations, and the core of Inclusivity
- **Do not add frameworks for their own sake.** Innovation rewards a novel use that "sets them apart in their genre"; a widget nobody needs does not
- **Social Impact needs evidence** of benefit beyond the app's users — hard to claim without it
