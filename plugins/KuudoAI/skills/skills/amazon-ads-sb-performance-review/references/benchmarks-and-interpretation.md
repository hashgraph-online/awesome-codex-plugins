# Benchmarks and interpretation (SB)

These are **orientation ranges**, not pass/fail rules. Always label them as benchmarks and let the user override with account targets.

## CTR

- Common narrative range cited for SB: **~0.30%–0.80%** CTR (percentage of impressions). Your export may express CTR as a decimal or percent — **normalize before comparing**.
- **Low CTR flag** (default storytelling): below **0.20%** (as % of impressions) may warrant creative or targeting review **if** impressions are material (avoid flagging sparse rows).

## ACoS / ROAS

- Interpret relative to the user’s **target ACoS** or **target ROAS** and margin. Without targets, compare to portfolio median and best peers in the same file.

## New-to-brand (NTB)

- SB reports sometimes include NTB orders, NTB sales, or NTB percentages. If present:
  - Report **NTB order share** and **NTB sales share** with the same attribution definitions as total orders/sales.
  - Explain that high NTB can **contextualize** higher ACoS for acquisition-heavy goals; it does not remove the need for a ceiling tied to economics.

## Impression share

- If the report includes impression share or lost impression share fields, use them for **delivery constraint** narrative (budget vs bids vs relevance). Map column names from the actual file — names vary.

## Recommendations — pattern library

Use only when supported by the table:

- **High spend + high ACoS + low NTB**: tighten targeting, refresh creative, reduce bids or pause worst segments.
- **High CTR + low CVR** (if conversion columns exist): landing page / Store, offer, or query relevance — label as hypothesis.
- **Strong efficiency + capped delivery** (impression share metrics if present): consider budget or bid headroom tests.

Always state **what column** supported each conclusion.
