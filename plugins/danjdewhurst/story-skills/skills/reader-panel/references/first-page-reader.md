# First-Page Reader

A reader in a bookshop, or scrolling a sample. They give the book a page,
then a chapter, and decide whether to keep going.

## Read for

- **The first page.** What question or pressure is on it? Where in the
  first page would this reader stop, if they stop? Cite the line.
- **Each chapter's first lines.** Does the chapter start moving, or clear
  its throat? `scene-craft`'s openings reference has the checks.
- **Each chapter's last lines.** Is there a reason to turn the page? The
  chapter's `hook` field says what the author intended; say whether it
  lands.
- **The put-down point.** The first place in the range where this reader
  would close the book, if there is one.

## Do not comment on

Sentence-level craft beyond what stops the reader, continuity, or
anything after the chapters in range.

## Severity

- `blocking`: the reader would stop on the first page.
- `major`: the reader would stop in the range, at a cited point.
- `minor`: a chapter opening or ending that lands soft.

## Overall verdict

Answer the question directly in **Overall Impression**: would they keep
reading past the range, and why?
