# Contract Red Flags

Use this list to read a publishing, audio, translation, or film contract
with the author and find the clauses worth questioning. **This is not
legal advice.** Contract law differs by country, and a clause that looks
standard can hide a problem. Before signing, the author should have the
contract reviewed by their agent, a publishing lawyer, or an author
organization's contract-vetting service (for example the Authors Guild in
the US or the Society of Authors in the UK, for members). Writer Beware
and the Alliance of Independent Authors track predatory publishers and
services.

## How To Use

1. Read the whole contract, including schedules and anything
   "incorporated by reference".
2. For each red flag below that matches a clause, record the clause
   number, the plain-language effect, and the question to ask.
3. Hand the list to the author and their adviser. Do not tell the author
   to sign or not sign.

## Rights Granted

- [ ] **Rights grab:** all rights, in all formats, in all languages,
      worldwide, "now known or hereafter devised", for the full term of
      copyright, when the publisher only publishes one or two formats.
      Ask to limit the grant to formats and territories they will exploit.
- [ ] **Subsidiary rights** (audio, translation, film, merchandise) taken
      with no obligation to exploit them and no reversion if unused.
- [ ] **Copyright transferred** to the publisher, or the book treated as
      work for hire, rather than a license.
- [ ] **Series, characters, or world** claimed, so the author cannot write
      further books in that world elsewhere.

## Term, Reversion, And Out Of Print

- [ ] **No out-of-print clause,** or one triggered only when the book is
      unavailable in every format. Print-on-demand and ebooks never go
      out of print; ask for a sales or earnings threshold over a set
      period.
- [ ] **No reversion process:** no written request mechanism, cure
      period, or automatic reversion.
- [ ] **Term for the life of copyright** in a small-press or service
      contract, where a fixed term (for example 3-7 years, renewable) is
      common.

## Money

- [ ] **Payments from the author** to the publisher: setup, editing,
      marketing, or buy-back requirements. A traditional publisher pays
      the author. This is the mark of a vanity press.
- [ ] **Royalties on "net" with net undefined,** or net after deductions
      the publisher controls.
- [ ] **Low or unclear ebook and audio rates** compared with the
      publisher's own peers.
- [ ] **Joint accounting (cross-collateralization):** earnings of one book
      pay down another book's unearned advance.
- [ ] **Unlimited reserve against returns,** or reserves held with no time
      limit.
- [ ] **Infrequent or no statements,** no deadline for payment, or no
      right to audit.
- [ ] **Deep-discount or high-discount sales** paying a much lower rate,
      with no cap on how much of the sales can fall in that category.

## Control

- [ ] **Non-compete clause** that stops the author publishing anything,
      under any name, in any genre, for a long or undefined period.
- [ ] **Option clause** on the next book (or next several) with no time
      limit to respond, on the same terms, or covering all future work.
- [ ] **No consultation** on cover, title, or edits, or the publisher may
      change the text without approval.
- [ ] **Moral rights waived** where the law allows assertion.
- [ ] **Assignment:** the publisher may sell the contract to anyone
      without notice.

## Liability And Termination

- [ ] **Unlimited indemnity:** the author covers every claim, including
      claims arising from the publisher's changes.
- [ ] **No termination right** if the publisher fails to publish by a
      date, fails to pay, or goes bankrupt.
- [ ] **Publication date undefined:** no obligation to publish within a
      set time after delivery.
- [ ] **Agency clause** tying the author to an agent beyond the book the
      agent sold.

## Film, TV, And Audio Specifics

- [ ] **Option term and extensions** far longer than the option payment
      justifies, or extensions without further payment.
- [ ] **Purchase price** not stated, or only as a percentage of a budget
      that may never be set.
- [ ] **Reserved rights** (sequels, prequels, stage, print) not reserved to
      the author.
- [ ] **Audio:** exclusive distribution terms, their length, and whether
      the author can later move the audiobook.

## After Review

Record the outcome in `publishing/rights.md`: rights granted, term,
territory, and reversion terms, plus the contract date and where the
signed copy is kept.
