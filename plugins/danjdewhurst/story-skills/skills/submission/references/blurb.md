# Blurb And Retailer Description

The blurb sells the book to a reader, not an agent. It goes on the back
cover, the jacket flap, or the retailer page. Save drafts to
`submission/blurb.md`.

## Template

```markdown
---
type: blurb
updated: YYYY-MM-DD
---

# Blurb: {Title}

## Tagline

{One line, under 15 words.}

## Back Cover

{150-200 words.}

## Retailer Description

{Back-cover copy, optionally followed by a comp line. Plain paragraphs;
bold only the tagline if the platform allows formatting.}
```

## Length

- Tagline: under 15 words.
- Back cover: 150-200 words. Jacket flaps can run to 250.
- Retailer description: 150-250 words. Only the first two lines show before
  the "read more" cut, so they must carry the hook.

## Formula

1. **Tagline.** The central tension or promise in one line.
2. **Situation.** The protagonist and their world in one or two sentences.
3. **Disruption.** The inciting incident.
4. **Escalation.** What stands in the way and what it costs.
5. **Stakes.** End on the choice or the danger, never the resolution.

## Genre notes

- **Romance:** both leads, the reason they cannot be together, and the
  trope and heat-level signals readers look for. The happy ending is
  assumed; do not spell it out.
- **Mystery and thriller:** the crime or threat, the investigator, and the
  clock. Never name the culprit.
- **Fantasy and science fiction:** one or two world terms at most; lead
  with character, not worldbuilding.
- **Horror:** atmosphere and escalating wrongness; end on dread.
- **Literary:** voice and situation over plot mechanics; shorter copy is
  common.

## Rules

- Present tense, third person, even for a first-person book.
- No spoilers past roughly the end of the first act, and never the ending.
- Match the book's tone; check `style-sheet.md` for voice and spellings.
- Add a comp line ("For fans of ...") or review quotes only when the author
  supplies or confirms them.
