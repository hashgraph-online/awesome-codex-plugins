# Where to find references for characters, props and voices

Tested from an agent's shell on 2026-11-03 (curl, uvx, python3). "Reference only" means: look at it, learn from it,
describe it, remix the idea. Don't copy it, trace it, upload it as the image to edit, or ship it. Feed a model only
CC0, public-domain, licensed or your own images. Take features from several sources and mix them; never copy one
real person's face or one artist's design.

**Pinterest and rights.** Pins are all rights reserved and Pinterest's terms forbid automated collection. Default:
the agent writes the search links (patterns below) and the person browses. `gallery-dl` works technically (tested)
and returns board names you can use to filter AI pins; use it only where you have permission, and never feed pins
to a model.

### 1.2 Top 10 for agents (keyless, scriptable, fine to use as reference)

1. **Pinterest via gallery-dl.** Widest range of real-world faces, wardrobe and props. Characters: job + place + decade + "photograph". Props: "antique/vintage" + object. Voices: no.
2. **The Met (v1.1).** CC0, dated and labelled. Characters: dress, coat, uniform and armour by date range (dept 8 / 4). Props: tools, lanterns, instruments, jewellery.
3. **Wikimedia Commons.** License on every file. Characters: occupational portraits, regional dress, historical photos. Props: object photos. Voices: some CC audio (pronunciation files).
4. **Library of Congress FSA/OWI.** Public-domain documentary photos of real working people: faces, hands, workwear, posture. Props: tools of each trade.
5. **Europeana** (demo key). European folk costume, uniforms, studio portraits, with a rights filter. Props: crafts and tools.
6. **Poly Haven.** CC0 glTF props, textures and HDRIs that drop straight into three.js or Blender.
7. **Sketchfab search.** 3D props and characters with a license filter (`license=cc0`) and keyless thumbnails. A free token lets you download.
8. **ambientCG + physicallybased.info.** CC0 PBR materials and measured values for worn props and believable skin and metal.
9. **ArtStation** (a person browses it). Professional turnarounds, expression sheets and costume callouts, for learning layout and design language only.
10. **Speech Accent Archive.** 2,999 real accents, each with IPA and a speaker bio. Write a voice brief from it (CC BY-NC-SA, reference only). Then pick a stock TTS voice by description.

Worth knowing about: AI Pose Gallery (camera angles for poses), Posemaniacs (pose mannequins, hands), Are.na channels (taste-filtered boards), Vogue Runway (current fashion), Fashion History Timeline (decade accuracy), Character Design References (animation model sheets), IDEA (dialects; listen only).

### 1.3 Pinterest tips (tested)

- **Decade + place + job + "photograph" returns real photos.** "portuguese fisherman 1960s photograph" gave 6/6 real documentary photos. "fisherman portrait weathered" gave about 3/6 AI-looking images, one of them on a board named "AI vs Real Art".
- **For props, add "antique" or "vintage" and the material.** "antique brass ship lantern prop" gave 6/6 real product and auction photos.
- **For costumes, name a film or a designer.** Try "<film> costume", "screen-worn <film>", "<designer> costume sketch" or "wardrobe continuity photo". "film costume fitting photo costume designer" returned workroom photos (sewing tables), not costumes.
- **Words that pull in AI art:** "hyper realistic", "cinematic", "8k", "portrait of a", "concept", "masterpiece", "weathered" on its own. Pinterest search does not reliably support a "-AI" exclusion, so filter after download instead.
- **Use `--write-metadata`, then filter.** Drop pins whose `board.name`, `description` or `title` mention AI, midjourney, generated or "no camera", or that carry `gen_ai_topics`. `pin_join.visual_annotation` gives keywords you can reuse, such as "Old Maritime Photography" and "Vintage Fisherman Photography".
- **Better seeds:** documentary photographer names (Dorothea Lange, August Sander, Vivian Maier, Sebastião Salgado), "street portrait", "documentary", "archive", "museum", "eBay listing", "auction lot", "flat lay".
- **Boards beat search.** Once a pin looks right, download its board with `uvx gallery-dl "https://www.pinterest.com/<user>/<board>/"`. Costume designers' and prop masters' boards are curated by people who know the material.
