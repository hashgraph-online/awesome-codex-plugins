# maps-and-data-motion demo: route, chart, counter, visualizer

The preview video for the `maps-and-data-motion` skill: a 16 s HyperFrames film (1920x1080, 30 fps, with sound) that
shows four of the skill's templates back to back, cut on the downbeats of a synthesized 120 BPM track.

| Time (s) | Scene file | Preset | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0 – 4.5 | `compositions/route.html` | `vector-route-map.night-transit` | Natural Earth vector map of Europe; a Lisbon → Helsinki great-circle route draws itself with a plane riding the tip, then an ant path crawls on the hold |
| 4.5 – 8.5 | `compositions/chart.html` | `self-drawing-chart.bars` | Eight bars rise from a zero baseline in a stagger, values count with them, then all but one go grey and the key bar turns accent with a callout |
| 8.5 – 12.5 | `compositions/stat.html` | `count-up-stat.center-hero` | 48,200 counts up with CountUp smart easing, lands with a 5 % pulse and an underline, holds 1.75 s |
| 12.5 – 16.0 | `compositions/ribbons.html` | `audio-visualizer.silk-ribbons` | Five silk ribbons driven by the soundtrack's bands (bass bottom, treble top) plus a beat pulse |

A small label in the bottom-left corner names the preset (JetBrains Mono, 17 px = 1.6 % of frame height, 60 %
opacity).

## Values used

From `assets/presets/*.json` and each reference's "Timing and numbers":

- **Route (night-transit):** water #0B1220, land #16213A, borders #26355A 1 px, route #4FD1C5 5 px with a 12 px glow
  at 30 % and a 2 px-wider casing in the water colour, ant dash 14/10 crawling 40 px/s on the hold, city labels
  Inter 600 28 px (2.6 %) on a dark plate, title 60 px (5.6 %), traveller 44 px (4.1 %) rotated along the line, map in
  with scale 1.03 → 1 on cubic-bezier(0.05,0.7,0.1,1), pins back.out(1.6), draw on cubic-bezier(0.37,0,0.63,1),
  arrive ring 1 → 1.8 over 0.6 s power2.out. The stroke and the plane share one progress value. Projection: Lambert
  azimuthal equal-area centred on Europe (the reference's "one country / region" row); the route is a d3
  great-circle interpolation, which is close to straight in this projection.
- **Bars:** bar width 62 % of the slot, 4 px top radius, grow 0.7 s on cubic-bezier(0.33,1,0.68,1), stagger 0.07 s,
  values in Inter 600 tabular figures fading in over the last 40 % of each grow, grey #C9CED6, accent #2F6BFF,
  highlight 0.6 s power2.inOut, gridlines #E3E6EA 1 px, zero baseline, unit on the top tick ("100k") and in the
  subtitle, no legend, plot area 80 % of the width.
- **Count-up:** number 238 px (22 % of frame height) Inter 800 tabular figures, label 35 px (3.2 %) Inter 500,
  enter 0.4 s (rise 24 px, scale 0.96 → 1), 5-digit count over 1.7 s with smart easing (linear to N − 333 over 55 %
  of the time, easeOutExpo for the rest), landing pulse 1.05, final width reserved by a hidden sizer so nothing
  shifts.
- **Silk ribbons:** background #0B0B12 → #1B1030, ribbons #FF5EA8 #8A5CFF #4CC9F0 #F9C74F #FFFFFF from bass (bottom)
  to treble (top), main strand 2–4 px with a 16 px glow, additive ("lighter") blend, amplitude 4–14 % of frame
  height, phase 0.15–0.4 cycles/s, beat pulse 9 % decaying with tau 0.12 s, title reacts 2 % (limit 4 %).
- **Analysis** (`tools/analyze-audio.ts`): mono 44.1 kHz, FFT 4096 with a Hann window centred on each frame, 32 log
  bands 30 Hz–16 kHz, dB clamped −100…−30, per-band normalisation to the track's 98th percentile, attack 1 frame,
  release tau 0.45 s. The per-frame values are written into `compositions/ribbons.html`, so the render never fetches
  or analyses audio at runtime. Beat times are the track's known kick grid (every 0.5 s), which is what a beat tracker
  returns for this synthesized track.

## Deviations

- **Shorter beats.** Each template runs shorter than its preset default so four fit in 16 s: route 4.5 s instead of
  8 s (draw 2.2 s instead of 4.8 s, about 25 % of the frame width per second), chart 4.0 s instead of 6 s (highlight
  at 2.0 s instead of 3.2 s, hold 1.4 s), count-up 4.0 s as specified, ribbons 3.5 s instead of 20 s.
- **Bar colour before the highlight.** The preset gives grey and accent only for the highlight state; the bars grow
  in a neutral slate (#7A8494) so the "others go grey, one turns accent" change is visible.
- **Count-up background** is a plain blue gradient (no footage), so no scrim is used.

## Data, sound and credits

- **Map:** Natural Earth 1:50m admin-0 countries and populated places (public domain, no credit required). City
  coordinates come from `ne_50m_populated_places_simple`; the 3,360 km distance is the great-circle distance between
  them. `assets/map.svg` and `assets/route.json` are generated by `tools/build-map.ts`.
- **Chart and counter numbers are invented sample data** for an invented airline ("Northwind Air"); the chart says
  so on screen ("illustrative sample data").
- **Soundtrack:** `assets/beat.m4a`, synthesized with ffmpeg by `tools/make-beat.sh` (sine kick and bass, chord pad,
  arpeggio, noise hats and claps). No samples, no third-party music.
- Fonts: Inter and JetBrains Mono (OFL), bundled with HyperFrames.

## Run it

```bash
# from this folder; HyperFrames needs Node 22 and ffmpeg
npx --yes hyperframes@0.8.206 check
npx --yes hyperframes@0.8.206 preview
npx --yes hyperframes@0.8.206 render --fps 30 --crf 20 --output maps-and-data-motion.mp4

# rebuild the inputs (optional)
(cd tools && bun install)
bun tools/build-map.ts path/to/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson   # from github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector
bash tools/make-beat.sh
bun tools/analyze-audio.ts
```

`tools/build-map.ts` writes `assets/route.json`; if you change the cities, copy its route `d`, the two city points
and the distance into `compositions/route.html`.
