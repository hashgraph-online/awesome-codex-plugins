## Articles and guides

- **[1] EBU R 095 — Safe areas for 16:9 television production** — European Broadcasting Union technical recommendation. https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/r095  
  Took: Action-safe 3.5% and graphics-safe 5% per edge.  
  Used by: name-lower-third, place-lower-third, show-open-title, speaker-plates (+ basics)
- **[2] UtilityLab — How to calculate title-safe and action-safe margins for video** — Developer guide (dev.to) with pixel tables. https://dev.to/utilitylab/how-to-calculate-title-safe-and-action-safe-margins-for-video-1ifj  
  Took: Legacy 90/80% safe areas in px for 1080p and 1080x1920; YouTube bottom 8%; 864x1200 dual-format zone.  
  Used by: title-craft-basics only
- **[3] HopperHQ — Instagram Reel size: dimensions, aspect ratio and safe zones** — Social scheduling company blog relaying Meta's percentage safe zones. https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/

- **[4] Netflix — English (USA) Timed Text Style Guide** — Netflix Partner Help, subtitle delivery spec. https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/217350977-English-USA-Timed-Text-Style-Guide
