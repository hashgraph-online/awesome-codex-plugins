## B4 social — Why plane windows are round
40 s, 9:16, TikTok/Shorts explainer, faceless (AI visuals + voiceover). Facts (use only these): in 1954 two de
Havilland Comet jetliners broke up in flight (BOAC Flight 781 in January, South African Airways Flight 201 in April).
Investigators found metal fatigue: cracks grew from the corners of cut-outs in the cabin skin, where stress
concentrated. Rounded shapes spread stress more evenly; airliner windows today have rounded corners.
