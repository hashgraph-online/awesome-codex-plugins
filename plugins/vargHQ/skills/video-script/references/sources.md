## Story and script craft

| Who | Why listen | What this skill uses | Source |
|---|---|---|---|

| Andrew Stanton | Pixar (Finding Nemo, WALL-E) | the first line promises it is worth your time; give 2 + 2, not 4; make me care | [TED 2012](https://www.ted.com/talks/andrew_stanton_the_clues_to_a_great_story/transcript?language=en) |
| Emma Coats | Pixar story artist; her 2011 tweets became "Pixar's 22 rules" (never official) | get the ending working first (#7); throw away the first ideas (#12); one-sentence essence (#22) | [compilation](https://www.pixartouchbook.com/blog/2011/5/15/pixar-story-rules-one-version.html) |
| Kenn Adams | improv teacher who invented the Story Spine (Pixar rule #4) | Once upon a time / Every day / But one day / Because of that / Until finally | [NPR](https://www.npr.org/transcripts/nx-s1-5750619) |
| Kurt Vonnegut | novelist; 8 rules from Bagombo Snuff Box | start as close to the end as possible; every sentence reveals character or advances the action | [The Marginalian](https://www.themarginalian.org/2012/04/03/kurt-vonnegut-on-writing-stories/) |
| Billy Wilder | Some Like It Hot, Double Indemnity | voice-over adds to what they are seeing; never describes it | [Quote Investigator](https://quoteinvestigator.com/2015/05/04/two-plus/) |
| Alfred Hitchcock | suspense director (Hitchcock/Truffaut) | show the bomb under the table: suspense comes from what the viewer knows | [David Bordwell](https://www.davidbordwell.net/blog/2013/11/29/hitchcock-lessing-and-the-bomb-under-the-table/) |
| NPR (Kasell, Rosenbaum, Scott Simon) | NPR's own production guide, 1992 | one idea per sentence; read it aloud; name the three points they must keep | [Sound Reporting](https://worldradiohistory.com/Bookshelf/News/Sound-Reporting-NPR-Rosenbaum-Dinges-1992.pdf) |

## Pace and reading speed

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| natural narration about 150 words a minute | [NCVS](http://web.archive.org/web/20160103120632/http://www.ncvs.org:80/ncvs/tutorials/voiceprod/tutorial/quality.html) |
| audiobooks 140–180 wpm; radio news 170 (dense) to 190 (light) | [Brysbaert 2019](https://www.gwern.net/doc/psychology/linguistics/2019-brysbaert.pdf) |

## AI agents and improving the skill

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| without direction the model "samples from this high-probability center"; name defaults and alternatives | [Anthropic, frontend design skill](https://www.claude.com/blog/improving-frontend-design-through-skills) |
| evaluations before documentation; Claude A edits, Claude B tests; SKILL.md under 500 lines | [Anthropic, skill best practices](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/agents-and-tools/agent-skills/best-practices) |
| LLMs swap specific facts for generic praise; the tells | [Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) |
| models repeat the same plot elements on the same prompt | [Xu et al., PNAS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00273) |
| asking for several candidates with probabilities raises diversity 1.6–2.1x | [Verbalized Sampling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01171) |
| AI quotes sound formal and alike; default names | [Russell, Karpinska, Iyyer, ACL 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.15654) |
| stock phrases in AI fiction ("voice barely above a whisper") | [Antislop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15061); [Chakrabarty et al., CHI 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14509) |
| specific feedback helps; self-correction without outside signal can hurt | [Self-Refine](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17651); [Huang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01798) |
| judge pairwise in both orders, with another model family | [MT-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685); [Panickssery et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13076) |
