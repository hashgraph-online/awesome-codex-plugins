# Social shorts and YouTube intros

For organic TikTok, Reels and Shorts, and the first 30 seconds of a YouTube video. The viewer did not ask to see it,
so the first 3 seconds decide; YouTube's "Intro" metric is the share still watching at 30 s
([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415)).

## Rules

1. **The opening pays off the packaging.** The first shot and line show what the title, thumbnail or caption promised
   ([MrBeast production doc](https://prog.sijanec.eu/dir/How-To-Succeed-At-MrBeast-Production.pdf);
   [Paddy Galloway](https://x.com/PaddyG96/status/1499762666012913668)). Many people never see the thumbnail on
   autoplay, so the first seconds must convince them visually.
2. **The hook is a picture that reads on mute in 3 s or less:** centered, three objects or fewer, strong enough to be
   the thumbnail; 3–5 words on screen, the motion and the voice say the same thing
   ([Jenny Hoyos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Dy1UDCvmkw); [Kallaway](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LmXpbP7dD48)).
3. **Foreshadow the ending right after the hook.** Hook plus foreshadow takes 3 s or less: "...and at the end, [payoff]"
   ([Jenny Hoyos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=As7abwNhG7Y)). Then a story line, never "let's get started".
4. **First value by about 4 s.** No montage, title sequence or subscribe ask at the start (Kallaway; Paddy).
5. **Rehook.** Shorts: about 20–25 s in, at most twice in 60–90 s. YouTube: first at about 2–3 min. Never signal the
   end in the back half (Kallaway; MrBeast doc).
6. **Fifth-grade words, staccato only in the hook, active voice** (Hoyos; Kallaway; MrBeast doc).
7. **End right after the payoff.** Hoyos cut one second off an ending and retention went from 83% to 88%. A twist ending
   invites a rewatch.
8. **One version per platform.** Shorts can be slower and more story (Hoyos's sweet spot is about 34 s); TikTok is
   denser and sound-on; Reels are very visual, with key text inside the safe zone. MrBeast in 2023: "Focus less on
   screaming/moving fast and more on story!" ([X](https://x.com/MrBeast/status/1713586730257764713)).
9. **Make a series, not one-offs.** One recurring character, a series name and "part 2" turn views into follows; 11M
   views with a new cast every time brought few follows ([@laurgrowth](https://x.com/laurgrowth/status/2115901434130837516)).

Pace: social voice-over often runs 170–190 words a minute; set `wpm:` to the real voice.

## Hook formats

| Format | Template |
|---|---|
| Lean-in, but, snapback (Kallaway) | "[Topic] is [what you believe]. But [the obvious part] is actually the least of it. Because [the real thing]." |
| Benefit first (Kallaway) | "If you want [benefit], you need [thing]." |
| Don't Y, do Z (Kallaway) | "If you want [X], don't [common advice]. Do [Z]." |
| Hook + foreshadow (Hoyos) | "[What I'm doing], and at the end [payoff]." in 3 s or less |
| Counted promise (Hoyos, Colin & Samir) | "Here are three reasons [X]." The viewer knows it ends on three. |
| Reaction first (Hoyos) | open on the reaction; show what caused it at the end |
| Middle of the story (Colin & Samir) | "In [date], [subject] [did X]. But there was a problem." |
| Thumbnail as first frame (Paddy Galloway) | recreate the thumbnail, then the stakes and the reward |
| Extreme verb or stair-step (MrBeast) | "I survived [X]"; "$1 vs $10 vs ... vs the world record" |
| Suspense, surprise, emotion (TikTok Creative Codes) | tease an outcome, break an expectation, or open on a feeling |

## Structures

- **Hoyos short (about 34 s).** Write in this order: idea → hook → last line → foreshadow → middle. Then: hook,
  foreshadow, transition line, but/therefore beats (the voice carries the action, the picture the context), one peak in
  the middle, payoff with a twist, cut.
- **Kallaway speed to value (60–90 s).** Hook (0–3 s) → body 1 → rehook at about 20–25 s → body 2 → outro that sums up
  or points to the next video.
- **Hook, retain, reward (Hormozi).** Get noticed; keep them with one method (a list, steps or a story); deliver what
  they came for at the right moment, not early, not never, not a different promise.
- **MrBeast long-form minute map.** 0:00–1:00 deliver the title; 1:00–3:00 move fast through progress; about 3:00 a
  re-engagement moment; 3:00–6:00 the most exciting simple content; about 6:00 a second re-engagement; explanations in
  the back half; end abruptly after the payoff.
- **YouTube intro (Colin & Samir).** Confirm this is the video they clicked; open a loop that is not in the title; set
  the tone and pace; show why only you could make it.

## Example: steak on a car engine, YouTube Short, 34 s (fictional experiment)

```
format: social
length: 34s
ratio: 9:16
wpm: 180
viewer: people who like odd cooking experiments
promise: can a car engine cook a steak, and will my mechanic eat it
strategy: question-ladder
cta: none
```

| # | time | spoken | on screen | visual | sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00-0:03 | "Steak on my engine. My mechanic eats it." | STEAK ON AN ENGINE | a foil-wrapped steak set on an engine block, hood up | hood creak |
| 2 | 0:03-0:08 | "Foil, salt, wrap it three times, tuck it by the manifold." | | hands triple-wrap the steak and wedge it in | foil crinkle |
| 3 | 0:08-0:14 | "Ten minutes of driving. I pull over. Still raw." | 10 MIN · RAW | roadside, hood up, the foil opens on grey-red meat | traffic whoosh |
| 4 | 0:14-0:20 | "So, the highway. Hotter engine, hotter steak, right?" | | speedometer climbs, highway lines blur | engine roar |
| 5 | 0:20-0:25 | "Thirty minutes. Smoke from the hood." | 30 MIN | a thin smoke line from the hood seam; hazards on | hazard clicks |
| 6 | 0:25-0:30 | "Medium rare. Honestly perfect." | MEDIUM RARE | steak sliced on the bumper, pink center | knife on board |
| 7 | 0:30-0:34 | MECHANIC: "Tastes like a radiator." [beat] "Can I have another?" | | the mechanic chews, frowns, holds out his hand | cut to black |

Why it works: hook and foreshadow in 3 s; each beat turns (raw, BUT smoke, BUT perfect); one peak; the payoff is a
twist and the video ends on it.
