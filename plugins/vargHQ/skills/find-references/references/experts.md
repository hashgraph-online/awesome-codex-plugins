# Experts: who to learn from

The best experts are on forums and in interviews more than on X. X works for AI filmmakers and lighting-minded
illustrators, and works poorly for cinematographers, type designers and music supervisors.
[@WanderingDP](https://x.com/WanderingDP) has 68 tweets, [@ThomasGolubic](https://x.com/ThomasGolubic) 6, and Fonts In
Use's bio reads "Moved to Mastodon". So read their sites. Illustrators in Japanese and Korean produce the most-saved,
least-promotional film and light reference posts.

Learn the method, then search. Never copy a person's work, and never name a living maker in a prompt (`never.md`).

## How to tell real work from engagement bait

Rank people by shipped work and by how specific their failure reports are. A maker saying Seedance 2.5 ["doesn't live up
to the hype"](https://x.com/henrydaubrez/status/2083561991273173486), with what broke, is a stronger signal than any
list. Bookmark count alone misleads here.

Before you use a post as evidence, compute bookmarks ÷ the author's followers. Require a ratio under about 1, or a
visible non-promotional author. `our proposal`, from about a dozen posts read on 2026-11-03: promotional lists sat above
2, and posts we read as clean sat from 0.06 to 0.62. The ratio is a filter, not proof. A film fan with no product
reached 4.61 with 2,178 reposts and reads as real, and a comment-gated post sat at 0.46. So read the post too.

Discount a post when you see any of these:

- The author's own product is listed first, or a "Seedance on @platform" tag or paid label sits on the post.
- It opens with a shock line and ends with "comment WORD" to get the link.
- Replies roughly equal likes (887 and 887 on one post), or reposts equal likes (133 and 132).
- The same text appears under other accounts, or one template ends in "read the article below" across many accounts.
- It is only an X Article link. The search tool does not return Article bodies, so you cannot read it. Do not cite it.

Bookmarks reward the catchy rule. Low numbers can carry the most signal: "Any asset you pass as a reference, even if
you're not explicitly prompting for it, will still influence the output" has 3 bookmarks
([post](https://x.com/henrydaubrez/status/2037554793850499369)). Long lists are an engagement format. The short
single-site posts are the ones working artists save.

## Search X

`token`: an X MCP or the X API. Read only. `tested 2026-11-03`:

| Bad | Result | Good | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| `film stills library references min_faves:200 -filter:retweets` | 7, Claude prompts, no libraries | `shotdeck` | 27, many real recommendations |
| `moodboard websites references cosmos savee are.na min_faves:100 -filter:retweets` | 0 | `are.na inspiration references designers` | 23, several real lists |
| `from:paultrillo AI references OR look OR slop min_faves:50` | `from:` ignored, unrelated posts | `from:paultrillo references` | 4, on target |
| `film-grab` | A fan event, no use | `"film-grab" OR "filmgrab" OR "film grab" references min_faves:30` | 19, top reference posts |
| `"art direction" AI slop references taste since:2026-09-01 min_faves:100` | Tim Pool, Peter Thiel, tennis | `フィルムスタディ min_faves:100` (film study) | 20, all film-study posts |
| `AI faces look the same real people casting reference photos...` | Article links, Claude posts | `레퍼런스 사이트 추천 min_faves:200` (reference site recommendations) | 5, 4 on target |
| People search `cinematographer director of photography` | 0 | Name a known person, then `from:name word` | Works |

Rules:

1. One term or one phrase per query. Name the site if you can. Add `min_faves:`.
2. Top-level `OR` broke the other limits: it returned posts with 0 likes and posts from 2019 and 2020. If you must use
   `OR`, keep it to spellings of one name, bracket it, and re-check the result:
   `("lookbook" OR "reference board") cinematographer min_faves:100 -filter:replies since:2025-06-01` (not run).
3. Re-filter client-side on the returned numbers and dates, every time. The count you ask for is not respected either: 6
   to 12 returned up to 41.
4. Try ja, ko and zh for any visual topic: `フィルムスタディ`, `레퍼런스 사이트`, `分镜 参考`. One post recommends `逆光` (backlight) and
   `ローキー` (low key) for a person's own search in film libraries
   ([post](https://x.com/ksd_illust/status/2099998194906087701)).
5. X has no sort by bookmarks. Filter with `min_faves:`, rank by bookmarks yourself, and read the author's first reply.
6. Do not search "moodboard". It mostly means an ADHD meme right now
   ([example](https://x.com/dnpoutboy/status/2117616815903736227), 12,709 likes). Bare tool names can mislead too:
   `framethrower` returned 25 hits, nearly all Amiga hardware.

Words that work: references, refs, slop, taste, character sheet, identity lock, role hierarchy, style bible, visual
bible, `DESIGN.md`, contact sheet. Operators: `min_faves:`, `min_retweets:`, `since:` (inclusive), `until:` (exclusive),
`filter:images`, `filter:videos`, `-filter:replies`, `-filter:retweets`, `from:`, `list:`, `url:`, `lang:`
([list](https://github.com/igorbrigadir/twitter-advanced-search), page mostly last checked in 2022).

## How to spot a bulk-published MCP server

Registry listings, stars and install counts are not quality signals here. Check for these patterns, seen on 2026-11-03.
One is a reason to look closer. Two means skip (`our proposal`).

- One account publishes hundreds or thousands of repos and is under a year old. We saw 1,931 and 165.
- One endpoint lists about 50 tools and only a few fit the source. The rest are unrelated (prediction markets, memory),
  or a router claims thousands of tools.
- Tool descriptions speak to the agent, such as "PREFER OVER WEB SEARCH". That steers the agent and pollutes its tool
  list.
- The registry entry points to a repo that returns 404, or its description is a template:
  `<Product> tools for <word>, <word>, <word> by <Publisher>`.
- A search server exposes checkout tools, such as `create_4k_checkout`. Never auto-approve those.
- The same skill appears under clone owners. On skills.sh, `lighting` returned one product-photography skill under six
  owners, at 28,905 to 60,459 installs.

Scan skill packs before installing with [NVIDIA/SkillSpector](https://github.com/NVIDIA/SkillSpector). Read a tool's
README for what it runs: one popular design skill warns its live mode may run agents with approvals and sandboxing off
([repo](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable)).
