# Who goes on camera

"The creator and talent matter more than the script or the arbitrary hooks" (Dara Denney). The person must look, talk and act like the buyer (Hormozi's "likeness" callout), and the room must be where the problem happens.

## Rules

1. **Age matches the claim.** "You cannot use a 22-year-old to sell joint support." Write the age as a number.
2. **Room matches the problem.** Energy: kitchen at 6 am. Sleep: bedroom, lights off. Bloating: bathroom mirror. Joints: halfway up the stairs. Private problems: the car.
3. **Mirror an identity the buyer already holds** (Sarah Levinger). A non-alcoholic hop tea moved from "non-alcoholic" to belonging ("You can have your hops and drink them too") and cut CAC 30% in two weeks. Stack 2–3 identities at most.
4. **Build personas from reviews**, named by trigger ("Depleted Woman", "Burned-Out Professional", "Brain-Fogged Mom"), each anchored to a real customer quote pasted into the brief.
5. **Ugly beats pretty.** A studio video with a real creator died at $600 of spend; a phone-quality version with a better script did $61K (self-reported). Phone at arm's length, real skin, small flaws.
6. **A recurring character beats random faces** on organic pages: people get attached to the same person.

## By category

| Category | Who | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Skincare, beauty | Woman whose age matches the claim, end-of-day selfie vlog, freckles and pores visible | Bathroom, bedroom | "No retouching, no skin smoothing"; never results claims from an AI face |
| Supplements | Pharmacist or professor (teacher-question), podcast host + guest, street-interview strangers | Kitchen, lecture hall, studio | Structure/function claims only; never a fake credential |
| Women 50+ problems (knees, sleep, bloating, hot flashes) | 50–60-year-old woman, phone at arm's length | Kitchen, stairs | "She buys at 11 pm and tells nobody" |
| Home, cleaning, kitchen | Hands + a real person's voice, or a parent mid-chore | The messy room itself | Problem and fix in one clip |
| Gadgets | Hands-on demo, a skeptical guy who becomes convinced | Desk, car, garage | Show it working, one number |
| Apparel | People who look like the buyer, several body types; try-on haul | Bedroom mirror, street | AI try-on smooths texture and length: check against the real garment |
| Pets | Owner + the pet's reaction | Living room, park | Real animal reaction is the proof |
| Fitness | A real-muscle coach or a self-deprecating beginner | Gym, home | See `varg-character-fitness` |
| Gifting (Q4) | The confused partner ("husbands who don't know what to get") | Store, couch | Lead with the delivery date in December |

## AI people: when and how

- **OK:** paid ads on TikTok (labelled) and Meta, organic pages (labelled), presenters who demo and state facts.
- **Not OK:** an AI person framed as a real customer with results; an AI salesperson on TikTok Shop; real people's likeness without permission; minors.
- **Realism:** prompt the camera's flaws (handheld tremor, autofocus hunts, rolling shutter, sensor noise), breaths and swallows between lines, phone-mic audio, natural asymmetric blinks. Lock face, wardrobe, light and "exactly two arms and two hands, one hand always holding the phone".
- **Consistency:** an identity sheet (front, side, ¾, full body, expressions), the same reference on every shot, one model per character. Character design skills: `varg-character-design` and its category recipes (`varg-character-grandma`, `varg-character-fitness`, …).
