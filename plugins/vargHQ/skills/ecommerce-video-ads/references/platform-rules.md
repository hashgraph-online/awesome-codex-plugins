# Platform rules for ecommerce videos (checked October 2026)

Tags: **[official]** read on a TikTok, Meta or FTC page · **[secondary]** a trade outlet summarising a login-gated document (mostly TikTok Seller University) · **[practitioner]**. Re-check secondary rules in Seller Center before relying on them.

## TikTok Shop (shoppable and affiliate videos)

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Hook window | Hook in the first 3–6 s, and it must relate to the featured product | [secondary] [ppc.land, Shop content styles](https://ppc.land/tiktok-shops-shoppable-video-content-guide-tells-creators-what-actually-works/) |
| Official content styles | Personal review · Before & after (typical results) · Unboxing · Step-by-step tutorial | [secondary] same |
| Moving footage | At least 3 s of dynamic footage; real setting, camera movement, visible people. No still images, slideshows, looping images or screenshots of the product page | [secondary] [ppc.land, quality rules 2026-05-23](https://ppc.land/tiktok-shops-quality-rules-ban-ai-voices-and-still-images-from-lives/) |
| AI voices | AI voices or pre-recorded narration standing in for a presenter are banned | [secondary] same |
| Hands-only / TTS | Repetitive-content policy (2025-09-25) lists hands-only demos with no creator presence and text-to-speech-only videos as low quality | [secondary] [BigSeller](https://www.bigseller.pro/blog/articleDetails/3766/tiktok-shop-policy-update-sept-2025.htm) |
| AI material | "Unverifiable or manipulated content, including AI-created or AI-modified material, is not allowed for general audiences" (2026-08-04) | [secondary] [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/tiktok-shop-bans-minors-from-selling-in-creator-videos-and-livestreams/) |
| Posting limit | 5+ "non-interactive or misleading" Shop videos in 7 days → product links capped at 7 videos for a week | [secondary] [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/tiktok-shops-tightening-grip-new-rules-that-could-freeze-your-account/) |
| Price in video | A price or deal said or shown must match the product page exactly and still be live. One affiliate deleted a 500K-view video and 30 others after a violation | [practitioner] [@ugctiamarie](https://x.com/ugctiamarie/status/2099871041661157549) |
| Claims | No weight-loss claims (hashtags included), no unsupported or "scientifically impossible" claims; before/after must show typical results; no body-weight before/after | [secondary] ppc.land (above) |
| Minors | Minors may not sell, narrate or recommend; adults lead demos | [secondary] ppc.land 2026-08-04 |
| Music | TikTok's commercial sound library only | [secondary] quality rules |
| Enforcement | 6 violations in 90 days can remove e-commerce permissions and freeze commissions | [secondary] ppc.land |
| Before posting | Run TikTok's Video Pre-Check | [practitioner] |

**Practical read:** on TikTok Shop, show a real person (face, real voice or a creator). Use AI for b-roll and product shots, never as the salesperson.

## TikTok ads

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| AI disclosure | Fully AI-generated content, real people shown doing what they didn't do, and cloned voices need the AIGC label or a visible disclaimer, or the ad is rejected. Minor edits (lighting, background removal) are not "significant" | [official] [TikTok misleading content policy (Apr 2026)](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-misleading-and-false-content) |
| Toggle | "This ad contains AI-generated content" in Ads Manager; resets when a campaign is duplicated | [secondary] agency reports |
| Claims | No exaggerated results, no absolute claims ("Number 1"), no before/after that gives a false impression (TikTok's example: wrinkles vanishing after a cream); promo and price must match the landing page; no fake close buttons | [official] same page |
| Weight | No "without diet or exercise", nothing easy or guaranteed; 18+ targeting; no body shaming | [official] [weight management policy](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-weight-management) |
| Music | Commercial Music Library; licensed for TikTok only | [official] [Creative Codes](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/creative-best-practices-top-performing-ads) |
| Specs | 9:16 recommended, ≥540×960 (make 1080×1920), ≤500 MB; safe zone depends on caption length, use TikTok's overlay | [official] [video ad specs](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/video-ads-specifications?lang=en) |
| Creatives | 3–5 very different creatives per ad group, 3–5 ad groups per campaign; ≥12 new videos a month on Smart+ and GMV Max | [official] [creative best practices](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en), [account recommendations](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/account-optimization-recommendations?lang=en) |

## Meta (Instagram Reels, Facebook)

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| AI label | Meta adds "AI info" to ads made or significantly edited with its own or detected third-party AI tools; self-disclosure is mandatory only for political and social-issue ads | [official] [Meta AI transparency](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/02/gen-ai-transparency-metas-ads-products/) |
| Health | Weight loss: 18+, no pinched-fat close-ups; supplements state the time to results; no cure claims. Before/after allowed for general cosmetics at 18+ | [official] [health and wellness policy](https://transparency.meta.com/en-gb/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness) |
| Personal attributes | No copy that asserts the viewer's attributes ("Do you have insomnia?") | [official] [personal health policy](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/objectionable-content/personal-health-and-appearance) |
| Music | No licensed music in Reels ads; original audio or Meta Sound Collection | [official] [Reels ad specs](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/instagram-reels) |
| Safe zone | Keep the top 14%, bottom 35% and 6% on each side clear | [official] same |
| Similarity | Ads that look or feel alike are grouped as variations of one creative; diversify by persona, story, look and format | [official] [demystifying creative diversification](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/demystifying-creative-diversification) |

## Safe script patterns

- AI presenter: "This sleeve has a silicone grip strip, so it doesn't roll down" (a fact) — fine. "I wore it for two weeks and my knees stopped hurting" — fabricated testimonial.
- Real proof: "4.8 stars from 2,100 reviews" (if true), a real customer's words quoted with permission and named as a review.
- Results: "helps reduce the appearance of…", "results vary"; never "cures", "guaranteed", "in 3 days".
- Offers: say a price only if it is the live product-page price; otherwise "tap the cart to see today's price".
