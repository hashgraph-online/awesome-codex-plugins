# Ecommerce video formats (October 2026)

Measured on Meta by Motion's 2026 benchmark (578,750 creatives, $1.29B spend; a "winner" spends ≥10× the account median): Unboxing 9.8% hit rate (only 2.1% of creatives), Offer-first banner 8.6%, Demo 8.1%, Testimonial 6.5%, then Headline, Montage, Before & After, Listicle, Split screen, Us vs Them, Feature-benefit, Cinematic b-roll, Problem-agitation, Review, How-to, POV, Founder ([source](https://runneth.com/library/research/creative-benchmarks-2026/top-visual-formats)).

| # | Format | What it is | When it works | AI-friendly? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unboxing | Package → reveal → first use, hands or creator | Gifts, gadgets, beauty; most under-made winner | Yes (hands + product lock) |
| 2 | Hands-on demo | The product doing its job in a real setting | Anything with a visible job | Yes |
| 3 | Problem and fix in one clip | The problem state, then the same scene fixed by the product; the bigger the visual gap, the better | Top TikTok Shop structure: cleaning, organizing, phone cases | Yes |
| 4 | Yapper | One person talks to camera 30–90 s like telling a friend; minimal editing, scripted beats, free delivery | Cold traffic, every category | Yes, with realism prompts; not for TikTok Shop |
| 5 | Street interview | A stranger is stopped and asked a question; "the question does the hooking"; no product for ~15 s | Social proof, gifting season | Yes (reference chain keeps the face) |
| 6 | Podcast clip | Host + guest discuss the problem; the product sells in conversation | Anything that needs explaining, supplements | Yes (`varg-podcast-video`) |
| 7 | Founder story | The founder tells why they built it | Rarely fatigues; trust | Real founder preferred |
| 8 | Us vs Them | Side by side with the alternative or the old way, on ONE attribute (price, time, ingredients) | Solution-aware buyers | Yes |
| 9 | David & Goliath | The underdog against the category giant | #1 in Dara Denney's accounts (Apr 2026) | Yes |
| 10 | Love letter | A creator's "love letter" to the product | Top-3 for Denney | Real creator preferred |
| 11 | "We're not cheap" | Openly justifies a premium price | Premium products | Yes |
| 12 | Listicle / 3 reasons why | Numbered reasons | Every awareness stage | Yes |
| 13 | Shelf walk | A creator in a store dismisses each competitor, then reveals yours | Retail categories | Real footage |
| 14 | Before / after | Typical results only; never for weight | Beauty, cleaning, home | Careful (claims) |
| 15 | Green screen reaction | Creator in front of a review, comment, ad or product page | Native TikTok; objection handling | Yes |
| 16 | Reply to a comment | Video reply to a real comment ("does it really work?") | Modifier for any format; TikTok shows it to people who saw the original | Real comment required |
| 17 | Split screen | Explain on one side, demo on the other; or a "did vs didn't use it" day | Energy, sleep, routines | Yes |
| 18 | Teacher question | Looks filmed from a lecture seat; a professor explains the mechanism | Supplements, health-adjacent | Yes |
| 19 | Expert / pharmacist | An expert explains the ingredient | Supplements (claims rules apply) | Label AI experts; never a fake credential |
| 20 | ASMR / satisfying | Macro sounds and textures: lid snap, foil peel, pour | Beauty, food, drinks | Yes (`product-photo-to-video`) |
| 21 | Ingredient burst / exploded view | The product's parts or ingredients fly out in slow motion | Premium, tech, food | Yes |
| 22 | AI animation | Claymation, Pixar-style, "inside your body" | Mechanisms a camera can't film; claymation has "the highest usable rate" | Yes |
| 23 | Micro-drama | A short show with a character and conflict; product arrives late | When classic UGC tires | Yes (`varg-consistent-characters`) |
| 24 | Recurring character world | The same AI character in every post (a grandma and her cat across seasons) | Organic pages, attachment | Yes |
| 25 | In-car | Private, unscripted feel; cheapest to render well with AI | Private problems, trust | Yes |
| 26 | Day in the life | The product across 3–5 scenes | Bottom of funnel | Yes |
| 27 | Mini VSL / education | 60 s–2 min: problem, mechanism, offer; product before halfway | Biggest spenders in some accounts | Yes |
| 28 | Native statics | Notes app, iMessage, review card, Reddit screenshot | Cheapest angle test; include 2 in every batch | Yes (image models) |
| 29 | Apology ad | The brand apologises (sold out, delays) | Restocks, launches | Yes |
| 30 | Seasonal | Halloween party, "guaranteed before Christmas", January fresh start | Q4 and January | Yes |

## Choosing

1. TikTok Shop → 1, 2, 3, 14, 15, 16 with a real presenter (see `tiktok-shop-video`).
2. Cold Meta traffic → 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8 (one concept each, different first frames).
3. A product that needs explaining → 6, 18, 27.
4. A winner exists → keep its format, test new hooks; then move its message into two other formats.

Sources: [Motion 2026](https://runneth.com/library/research/creative-benchmarks-2026/top-visual-formats), [TikTok Shop styles via ppc.land](https://ppc.land/tiktok-shops-shoppable-video-content-guide-tells-creators-what-actually-works/), [Dara Denney](https://runneth.com/library/talk/dara-denney-stop-guessing-start-diagnosing-ad-creative-testing/), X posts collected in [sources.md](sources.md).
