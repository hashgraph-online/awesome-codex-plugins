# Second-by-second templates

## Length

- TikTok Shop: no official length; practitioners use 20–35 s (unverified). Bottom-of-funnel Shop ads around 18 s.
- TikTok reservation ads: 9–15 s recommended ([official](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-reservation-in-feed-ads-reach-frequency?lang=en)).
- Meta Reels direct response: 15–30 s typical; education ads 90 s–2 min with the product before halfway.
- Test a 15 s cut against a 30 s cut with the same hook. TV data (MNTN, 1,800 ads): 15 s won visits (+46%), 30 s won conversions (+24%).

## Template A: universal 15–30 s

| Time | Beat | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1.5 s | Pattern interrupt + product or face | Movement, product in hand, unexpected visual |
| 0–3 s | Promise | Spoken line + matching text overlay |
| 3–6 s | Bridge into the product | No unrelated stunt hooks |
| 6–20 s | Demo + 1–3 benefits | Real setting, product shown more than once, one specific number |
| 20–26 s | Proof | Real review, result, count |
| last 3–4 s | CTA | Spoken + on screen; offer matches the product page |

## Template B: TikTok Shop bottom of funnel, 18 s

- 0:00 product visible from the first frame + a callout hook ("if you're over 50 and your knees crack…")
- 0:03–0:13 demo with **one specific number**
- 0:13–0:18 CTA pointing at the cart icon

## Template C: realistic UGC, 15 s in 4 beats

- 0:00–0:04 already mid-motion; the product comes up beside the face; one autofocus hunt; throwaway line ("Okay. Last thing, then bed.")
- 0:04–0:07 macro texture or label beat (label sharpest here)
- 0:07–0:11 using it, a real reaction (a flinch at the cold), talking while working
- 0:11–0:15 closest framing, checks herself, reaches to stop recording (frame tilts)

## Template D: yapper, 30–90 s

Story to camera, minimal edits. With AI, don't name the product as the solution until ~35 s. A human cuts on a blink, AI cuts on the pause: do the jump cuts by hand.

## Template E: street interview

The question hooks. No product or brand in the first seconds; it should not feel like an ad for ~15 s. The interviewee hedges, stalls and admits something rather than listing benefits.

## Template F: cold-traffic us vs them VSL

Open loop → the enemy (the whole industry) → deep on the problem → price anchor → the unique mechanism (~30 s, each material tied to a benefit) → benefit CTA + guarantee.

## Template G: founder story

Problem → what they tried → what worked → why they built it → what changed → offer.

## After a video pops (TikTok Shop)

Day 1 viral → days 2–3 answer the top comment questions → days 4–5 handle objections → days 6–7 post proof. Comments like "where do I buy this" and "restock?" mean buyers; shock or laughs alone don't. 5M-view videos often convert under 0.1%; 100K–800K views with high engagement is the sweet spot (self-reported).

## Pacing and sound

- Cut every 1–2 s in the hook, slower in the demo. Text ≤5–10 words per second.
- Speech + music together (Meta: 2.1× purchase intent). UGC generation: ambient sound only, add captions and music in the edit.
- -14 LUFS, captions in the safe zone (top 14%, bottom 35%, sides 6%).
