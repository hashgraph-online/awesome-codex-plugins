# Evidence: what is proven, what is not

The record behind `video-animate`. Read this before quoting a number publicly or changing a rule.

Labels: `[opened]` a researcher read the source. `[vendor]` a company selling or reselling it, or a paid partner.
`[self]` one person's own report, read in full; nobody here watched the videos, so verdicts on how an output looks belong
to their authors. `[measured]` varg's own measurement on local files, usually one film. `[internal]` varg's own files or
runs, not independently checked. `[inference]` our reasoning: no expert said it and nobody tested it. Checked 2026-11-03.

Three warnings. Seedance posts on X are not neutral: ByteDance's Dreamina pays for views through 2026-12-31
(https://x.com/dreamina_ai/status/2117243037835509798) and several tutorials carry partner or referral links. Most
hands-on tests come from resellers. Quotes taken from pages through a fetch tool, and translated Japanese or Chinese
posts, should be checked against the original before being used publicly.

## Common advice, tested

"Every motion has a cause" is an old idea with a new phrase: the wording comes from a 2026 skill for product UI demos,
and the idea from Disney-era animation. "Sound hits land on cuts" and "differ only in timing and camera" returned no
exact match on X, the web, GitHub or varg's repos, though that search has limits. Do not attribute the set to any expert.

| Advice | Verdict | Why | Use this wording |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Make a very cheap version first (Seedance 2.5 at 480p)" | **Holds with a change** | Draft-to-final frame correlation was 0.950 to 0.988 on five clips, against 0.257 for a direct 1080p control ([Segmind](https://blog.segmind.com/seedance-2-5-draft-final-5-clips-tested-and-the-real-cost-math/) [vendor]). The same seed at 480p and 720p gave different pictures: "These are not the same shot at two sizes." ([Segmind](https://blog.segmind.com/advanced-seedance-2-5-prompts-5-expert-patterns-tested-2026/) [vendor]). A Draft costs the same as plain 480p ([BytePlus](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/modelark/seedance-2-5) [opened]). varg shows no `draft` option ([varg spec](https://varg.ai/models/seedance_2_5.md) [internal]). | Draft every attempt at 480p in Draft mode. Approve staging, camera, timing and shot order. Finalize from the task ID. A re-run at higher resolution is a new take. |
| "One long generation for a long shot with a transition, short separate clips for different shots" | **Holds with a change** | "No cuts" held at 8 s and 20 s on Seedance 2.5 ([Segmind](https://blog.segmind.com/advanced-seedance-2-5-prompts-5-expert-patterns-tested-2026/), [Krea](https://www.krea.ai/blog/seedance-2-5-prompt-guide) [vendor]). But production prompts hold 5 different shots in 10 s ([Chris Capel via PJ Ace](https://x.com/PJaccetturo/status/2116160701400289449) [vendor, Dreamina partner]), and measured adoption favours 30 s over 5 s ([Shimura](https://x.com/ucmulleryu/status/2092572054872784904) [self]). The real split is world and job. | One run per location and light state. Start a new run when location or light changes, when a shot will need many retakes, or when the edit may move the cut. Harvest long takes. Keep obedient runs short. |
| "Something changes every 2 or 3 seconds" | **Holds with a change** | Generic retention advice, with no controlled evidence. The same account later wrote "SOMETHING CHANGES EVERY 4 TO 6 SECONDS" and added that a transition effect does not count ([MethodByVid](https://x.com/MethodByVid/status/2099815995502436365) [self]). Launch films run about one visual event per 1.6 s ([earshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/earshot/main/docs/launch-video-sound.md) [opened]) and big Hollywood films around 2000 average about 3.6 s ([Cutting](https://cinemetrics.uchicago.edu/article/590235ff-85aa-4714-9553-71d4c44fd61c) [opened]). | Something new happens every few seconds, at a rate set by the format. A change means new information, an action, a camera move or a sound event, never a transition effect. Vary the intervals. |
| "Every motion has a cause" | **Holds** | The wording comes from a 2026 skill for fake product UI demos, not from film: "every motion has a cause" ([Stageshow](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/benjitaylor/skills/main/stageshow/SKILL.md) [opened]). The idea is old craft. Walt Disney: "the driving force behind the action is the mood, the personality, the attitude of the character" (Walt Stanchfield, Drawn to Life). Lasseter: "some part must initiate the move, like the engine of a train." (John Lasseter, "Principles of Traditional Animation Applied to 3D Computer Animation", SIGGRAPH 1987). No test shows it improves generated output. | Every motion has a cause, named in the same sentence with a time. Every sound has a cause in the story. A camera move needs one too. |
| "Sound hits land on cuts" | **Does not hold** | In 7 launch films, a salient hit sits within ±100 ms of a visual event 29% of the time, against 27% by chance ([earshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/earshot/main/docs/launch-video-sound.md) [opened]). "About 85% of hard cuts carry no dedicated sound and under 5% a whoosh" ([cinetic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leonxlnx/cinetic/main/skills/cinetic/references/sound.md) [opened]). Launch-style sample by one author. Music videos and sparse soundtracks differ. | Hits land on the moments that matter. Most cuts ride on the music. With no music, every cut needs sound. |
| "Within 5 ms" | **Does not hold** as a perceptual or QA target. Fine as script placement | It comes from one synthesis parameter: "`apex` puts a whoosh's loudest moment within 5 ms of the target" ([earshot](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/earshot) [opened]). The only standard that says 5 ms is EBU R37's per-stage budget, which controls build-up in a chain; its end-to-end limit is 40 ms early, 60 ms late ([EBU R37](https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/r/r037.pdf) [opened]). One frame at 24 fps is 41.7 ms, and mishandled AAC priming alone adds 44 ms ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/technotes/tn2258/_index.html) [opened]). Beat trackers are scored at ±70 ms ([mir_eval](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/craffel/mir_eval/main/mir_eval/beat.py) [opened]). | Place sound by script to the sample. Accept to the frame. Past 40 ms early or 60 ms late is a defect. Report offsets by sound type. |
| "Make 3 takes that differ only in timing and camera and render each as a short clip" | **Does not hold** on a generative model. True for deterministic renders | Not documented anywhere the researchers searched. Changing one clause re-rolls the rest: "Everything it left to the model was re-rolled." ([Segmind](https://blog.segmind.com/advanced-seedance-2-5-prompts-5-expert-patterns-tested-2026/) [vendor]). Published practice locks camera and varies acting: "the only thing changing between attempts is the performance" ([Chris via PJ Ace](https://x.com/PJaccetturo/status/2116160709180723486) [vendor, Dreamina partner]). The number 3 has support: cuts given 3 takes reached the screen at 22.3%, 1 take at 8.8% ([Shimura](https://x.com/ucmulleryu/status/2091821264302412030) [self]). | Decide timing and camera in the previz, where variants are free and exact, and pick one. Then roll 3 takes with camera and timing locked by `@video1`, varying only the performance. Allow 5 to 7 for acting. Same failure twice: stop and change the shot. |

## What nobody has measured

- Draft to final on video-to-video from a previz, and identity (faces) between draft and final.
- Upscaling 480p to 1080p independently. The one test covers 720p to 1080p and comes from a reseller.
- One previz run through all the main models. Rankings on Artificial Analysis and OpenArt are preference votes, do not
  score fidelity to a previz, and do not list Kling 4.
- Length thresholds. No decay curve is published for Seedance 2.5, Kling 4, Veo 3.1 or Wan 3. 4 to 11 s is varg's
  working range, not a finding.
- Whether a model obeys beat timing in an audio reference. No vendor documents it and nobody has measured it.
- Perception of a transient on a hard cut. The sync figures extrapolate from lip sync, object action and launch films.
- A fully textured, lit render against grey on the same shot.
- Whether keyed anticipation or overshoot survives the model, frame by frame, and whether a stepped reference survives.
- Drift from the previz on Kling 4, Veo, Wan or MiniMax 3D to Video. Our drift figures are one film on Seedance 2.5.
- A side-by-side cost of the same shot by code, AI and hybrid. The one comparison is David Ariew's, for fast particles.
- Baked grain against clean plates, and upscaling before or after matting. Those rules are reasoning only.

Limits of this research: no researcher watched the videos, YouTube transcripts failed, X Articles returned no text, the
web search budget ran out during several threads, and the wider 2026 scan for shot-indexing startups did not finish.

## Seven cheap tests that would replace most of the guesses

None has been run, because each needs a spend approval. Costs are our estimates in credits.

| # | Test | What it settles | Rough cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One clay shot, 5 s at 720p or 768p, through BytePlus Seedance, H3 3D to Video, Kling 4, Wan 3 and HeyGen Video 1 | The model table. No head-to-head exists | About 403 credits |
| 2 | Three previz runs, 30 s against 3 x 10 s, at 480p video-to-video | Long against short under locked timing | About 2,300 to 2,900 credits (180 s out) |
| 3 | Same shot: grey, flat ID colours, depth, with and without mapping text | Conflict 2, capsule logs, depth from previz | About 400 credits (6 arms, 5 s at 480p) |
| 4 | Click track as `@audio1`, direct against black-screen, offset of visual events to clicks | Beat obedience, the key missing experiment | About 400 credits (2 arms, 3 takes) |
| 5 | Hits at 0, +40, +80 and +125 ms on the same cuts, blind viewers on generated footage | Whether 80 ms reads wrong in practice | No generation cost |
| 6 | Smooth previz, stepped previz, cadence prompt, post-only, measured for held frames | Cadence on current models | About 200 credits (3 generated arms) |
| 7 | Same prompt and seed, with and without "film grain"; `mov` against mp4 through varg's mirror; tracker and matte metrics | Clean plate rule and output format | About 600 credits per pair at 1080p |

## Where this skill overrides older varg skills

When `video-animate` is in charge, apply these over the older text until those skills are updated.

| Older line | Skill | Apply instead |
|---|---|---|
| Hard-cut timing "kept exactly" | `varg-previz`, `threejs-animatic`, `varg-video-prompting` | Within a frame on our 4 to 12 s `@video1` runs, about a second from text timestamps, ±0.6 s on a dense previz on Seedance 2.0. Measure every take |
| Lands each effect "within 5 ms"; a hit "80 ms after the cut already reads wrong" | `varg-previz`, `varg-video-prompting` | 5 ms is a whoosh `apex` parameter. Place by script, accept to the frame, past 60 ms late is a defect (EBU R37) |
| "every shot start / end is a downbeat"; "Cuts land on downbeats. Always." | `varg-previz`, `varg-music-video` | Structural moments on downbeats, ordinary cuts on any grid point, off-grid cuts declared |
| "these shots go out with `audio: false`" | `varg-previz` audio-lock | Sound on for mouths, then discard. `audio: false` for everything else |
| "up to 9 images, 3 videos, 3 audios" | `varg-previz`, `threejs-animatic`, `varg-ai`, `varg-video-prompting` | Per route: 9/3/3 on PiAPI, 30/10/10 with 50 assets on BytePlus. Plan for 9/3/3 and read the live schema |
| "about 16 credits per second" at 480p | `varg-previz`, `varg-video-prompting` | Predates pass-through pricing. About 13 on the BytePlus route, an estimate: confirm with `estimate_cost` |
| "Draft at 720p; rerun the chosen take at 1080p" | `kling-4-keyframe-film` | By analogy with Seedance a rerun is a new take: upscale it, or budget new takes at 1080p. No Kling test either way |
| "Mannequins need real limbs, a head with a nose, and feet" | `threejs-animatic`, `varg-previz` | Solids plus a written mapping by default, jointed figures when pose or contact is the point. Test 3 settles it |
| A pose change is "never a pop" | `threejs-animatic` | True for realistic work. Cartoons need a pop and a 2-frame per-link lag |
| "One shot per lyric line"; "pair one dance move with one cut" | `varg-music-video` | One mode among several. For dance, master plus inserts is the better-sourced default; holding 30 s of full-body dance is untested |
| Previz row names only `seedance_2_5` | `varg-ai`, `varg-video-prompting` | Also `minimax_h3_max_3d_to_video` |
