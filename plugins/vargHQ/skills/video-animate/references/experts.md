# Experts: who has animated from a previz, and what to cite them for

Who to cite for which decision, which posts and videos to read first, and whose numbers to discount. Cite a person for
the technique listed, never as a general authority. Checked 2026-11-03; engagement numbers are as read that day.
`[vendor]` = company selling or reselling it, or a paid partner; `[self]` = one person's own report; `[measured]` = varg's own measurement; `[inference]` = our reasoning; no tag = opened primary source.

## Months of experience, not years

The method only spread from June 2026, so its experts have months of experience. About a dozen people have shipped
anything with it, and no named expert publishes a full rule for which shot goes to which tool. The usable record comes
from individuals on X rather than studios, and many of the strongest write in Japanese or Chinese, so an English-only
search misses the best measured data.

- Adoption rates, costs and take counts are self-reported. No researcher watched the videos: we read post text, replies,
  X Articles, GitHub files and YouTube metadata, so every verdict on output quality belongs to its author. The one
  independent note found is [an animator on character weight](https://x.com/isidro_quintana/status/2090920278985498629),
  which [Shimura accepted](https://x.com/ucmulleryu/status/2091103355657666901).
- Rank by shipped work, not reach: the [32.2% versus 3.3% post](https://x.com/ucmulleryu/status/2092572054872784904) drew
  58 likes while promotional threads drew thousands.
- "translated" marks a Japanese or Chinese post, given in our translation. Others may be too: check before quoting.

this means do not cite them for it, not that they have none.
