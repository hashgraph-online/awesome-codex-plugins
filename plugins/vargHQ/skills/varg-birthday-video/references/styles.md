### Clauses

**Carved-number milestone poster**: luxury milestone poster: a giant number [age] carved deep into an off-white paper-textured wall with beveled edges and inner shadows, the hollow filled with pastel balloons, white roses and baby's breath, [subject] sitting on the lower curve of the number with one leg and one hand breaking out of the cut, warm side sunlight with a soft rim light, elegant serif name and 'CHAPTER [age]' typography at the top, 3:4
Avoid: foil number balloons instead of a carved wall, flat sticker digits, misspelled or warped numbers, cluttered party backdrop, neon colors, cartoon props, limbs that melt into the wall where the body crosses the cut

**Pop-up card that unfolds**: [subject] as a handcrafted 3D pop-up card on a wooden table: the card starts closed, slowly opens, and the scene rises from the fold as layered matte cardstock with visible fibers, crisp fold lines and small drop shadows between the layers, soft pastel palette, warm tabletop light, slow push-in, real paper physics
Avoid: glossy plastic or CGI look, a flat picture printed on the page, paper bending like cloth, morphing instead of unfolding, extra pages appearing, warped text on the card

**Classic illustrated holiday card**: a traditional illustrated holiday greeting card of [subject]: ink line drawing with soft watercolor and gouache washes on cream card stock, holly-and-pine border, gently falling snow, a winter village behind, warm muted reds and greens, hand-lettered greeting '[message]' set into the top of the design, friendly classic card layout, portrait 5x7
Avoid: photorealistic faces, glossy 3D render, neon colors, clip-art clutter, misspelled greeting, stock-photo frame, harsh black outlines

**Snow-globe keepsake**: a glass snow globe (or mason-jar snow globe) on a rustic wooden table containing a tiny, highly detailed [subject] standing on fluffy white snow beside a miniature bottle-brush pine tree, gently falling snowflakes inside the glass, fairy lights wrapped around the base, warm golden bokeh behind, glass reflections and refraction, macro shallow depth of field
Avoid: subject too big for the globe, no visible glass edge or reflections, plastic toy face, snow outside the glass, a face distorted by the glass, cluttered background

**Photo-booth strip**: a vintage photo-booth strip of [subject]: four frames in a vertical strip (or a 2x2 sheet) with thin white borders, the same plain curtain backdrop in every frame, direct on-axis booth flash from slightly above, playful poses and expressions changing frame to frame, black-and-white or warm faded color, light film grain, slightly uneven exposure
Avoid: a different background in each frame, softbox glamour lighting, identity drift between frames, extra people appearing, app UI overlays, perfectly symmetrical poses

**Cozy holiday film still**: [subject] in a nostalgic 1990s holiday-movie film still: chunky knit sweater, warm tungsten lamps and Christmas tree lights, snow falling outside, soft halation around every light, gentle 35mm film grain, slightly lifted blacks, candid mid-moment medium shot, shallow depth of field
Avoid: HDR glow, oversaturated red and green, plastic skin, TV screens showing real films or characters, logos on mugs or sweaters, studio flash, posed catalog look

**Diwali diya-glow portrait**: [subject] in a Diwali night portrait lit by dozens of clay diyas and one sparkler: marigold garlands, a colorful rangoli on the courtyard floor, paper lanterns overhead, red and gold silk with zari embroidery catching warm amber flame light, deep golden bokeh of tiny lights, soft smoke, 85mm shallow depth of field
Avoid: electric LED look, cold white light, fireworks covering the face, cartoon diyas, idols or temple logos, text banners, plastic skin

**Studio birthday editorial**: studio birthday editorial portrait of [subject]: seamless paper backdrop, oversized number balloons and a small cluster of black, silver or pastel balloons, one tall cake with lit candles, polished outfit, a single hard key light with soft fill (or direct flash mixed with candlelight), clean negative space, 85mm, natural skin retouching
Avoid: busy party decor, banners with misspelled text, plastic skin, extra fingers around the cake, melted candles, wedding-style backdrop, cartoon props

**Diorama inside an object**: a miniature diorama of [subject] built inside a [container: hollowed-out standing book, teacup, ring box or lightbulb], layered handcrafted details receding into the object, tiny figures on the ledges, warm light glowing from deep inside, macro photography, tilt-shift blur at the edges, visible paper and model textures
Avoid: a flat open book with a printed picture, scale mismatch between figures and object, the object's edges missing, real book titles or logos, plastic CGI sheen

**Doodle scrapbook overlay**: [subject] as a playful scrapbook page: the photo framed by torn paper and washi-taped instant prints, hand-drawn marker doodles (hearts, stars, arrows, sparkles), sticker smileys, handwritten captions reading '[message]', pastel palette, visible paper grain, slightly chaotic journal layout, 9:16
Avoid: clean corporate poster layout, misspelled captions, doodles covering the face, real brand stickers, neon gradients, 3D render

**1990s film snapshot**: candid 1990s 35mm film snapshot of [subject]: point-and-shoot flash or soft window light, warm faded color with slightly green shadows, soft grain, slight motion blur, a lived-in room with posters, cassettes and a CRT television, imperfect off-center framing, printed-photo look
Avoid: modern smartphones or flat-screen TVs, crisp digital sharpness, HDR, brand logos on clothes, perfect studio lighting, Y2K chrome

## Rules for every look

- Names, ages and dates: GPT Image 2.5 spells short text on a still when you quote and spell it; anywhere else add the words as overlay type, never as generated video text.
- No studio characters, real brands on balloons or cakes, or celebrities; no realistic children made from scratch.
- One look per video. Mixing a camcorder tape with a glossy 3D card reads as two videos.
