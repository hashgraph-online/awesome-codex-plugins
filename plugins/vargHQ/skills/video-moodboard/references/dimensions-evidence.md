# Evidence per dimension (from 1,540 X posts read by agents)

⚑ marks a promo-style post (DM funnel, income or cost claim, hype): its craft tip is kept, its claims were not checked. 
For each dimension: the readers' verdict, every tip they kept (strongest first), what fails, and the vocabulary that showed up in the best posts. Links go to the post. Use the vocabulary list when you write a board line; never paste a vague word from it without a number or a named thing.

## Palette

**Verdict:** Palette matters as a real lever but the posts show it is mostly handled as hex-coded fields in code-made video and as a post-grade step for photoreal AI, not as a prompt adjective. The strongest posts agree on a small named palette with roles, one warm/cold contrast, and a grade copied from a reference (JSON extraction, Match Color) rather than invented. Agents most often leave the palette to model defaults (flat grey look, purple gradients) or describe it as vague mood words instead of exact hex, colour names per garment and a stated grade.

**Tips**

- Do: Give the model exact hex values for the big colour fields (sky, floor, canvas, gradient ends) instead of colour adjectives. Example: `a vertical gradient #081d2c to #102348` ([@finnardley](https://x.com/finnardley/status/2114305886420086817), 5,291 saves · 3,098 likes · 257,155 views)
- Do: Define a tiny named palette with roles (paper, ink, accent, neutral, card) and hex for each. Example: `warm cream paper (#EBE7E1 with fine noise grain). Colours: ink #1E1C1A, red #D7372C, grey #8A857E.` ([@finnardley](https://x.com/finnardley/status/2116010421740007894), 1,817 saves · 987 likes · 58,987 views)
- Do: For code-made video, give two hex values for the backdrop (centre and corners) so light falloff reads as a studio floor. Example: `#f9fbfb in the middle, #f2f3f6 in the corners` ([@twoclipping](https://x.com/twoclipping/status/2110329502676951367), 4,372 saves · 2,175 likes · 153,516 views)
- Do: Build the palette on one warm versus cold contrast and name both sides (cold night, warm firelight and windows). Example: `a cold, rainy night against warm pockets of firelight and glowing windows` ([@zer0point_eth](https://x.com/zer0point_eth/status/2116832762912973220), 797 saves · 844 likes · 75,407 views)
- Do: For realistic AI footage, grade for realism: lower contrast, lift shadows, add a slight fade, then add grain. Example: `Drop contrast, push shadows up, add slight fade` ([@brendanhaze](https://x.com/brendanhaze/status/2031092943000346814), 1,716 saves · 892 likes · 82,652 views)
- Do: Take the grade from a real reference photo: have an LLM extract it as colour-grading JSON and paste that into the image prompt. ([@recap_david](https://x.com/recap_david/status/2023792981208429046), 1,370 saves · 659 likes · 21,326 views)
- Do: When swapping characters or restyling a clip, tell the model to keep the source grade. Example: `keep the same motion, color grading and camera quality.` ([@simonecanciello](https://x.com/simonecanciello/status/2105674718346871149), 1,770 saves · 1,111 likes · 231,183 views)
- Do: Match colours across shots by grading everything to one reference frame (Match Color, color-matcher). ([@ciguleva](https://x.com/ciguleva/status/1994131108385214763), 1,530 saves · 1,537 likes · 70,919 views) Also: [@0xwhrrari](https://x.com/0xwhrrari/status/2117240315069276186)
- Do: Limit a stylised piece to one ink colour (optionally a restrained second overprint) with lots of white space. ([@yanliudreamer](https://x.com/yanliudreamer/status/2093968800316293400), 10,649 saves · 6,990 likes · 1,392,505 views)
- Do: Use a limited vintage palette tied to the scene: warm muted tones on ivory or off-white paper. Example: `limited vintage color palette inspired by the original environment, with warm muted tones printed on an ivory/off-white paper background` ([@Naiknelofar788](https://x.com/Naiknelofar788/status/2101161664892829838), 2,220 saves · 1,696 likes · 129,472 views)
- Do: Lock outfit colours in the subject line with muted, specific names so Seedance keeps them across shots. Example: `faded charcoal-grey sleeveless crop top, loose high-waisted light-wash jeans, black canvas sneakers` ([@john_my07](https://x.com/john_my07/status/2071977017474789557), 21,231 saves · 15,983 likes · 12,927,356 views)
- Do: Describe HDR-style grade in words: deep clean blacks, controlled saturation, luminous specular highlights. Example: `graded for HDR with luminous specular highlights that exceed paper white, deep clean blacks, rich but controlled saturation` ([@kloss_xyz](https://x.com/kloss_xyz/status/2116612471880610054), 273 saves · 213 likes · 16,192 views)
- Do: Pair colour with colour temperature in Kelvin when asking an agent to reverse-engineer or write a look. Example: `3200K tungsten` ([@starks_arq](https://x.com/starks_arq/status/2035367453711360340), 1,753 saves · 935 likes · 87,666 views)
- Do: For brand work, derive every colour from the logo and ask for primary and secondary palettes with short HEX codes and usage roles. ([@Naiknelofar788](https://x.com/Naiknelofar788/status/2050229191841620373), 2,048 saves · 1,702 likes · 129,152 views)
- Don’t: Never hand the model a vague palette and hope: without a reference or a palette it falls back to a default gradient look. ([@rexan_wong](https://x.com/rexan_wong/status/2103707054108299437), 18,187 saves · 6,889 likes · 647,796 views)
- Don’t: Do not leave the palette to the model's defaults (purple/blue gradients on white); name the colours or ban the slop palette. Example: `NO purple/blue gradients on white backgrounds` ([@cloudxdev](https://x.com/cloudxdev/status/2002526815022190985), 4,322 saves · 1,681 likes · 156,357 views)

## Light

**Verdict:** Light is treated as the main realism lever by the most specific posts: one says AI films look fake because nobody directed the light, and the strongest advice is concrete (one key light 30-60 degrees to the side, motivated source, 3200K, 4:1 fill, natural window light only for UGC). Posts agree that lighting must be named as physical source, direction and colour temperature, and kept identical across clips and reference sheets. Agents most often write 'cinematic lighting', leave scenes evenly bright, or ask for perfect clean light where the target look needs imperfect, motivated or flat light.

**Tips**

- Do: Write light as measurable specs (key direction, Kelvin colour temperature, fill ratio) instead of 'cinematic lighting'. Example: `85mm. Hard key upper left. 3200K tungsten. 4:1 fill ratio.` ([@starks_arq](https://x.com/starks_arq/status/2035367453711360340), 1,753 saves · 935 likes · 87,666 views) Also: [@zazzygfx](https://x.com/zazzygfx/status/2042690351526248917)
- Do: For realistic influencer/UGC looks, ask for natural window light only and drop 'perfect lighting'. Example: `Natural window light only` ([@recap_david](https://x.com/recap_david/status/2021970126091039028) ⚑, 2,168 saves · 963 likes · 41,478 views)
- Do: Keep lighting, time of day and actor look identical across every clip in a sequence. Example: `same time-of-day and lighting style` ([@brendanhaze](https://x.com/brendanhaze/status/2031092943000346814), 1,716 saves · 892 likes · 82,652 views) Also: [@firatbilal](https://x.com/firatbilal/status/1996027417215815991)
- Do: Shoot character reference sheets on a flat 18% gray background with shadowless light so no lighting is baked into the identity. Example: `Clean light gray background, consistent and natural lighting.` ([@sirlifehacker](https://x.com/sirlifehacker/status/2115227821912281228), 943 saves · 637 likes · 30,400 views) Also: [@simonecanciello](https://x.com/simonecanciello/status/2116268465463976312) ⚑, [@simonecanciello](https://x.com/simonecanciello/status/2111099437065855376)
- Do: Use named lighting types from a vocabulary list rather than a generic 'good lighting'. Example: `window light with bounce` ([@madamayo_](https://x.com/madamayo_/status/1998331906996256877), 2,028 saves · 1,355 likes · 59,111 views)
- Do: Start with one key light 30-60 degrees to one side and slightly above eye level so direction gives the face shape. ([@beginnersblog1](https://x.com/beginnersblog1/status/2117355436789977462), 195 saves · 121 likes · 5,252 views)
- Do: Motivate every light: name a window, lamp, monitor or streetlight as the source of the glow. ([@beginnersblog1](https://x.com/beginnersblog1/status/2117355436789977462), 195 saves · 121 likes · 5,252 views) Also: [@mightyking](https://x.com/mightyking/status/2089299068514148655)
- Don’t: Do not leave the scene evenly bright; direct the light, because flat even illumination is what makes AI film look artificial. ([@beginnersblog1](https://x.com/beginnersblog1/status/2117355436789977462), 195 saves · 121 likes · 5,252 views)
- Do: Light the whole scene from one source family (one sun) so every shot and extra shares the same light. Example: `light everything from one sun` ([@NEXUS_TO_NOVA](https://x.com/NEXUS_TO_NOVA/status/2117261732489925018), 263 saves · 276 likes · 15,067 views)
- Do: Build warm/cold contrast: a cold ambient base with warm pockets from practicals, firelight or windows. Example: `a cold, rainy night against warm pockets of firelight and glowing windows` ([@zer0point_eth](https://x.com/zer0point_eth/status/2116832762912973220), 797 saves · 844 likes · 75,407 views) Also: [@techhalla](https://x.com/techhalla/status/2083389002552664385), [@zazzygfx](https://x.com/zazzygfx/status/2042690351526248917)
- Do: Position the camera toward the shadow side of the face to get depth instead of asking for 'more cinematic'. ([@beginnersblog1](https://x.com/beginnersblog1/status/2117355436789977462), 195 saves · 121 likes · 5,252 views)
- Do: Reverse-engineer lighting from a reference frame: feed the frame to Claude and ask for lens, lighting and colour temperature before prompting. ([@starks_arq](https://x.com/starks_arq/status/2035367453711360340), 1,753 saves · 935 likes · 87,666 views)
- Do: For skin realism use side/rim light wording that keeps pores and natural highlights. Example: `soft diffuse directional light, gentle rim light, authentic skin texture with visible pores and natural highlights` ([@AYi_AInotes](https://x.com/AYi_AInotes/status/2117508777062584363) ⚑, 624 saves · 441 likes · 79,210 views)
- Do: For amateur night/flash footage, name the harsh built-in phone flash and auto-exposure flicker. Example: `with the phone's harsh built-in flash, shaky handheld, grainy, slight motion blur, auto-exposure flicker` ([@devgetsit](https://x.com/devgetsit/status/2117345392471793728), 539 saves · 826 likes · 65,149 views) Also: [@xmliisu](https://x.com/xmliisu/status/1993152669150822852)
- Don’t: Do not describe a shot as 'Cinematic, Realistic, 4K'; answer where the light comes from instead. Example: `Cinematic man running through a city. Realistic. 4K.` ([@beginnersblog1](https://x.com/beginnersblog1/status/2105685837812007258), 1,921 saves · 1,239 likes · 54,203 views)

**Fails**

- 'Cinematic, Realistic, 4K' describes a vibe, not a lit shot ([@beginnersblog1](https://x.com/beginnersblog1/status/2105685837812007258)): "That describes a vibe."

## Framing

**Verdict:** Framing is a core but under-specified dimension in these posts: the strongest ones say AI defaults to a medium shot unless you name shot size, angle, lens in mm and aspect ratio, and that vibe words do not direct a shot. They agree on explicit shot labels, numeric lens and tilt values, subject-size percentages, and imperfect off-center framing for UGC realism. Agents most often leave the shot and lens unstated, or force polished symmetric composition where a raw look was wanted.

**Tips**

- Do: Name the shot size and angle explicitly (establishing, full, medium, close-up, macro close-up, over-the-shoulder, high/low angle, profile, three-quarter, back view) instead of letting the model default to a medium shot. Example: `Establishing Shot: Far away, environment focus.` ([@tetsuoai](https://x.com/tetsuoai/status/1997397799936139739), 9,884 saves · 4,906 likes · 4,968,160 views)
- Do: Write the prompt as a directed shot: intent, subject and action, frame, optics, camera, performance, light, continuity, not a vibe word list. Example: `INTENT → SUBJECT + ACTION → FRAME → OPTICS → CAMERA → PERFORMANCE + PHYSICS → LIGHT + SPACE → CONTINUITY` ([@beginnersblog1](https://x.com/beginnersblog1/status/2105685837812007258), 1,921 saves · 1,239 likes · 54,203 views)
- Do: Name the lens for every shot (mm plus aperture) and write the prompt like a real shoot with one light source. Example: `Name the lens for each shot, let the camera react a beat late like a real operator, light everything from one sun` ([@NEXUS_TO_NOVA](https://x.com/NEXUS_TO_NOVA/status/2117261732489925018), 263 saves · 276 likes · 15,067 views)
- Do: Give numbers for lens, tilt and subject size in frame: focal length, canted-angle degrees and the share of the frame the subject fills. Example: `Shot on digital cinema camera with 85mm prime lens; Low-angle, Dutch/canted perspective, tilted 15-25°; logo occupies ~50% of vertical frame` ([@zazzygfx](https://x.com/zazzygfx/status/2042690351526248917), 1,823 saves · 1,403 likes · 83,580 views)
- Do: For portraits, state how much of the frame height the head fills and how much headroom to leave. Example: `head from hair crown to chin occupying 50–60% of frame height, full face and chin within the crop, little headroom` ([@VIBEQUIRKLABS](https://x.com/VIBEQUIRKLABS/status/2116168611773726743), 399 saves · 922 likes · 21,471 views)
- Do: If the model frames too tight, describe the subject as tiny in the frame to push the camera back; if legs are cropped in landscape, fix it with a full-body or wider shot term. Example: `describe your character as a "tiny silhouette" to force the camera back` ([@tetsuoai](https://x.com/tetsuoai/status/1997397799936139739), 9,884 saves · 4,906 likes · 4,968,160 views)
- Do: Front-load the non-negotiables (aspect ratio, medium, shot type, location, framing) at the start of the prompt. Example: `3:4 vertical format` ([@IamEmily2050](https://x.com/IamEmily2050/status/2018697592800981099), 2,677 saves · 1,423 likes · 121,886 views)
- Do: Pair an aspect ratio with a lens in the same line for phone-style video so the model matches the format. Example: `A photoreal selfie video, 3:4, 22 mm` ([@ZssBecker](https://x.com/ZssBecker/status/2113320998527221875), 3,136 saves · 1,050 likes · 136,630 views)
- Do: Use a shot-angle vocabulary grid (MCU, MS, OS, WS, HA, LA, profile, three-quarter, back) when you need coverage of one subject from many angles. Example: `MCU = Macro Close Up; MS = Medium Shot; OS = Over the Shoulder; HA = High Angle; LA = Low Angle; ThreeQ = Three-Quarter View` ([@ProperPrompter](https://x.com/ProperPrompter/status/1998867010244280559), 3,215 saves · 2,270 likes · 162,194 views)
- Do: Label the shot size at the start of each time block in a multi-shot video prompt. Example: `0-4s: [Medium Wide] A striking young woman in her early 20s sits alone at the counter` ([@techhalla](https://x.com/techhalla/status/2083389002552664385), 2,537 saves · 2,870 likes · 229,013 views)
- Do: For raw UGC realism, ask for wide-angle, deep focus, handheld and imperfect, off-center framing instead of polished composition. Example: `handheld throughout, filmed by a friend who walks with the group, casual sway, natural night lighting, wide-angle lens, deep focus` ([@mightyking](https://x.com/mightyking/status/2089299068514148655), 2,833 saves · 2,034 likes · 424,537 views) Also: [@recap_david](https://x.com/recap_david/status/2021970126091039028) ⚑, [@genuenci](https://x.com/genuenci/status/2088679915491074155) ⚑
- Do: Reverse-engineer framing from a reference frame (ShotDeck or your own still) and paste the lens, light and placement it returns into the prompt. Example: `85mm. Hard key upper left. 3200K tungsten. 4:1 fill ratio. Shallow DOF. Subject in right third.` ([@starks_arq](https://x.com/starks_arq/status/2035367453711360340), 1,753 saves · 935 likes · 87,666 views)
- Do: Use portrait optics wording for shallow depth of field: long lens, wide aperture, optical compression. Example: `85mm portrait lens, f/1.8 shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, natural optical compression` ([@AYi_AInotes](https://x.com/AYi_AInotes/status/2117508777062584363) ⚑, 624 saves · 441 likes · 79,210 views)
- Do: Describe a non-film medium by its camera: a game camera gets rigid follow distance, flat exposure and deep focus, not cinematic framing. Example: `I described the camera like a game camera. Rigid follow distance. Snap turns.` ([@_VVSVS](https://x.com/_VVSVS/status/2090813096310784160), 1,893 saves · 1,846 likes · 219,202 views)
- Do: Once strong anchor frames exist, let the video model choose framing and camera operation rather than over-specifying it. ([@CharlieDFilm](https://x.com/CharlieDFilm/status/2098929162735997071), 6,062 saves · 6,041 likes · 1,271,559 views)
- Do: Keep orbit/arc camera moves small, under 30 degrees, for product and hero shots. Example: `Arc / orbit — circles the subject` ([@beginnersblog1](https://x.com/beginnersblog1/status/2065759796104761770), 2,269 saves · 1,408 likes · 69,499 views)

**Fails**

- Vague vibe prompt that names no shot, lens or camera position. ([@beginnersblog1](https://x.com/beginnersblog1/status/2105685837812007258)): "That describes a vibe."
- Default output is a medium shot when no shot type is given. ([@tetsuoai](https://x.com/tetsuoai/status/1997397799936139739)): "get the same boring medium shot every time."

## Texture

**Verdict:** Texture is one of the main anti-slop levers: the strongest posts treat the plastic, too-clean look as the top tell of AI video and fix it with named capture devices and eras, specific skin flaws and grain. The posts agree that flaws must be specific and physical (pores, asymmetry, fingerprints, halation, handheld flicker) and that a cheap grain and compression pass in post is worth doing. Agents most often write generic 'photorealistic, 8k, cinematic' or add grain to mediums that should be clean, instead of naming one concrete capture medium.

**Tips**

- Do: List specific skin and face flaws (visible pores, uneven skin texture, facial asymmetry, under-eye darkness, smile lines) plus 'shot on iPhone' and 'subtle phone-camera grain' instead of asking for perfect skin. Example: `Subtle phone-camera grain` ([@recap_david](https://x.com/recap_david/status/2021970126091039028) ⚑, 2,168 saves · 963 likes · 41,478 views)
- Do: Name a real capture device and its flaws (phone, built-in flash, handheld, auto-exposure flicker, grain) and say 'not cinematic' for a candid look. Example: `Amateur vertical iPhone video, 9:16, filmed at 2am at a Miami hotel pool with the phone's harsh built-in flash, shaky handheld, grainy, slight motion blur, auto-exposure flicker, candid real home video look, not cinematic` ([@devgetsit](https://x.com/devgetsit/status/2117345392471793728), 539 saves · 826 likes · 65,149 views)
- Do: After generation, add 2-3% film grain, a light room-tone bed, export at 1080p and run one compression round to kill the clean digital look. Example: `add 2-3% film grain in capcut` ([@knoxtwts](https://x.com/knoxtwts/status/2028840621423644728), 5,763 saves · 3,446 likes · 210,846 views)
- Do: Pick an era-specific consumer medium (early-2000s DV home video, VHS, webcam, 35mm analog) and state it as the first line of the style block. Example: `Raw friend-recorded early-2000s consumer DV footage` ([@kingofdairyque](https://x.com/kingofdairyque/status/2096833375369929039), 4,208 saves · 5,435 likes · 754,103 views)
- Do: For 35mm analog looks, name the format and add flash glow plus halated lights: 'Natural texture with slight glow from flash', 'slightly halated streetlights'. Example: `Blurred and slightly halated streetlights, car headlights, or neon signs` ([@xmliisu](https://x.com/xmliisu/status/1993152669150822852), 15,976 saves · 4,474 likes · 7,490,286 views)
- Do: Keep a swipe file of camera-style prompts (old school VHS, Fujifilm camera, MacBook webcam, iPhone 3G photo) to avoid the smooth, plastic AI look. Example: `old school VHS` ([@beechinour](https://x.com/beechinour/status/1991895004491780535) ⚑, 3,317 saves · 4,057 likes · 357,630 views)
- Do: Write 'slight grain and motion of a professional TV camera broadcast' (or 'heavy film grain' with handheld texture) directly in the video prompt. Example: `The video has the slight grain and motion of a professional TV camera broadcast.` ([@Sairah_0](https://x.com/Sairah_0/status/2054177744419000812), 4,000 saves · 4,610 likes · 759,068 views) Also: [@techhalla](https://x.com/techhalla/status/2083389002552664385)
- Do: Pair optics and directional light with named skin texture: 85mm, f/1.8, subtle film grain, soft diffuse directional light, gentle rim light, visible pores.
