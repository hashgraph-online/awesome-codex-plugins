# Archetypes that perform (2025–2026)

What spreads is not the prettiest character but the one with the biggest gap between what people expect and what they see, played completely straight. Numbers below were seen on X or in the cited press on 2026-10-27; money figures marked *claim* come from creators and were not verified. Many teardown accounts on X also sell courses, so treat their account numbers as second-hand.

## The rules behind every archetype

1. **Contrast, played straight.** "The bigger the gap between what people expect and what they see, the harder they share" ([@0xAI42exe](https://x.com/0xAI42exe/status/2115056920658846171)). No winking at the camera: "He doesn't explain the joke."
2. **One silhouette readable in one frame**, plus one signature colour or item locked with hex codes.
3. **A fixed format, only the situation changes.** Same character, same costume, new place or new problem every post.
4. **No words if possible**, so it travels across languages (babies, grannies with cats, creatures).
5. **Flaws for realism**: pores, asymmetry, phone framing. "Do not average the face with generic beauty features."
6. **Test 3–4 characters, 10 clips in 10–14 days, scale the one that spikes.**
7. **Label it as AI.** Instagram has demoted unlabelled AI-person accounts since 2026-08-31; the FTC treats an AI "customer" with an invented experience as a fake testimonial. An AI character or spokesperson is fine; a fake customer is not.

## 1. The odd, real-looking guy (the late-2026 meta)

A photoreal man with one signature outfit doing one repeated action everywhere. Jean Phil (blonde bob, curled moustache, brown houndstooth suit, shadowboxing around Paris) reached 244K Instagram followers from 12 posts; one clip had 35.9M views ([creatorflow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/jean-phil-ai-influencer/), [@simonecanciello](https://x.com/simonecanciello/status/2105674718346871149)). Copycats: Bill Smith (burgundy suit, platinum bangs, top clip 18.3M).

- **Why:** bizarre but believable, so the comments argue "is he real?"; motion copied from real viral clips, so he moves like a person.
- **Recipe:** sheet → motion transfer from your own or licensed footage (Kling motion control, Seedance "replace the person in @video1 with the character from @image1", MiniMax Recast) → same outfit, new location each post.
- **Seed:** "a 61-year-old Belgian man, extremely tall and lanky, thin ginger comb-over, huge tinted aviators, droopy moustache, mustard corduroy three-piece suit, white roller skates, completely deadpan."
- **Money:** CapCut paid creators to ride the trend; no verified brand deals yet. Natural fits: apparel, apps, energy drinks.

## 2. Extreme bodies played straight (very tall, very thin, very big, very strong)

Bony Tony, 6'7" and 154 lb in sandals among strongmen: top reel 4.6M. Brick, an 80s bodybuilder with a mullet and pink headband: 9.1M. Private Pickles, a 280-lb clown lifting cars: 14.6M ([@0xAI42exe](https://x.com/0xAI42exe/status/2114380781866860943)).

- **Why:** physical comedy needs no words; the gap is visible in one frame.
- **Craft:** numbers alone get under-rendered. Describe what the camera sees: "wide round belly that pushes the apron forward, thick neck, double chin" or "very long thin legs, arms hang to mid-thigh, small head for his height". Clothes that fit true to size; full-length framing.
- **Seed:** "Bruno, 48, a very large heavyweight florist of about 160 kg in a tiny pastel-pink apron that looks too small on him."
- **Money:** gym, supplement and apparel slots; accounts sell "partnerships" (*claim*).
- **Line:** the joke is the situation, never mockery of the body.

## 3. Very old characters (grannies, grandpas, monks)

Granny Spills, an AI grandmother in a pink suit: 1M Instagram followers in about a month (TIME, Nov 2025), 2.1M by July 2026, brand deals with Kurt Geiger and Papi Steak. A wordless cosy granny and cat in a storm: 16M views. A pink-fur-coat granny: 30M ([@growthnoir](https://x.com/growthnoir/status/2115060801941692809)).

- **Why:** age-versus-behaviour contrast (cash, swagger, strength); grandparent warmth and trust; buyers are women 38–65.
- **Craft:** exact age, deep wrinkles, age spots, visible veins on hands, slightly stooped posture; "no airbrushed skin".
- **Seed:** Nonna Rosa (see SKILL.md): "84-year-old Sicilian grandmother, gold front tooth, cat-eye glasses, curlers under a lace headscarf, leopard housecoat, red slippers, wooden spoon."
- **Money:** the strongest evidence of any archetype: brand deals, Amazon colouring books of the same granny, affiliate links. Undisclosed "granny recommends this supplement" pages break FTC rules; keep her a character, not a customer.

## 4. Beautiful but real (AI influencers, UGC actors)

An "AI cute girl" fashion-test reel: 4.5M views (*claim* of revenue). Raw-selfie realism prompt: 717K views, 7.3K bookmarks ([@jackcashout](https://x.com/jackcashout/status/2008868215334588808)). Photoreal AI UGC actors reach roughly parity with human UGC on hook rate and CTR at a fraction of the cost, and lose in trust-heavy verticals (skincare, supplements) ([Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/ai-ugc-vs-human-ugc-meta-ads-roas-2026.md)).

- **Why:** a reusable actor for try-ons, unboxings and reviews; parasocial attachment.
- **Counter-evidence:** a pet-costume brand's generic AI influencers got 212–785 views while its AI cat in a costume got 1.44M ([@Skylar_](https://x.com/Skylar_/status/2115101756765982741)). Generic beauty is the weakest archetype; specific beauty works.
- **Craft:** `varg-character-beautiful-woman`. Cast her like a model agency (striking features one by one), keep real skin, style her (makeup, outfit named exactly) and light it like a shoot.
- **Seed:** "strikingly beautiful 26-year-old woman with model looks: high cheekbones, full lips, almond-shaped green eyes, sharp jawline, long dark glossy hair in soft waves, sun-kissed olive skin with visible pores; fitted black satin slip dress, golden hour, 85mm."

## 5. Fitness creators and athletes

"This fitness coach doesn't exist" (post claims 8M views): grey tee, dark joggers, **lime wristbands** as the locked signature ([@ArchiveExplorer](https://x.com/ArchiveExplorer/status/2104244128238141554)). Leo Carter, a skinny gym kid, every reel above 892K.

- **Why:** a natural home for activewear, supplements and apps; transformation arcs.
- **Craft:** catalog phrasing so filters pass (see [moderation.md](moderation.md)): adult age as a number, garment names, an activity, "activewear brand catalog photo", public gym.
- **Seed:** Mara: "31-year-old CrossFit coach, athletic muscular build, bleached buzz cut, scar through left eyebrow, high-support lime-green sports bra, high-waisted black 7/8 leggings, lifting belt, chalk on her hands."

## 6. Babies and toddlers (cartoon or 3D, never sexualised)

Mello, one baby in one living room: 44M top reel. One AI baby account: 66.5M across 9 reels. A talking-baby podcast ad reported 5.41% outbound CTR (*claim* with dashboard).

- **Why:** zero words, every country; role flip (the baby is the boss).
- **Craft:** keep babies stylised (3D family-film look), clearly fictional, in comedy roles. Never in testimonials; kids'-content rules apply.

## 7. Absurd hybrids (the Italian brainrot format)

Tralalero Tralala (a shark in sneakers), Ballerina Cappuccina (55M+ TikTok views in H1 2025). *Steal a Brainrot* on Roblox hit 25.4M concurrent players. Ownership of the named characters is in court ([NPR, 2026-08-19](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/19/nx-s1-5867638/artificial-intelligence-brainrot-memes-spyder-tung-tung-sahur)).

- **Why:** maximum pattern interrupt; collectible lore; a fake-Italian name and a chant.
- **Use the format, not their characters.** Make your own hybrid: an object or food + an animal + one human accessory.
- **Seed:** Baguettosaurus: "a small T-rex whose whole body is a crusty golden baguette, flour-dusted scoring marks, googly eyes, red-and-white striped scarf, black beret, tiny white sneakers; stylised 3D CGI feature-film look."
- **Money:** weakest per view (Fruit Love Island hit 3M followers in 9 days and quit citing no revenue). Works for brand mascots and toys.

## 8. Fruit and object people in a drama

Fruit Love Island: 300M views and 3M followers in 9 days ([@tibo_maker](https://x.com/tibo_maker/status/2036390103921180815)). Reality-TV melodrama (betrayal, cliffhangers) made absurd by the cast.

- **Craft:** one sheet per cast member, one set, episodic hooks.
- **Risk:** mass reporting took 12 of 22 episodes down. Brands now commission their own micro-drama casts.

## 9. Animals in human roles (cat dramas, monkey sitcoms)

FUNTASTIC (orange kitten and dad): about 600K subscribers, peak month $9,000 (NPR). "Chubby" the fat orange cat: one video 136M views in 4 days. Kapwing's AI-slop report: 278 all-AI channels with 63B views.

- **Why:** human problems on an animal give comedy plus empathy; one consistent animal actor.
- **Craft:** a sheet for the animal with a human wardrobe item (a cap, an apron) as the signature.
- **Risk:** YouTube's "inauthentic content" rule removed AI channels with 35M subscribers in Jan 2026: originality and a recurring story matter.

## 10. Creatures and cryptids doing mundane things

Bigfoot vlogs (676K likes in a month), Big Yowie (35M views from 20 posts at $50–150 per short). A desert oracle versus a sand leviathan (GPT Image 2 → Seedance 2.0) as a craft showcase.

- **Why:** myth plus mundane influencer behaviour (selfie stick, complaining).
- **Craft:** a creature sheet with one human accessory; vlog grammar (handheld, talking to the lens).
- **Risk:** the Stormtrooper version is Lucasfilm IP. Use original creatures.

## 11. Mascots and brand characters

Coca-Cola's 2024 AI holiday ad scored the maximum 5.9 stars on System1. Kalshi's absurd-cast Veo 3 ad cost under $2K and got 3M+ views. Lu do Magalu: 7M+ Instagram followers, about 74 sponsored posts.

- **Why:** ownable, non-human, no uncanny valley and no "AI person" label problem; carries brand memory.
- **Craft:** expression sheet first; hex-locked colours; one prop sheet for the product.
- **Seed:** Señor Pincho: "a grumpy saguaro cactus mail carrier, bushy white spine moustache, tiny blue postal cap, leather mailbag, one pink flower; stylised 3D family-film look."

## 12. Style transforms and self-as-toy

The Ghibli wave added 1M ChatGPT users in an hour; the Nano Banana figurine brought Gemini about 13M first-time users in 4 days. Action-figure and caricature prompts reach millions of views.

- **Why:** your own face, or your character, in a loved style or as merch.
- **Conversion goes to the AI app, not the brand**, and named styles are IP risks in ads. For brands: put **your** character in a described look ([styles.md](styles.md)), or make merch-style shots of your own mascot (Nonna Rosa as a boxed vinyl figure).

## Formats to run with a finished character

- Same character, new location every post (Jean Phil).
- Same set, new situation (Mello the baby).
- One impossible feat per post (Sierra the stunt girl: 213M views on one clip, openly labelled AI).
- A cosy wordless routine (granny and cat).
- Micro-drama episodes (fruit, cats).
- Try-on, unboxing and product-slot scenes for affiliate.
