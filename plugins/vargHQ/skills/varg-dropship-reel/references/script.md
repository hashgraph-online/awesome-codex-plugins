# Script: the 55-65 second dropshipping guide reel

Breakdown of four reels from @tradelle, a dropshipping product-research tool (Sept-Oct 2026). Each runs 56-64 s at 1080×1920 and keeps the same skeleton and almost word-for-word the same voiceover. Only the product, the prices and the hook change. That is the point of the format: one template, a new product every day.

Variant seen in one reel: "First, make a Shopify account for a dollar. Then head over to <tool> and import the product to your Shopify store in one click. Now design a clean-looking product page, generate the description with <AI>, add complementary products to increase profits, offer free shipping, configure your payment processor and launch the store… You promote the product organically on social media, having zero in marketing expenses. This is called automated dropshipping."

Pace: about 2.6 words per second, no pauses longer than 0.5 s, one flat confident male voice (ElevenLabs-style narrator). No music under the voice, or a very quiet bed.

## Template

Fill the brackets. Keep it at 140-160 words for 55-60 s.

```
[HOOK LINE, 15-25 words: what the product is and the one thing it does, said like a user.]
They can be purchased for [$COST] in China and can be sold in a Shopify store for [$PRICE].
Even after shipping, the profit is decent and the demand for it is exceptionally high.
Here is how to profit from it without even touching the product.
First, go to [RESEARCH TOOL], pick the product and generate a free Shopify store in minutes.
Offer free shipping, configure your payment processor and launch the store.
Then go to [AI AD TOOL, e.g. varg dot A I] and paste your store link there. It will generate viral AI videos for the product.
Start posting them on [PLATFORM] with your store link in bio,
and when a customer purchases the product in your store, [SUPPLIER] ships the products out automatically,
and you pay for it after the customer has already paid you.
Comment "[KEYWORD]" and I will send you a free step-by-step guide on how it's done.
```

Write "varg.ai" as "varg dot A I" so the voice says it right; local Whisper then writes "varg .ai" and `captions.mjs` glues it back into one beat. Write numbers the way the voice should say them ("$4.47" reads as "four forty-seven" in ElevenLabs v4; check one take). Pick prices the viewer can verify on screen: the same figures must be visible on the screenshots in beat 2.

## Who uses this format, and how to adapt it

- **Affiliates of a tool** (the original): every reel sells the research tool and the AI ad tool; the "free guide" in the DM carries affiliate links. Swap in the tools you are an affiliate for.
- **A store owner** selling one product: drop beats 3-4, keep hook → price on your store page → "link in bio" CTA.
- **A course or community**: keep everything, the guide is the lead magnet.

## Claims to avoid

Price math is fine ("can be purchased for $3.49 and sold for $19.99"). Don't promise income ("make $10k a month"), don't show fake order dashboards as your own results, and say it's an ad if you're paid or an affiliate (#ad, or the platform's paid-partnership label). The FTC and the platforms treat earnings claims in "make money" content as ads that need proof.
