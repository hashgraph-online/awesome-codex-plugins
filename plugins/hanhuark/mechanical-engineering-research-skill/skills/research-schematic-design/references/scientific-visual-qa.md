# Scientific Schematic QA

Inspect the delivered figure at its final manuscript width or presentation size. Check the actual rendered output, not only the source file.

## Scientific Meaning

- Every object, boundary, arrow, color, and annotation has a defined meaning.
- Flow, heat, signal, and causal arrows point in the correct direction and do not use the same style without a legend.
- Labels, symbols, units, acronyms, dimensions, and operating conditions exactly match the manuscript or source data.
- Measured, simulated, proposed, conceptual, and illustrative elements are distinguishable.
- The figure does not imply numerical scale, causal proof, validation, or physical detail that the evidence does not support.

## Visual Construction

- The primary message is visible first; secondary details do not compete with it.
- Text is readable at final size and does not overlap arrows, objects, or other labels.
- Line weights, arrows, fonts, and colors are consistent; grayscale and color-vision use remain interpretable.
- Vector elements remain editable, and all technical text is editable rather than rasterized into an image.
- The composition has no accidental tangencies, clipped elements, unused legends, or decorative objects without a scientific role.

## Delivery

Verify the editable source opens, the exported PDF/PNG matches it visually, and the caption explains the visual's scope and evidence status. Report any unverified geometry, ambiguous label, or conceptual element that still needs author confirmation.
