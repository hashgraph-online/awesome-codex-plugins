# Single-Tweet Checklist

Run every single-tweet draft through this before showing the approval card.

## Fit

- [ ] Under 280 chars on a standard account. Count emoji as 2 chars each.
- [ ] If over 280 and the user is not on X Premium: tighten, or escalate to a
      thread. Never ship a truncated thought.
- [ ] First line stops the scroll on its own (no reliance on a second line).

## Voice scrub

- [ ] At most one em dash (`—`) in the tweet, and none if it reads fine
      without it. Replace the excess with a comma, colon or `..`, never a
      period. No en dashes (`–`) between clauses, no double dashes (`--`).
- [ ] No cluster of 2026 AI vocab (3+ markers in one tweet = rewrite the
      tweet; one is fine): significant, crucial, notably, comprehensive,
      insights, robust, leverage, foster, landscape, nuanced, streamline,
      elevate, empower, fundamentally, essentially, ultimately; plus "-ing"
      clause openers and "the X-tion of" nominalisations.
- [ ] No reveal bridge ("The result?", "Here's what", "Stop X, start Y") and
      no "It's not X, it's Y".
- [ ] No sincerity announcement ("let me be honest", "not gonna lie",
      "unpopular opinion:" on a popular take) and no hedge the author did not
      write.
- [ ] No staccato stack ("Short. Punchy. Done.", "No X. No Y. Just Z.") and
      no one-word line for drama. Do not chop a sentence to add punch; uniform
      rhythm is fine in a single tweet.
- [ ] At most one rule-of-three triplet, with concrete items.
- [ ] Names capitalized (Stripe, Claude, Vercel). Lowercase sentence starts are
      fine if the voice calls for it.

## Substance

- [ ] One idea only. Two ideas means two tweets.
- [ ] At least one odd-precision number with a named referent, or a named
      entity, where the claim allows it. A bare number does not count.
- [ ] A clear primary goal: replies, reposts, likes, or bookmarks. Not all four.

## Format

- [ ] 0 or 1 hashtag, at the end.
- [ ] 0 or 1 emoji, and only if it earns its place. None on a serious/contrarian take.
- [ ] No external link in the tweet body (offer to drop it in a reply).
- [ ] Line breaks used as beats, not decoration.

## Close

- [ ] Ends on a landing or a specific question, never "what do you think?".

## Char-count quick reference

| Account | Per-tweet limit | Emoji cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 280 | 2 chars each |
| X Premium | 25,000 | 2 chars each |

Publora reserves ~8 chars per tweet for the `(X/N)` marker when it auto-splits a
long draft into a thread.
