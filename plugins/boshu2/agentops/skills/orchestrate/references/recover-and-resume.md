# Recover and resume

Conditional detail for [Orchestrate](../SKILL.md): open it when a worker died,
when new evidence disproves a running assignment's premise, before removing a
finished worker's workspace, branch or stash, or when a caller-selected factory
runs the work.

## Recover a dead worker

Confirm its process stopped, not only its pane. Capture its state and preserve
its partial work recoverably. Move its tracker assignment to the replacement
rather than filing a duplicate, and start the fresh worker from that branch as
exact starting content while dependents hold. Replacement workers, retries, new
subjects and compaction never reset the caller's bounds.

## Recover the actual work

Read acceptance, examples, scope, settled choices and open questions from their
owner and the existing native handoff, without repeating settled interviews or
requiring the full transcript. A missing or contradictory pointer needs source
investigation, not a guess. When maintained context could change the next
action, use [Memory find/recall](../../memory/references/recall.md).

## Reconcile feedback

Preserve successful and failed evidence in the existing native task or handoff,
and update affected unfinished work's native dependencies or handoff within
authority. Stop or explicitly re-scope an affected active assignment before it
continues on a disproven premise; the revision takes effect only on observable
acknowledgment or stopped runtime state. Unaffected work continues unchanged;
reconciling unchanged facts creates no new artifact or dispatch.

[Plan](../../plan/SKILL.md) owns consequential uncertainty and refining the
next slice. New evidence may change an approach within the accepted outcome; a
different promised outcome or authority choice returns to the caller. Do not
reopen accepted work to fit a revised story.

## Prove integration before removal

Before a caller-authorized removal of a finished worker's workspace, branch or
stash, compare its content with the integration target as it stands now, by
patch or file diff, not by merged status or by matching the commit that was
merged: squash and rebase hide ancestry. Preserve uncommitted, unique or
unintegrated content in a recoverable form, such as an archive ref or bundle,
first. Report what was kept, what was removed and the evidence for each.

## A caller-selected factory

A factory keeps its coordinator in control: hand it source intent through its
supported door, never run its sessions by hand or mirror its work in a second
tracker, and judge the returned content independently. Its completion neither
authorizes delivery nor establishes acceptance.
