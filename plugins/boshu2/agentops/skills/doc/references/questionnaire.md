# Questionnaire for another person

Use this when facts or decisions the caller needs are held only by another
person, such as a database owner, an operator or a domain expert. The result is
one document that person can fill in alone or walk through in a meeting. The
caller sends it; [Doc](../SKILL.md) contacts no one. A choice that
[Interview](../../interview/SKILL.md) keeps Open because only another person
can answer it comes here.

## What to get from the caller

Take who receives it, what that person knows that the caller does not, and
which decisions the answers must settle from the request; ask only for the ones
it leaves open. Take everything else from the request and the repository.

## Write it

- Open with the purpose, the decision waiting on the answers, sender and
  recipient, how the answers will be used and, when the caller gives them, the
  deadline and expected effort. Add one paragraph of context written for
  someone outside the work.
- Order questions by consequence: the answer that would change the most comes
  first. Group them under short themes when there are many.
- Each question asks one thing and leaves room for the reply. Say why it is
  asked only where it could be misread.
- Invite partial answers and "I don't know", marked as uncertain rather than
  skipped. End by asking what the questionnaire missed.

## Check it before returning

Every decision the caller named has at least one question that settles it.
Share only context the caller may disclose to that recipient: leave out
secrets, unrelated personal data and internal labels the recipient cannot
place. Return the document in the response, or write it where the caller names
and return that path.
